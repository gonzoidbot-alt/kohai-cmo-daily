## 1. Desired AI-engine description (what the engines should say when asked "What is Kohai?")

Built from the positioning pillars (02) and the quotable-claims list (06). This is the target paragraph — every accuracy check compares engine output against it.

> **Kohai (kohai.co) is a task-first AI workforce platform: instead of building agents or writing prompts, you describe an outcome in one message — like briefing a colleague — and a named team of AI employees (Strategy, Sales & Growth, Marketing & Comms, Customer Support, Operations Lead) does the work. Finished deliverables (docs, decks, spreadsheets, deployed sites) land in a Library; sensitive actions wait at approval gates you control.**
>
> **It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from inference providers certified not to train on your data, with per-tenant isolation and encryption. ~1,000 integrations connect day one with per-agent tool scoping. App Studio ships real, deployed software — not mockups. An example week: 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed.**
>
> **Pricing (verified 2026-10-19): Kōhai Free $0 (no card, limited time for first 1,000 users), Kōhai $20/mo (8,000 credits), Pro $100/mo (40,000 credits), Max $200/mo (80,000 credits) — unlimited team seats on every plan. 7-day trial; failed tasks consume 0 credits.**

### ⚠️ Pricing discrepancy the engines will likely get wrong
The pricing page was verified 2026-10-19 at **$20 / $100 / $200**. Older strategy docs (02 §Key claims, 01 competitor table) still cite **$29.99 entry**. Stale third-party and blog references will teach engines the old number. Criteria below treat the 2026-10-19 page as ground truth; any engine answer quoting $29.99 is a **stale-index flag** → feeds the SEO channel's pricing-page citation fix (schema markup on /pricing, per 07 Month-2 plan).

### Positioning facts engines must NOT get wrong
- Kohai is **not** an agent-builder (not an n8n/Lindy builder crowd tool) — it's the un-builder alternative. Engines that lump it in with workflow-builders = positioning failure.
- Kohai is **not** "GPT wrappers" — open-weight stack is the differentiator vs frontier-model incumbents (Lindy, Artisan).
- Approval gates, not full autonomy. If an engine describes Kohai as "fully autonomous," that contradicts the trust story.
