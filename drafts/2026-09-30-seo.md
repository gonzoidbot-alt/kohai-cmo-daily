## KEYWORD GAP — one concept, three vocabularies: "teammate" on-site, "colleague" in strategy, "employee" in the money phrases

This is new today and it sits under both fixes above. The site, the strategy docs, and the demand curve each use a different word for the same product:

- **The site says "teammates":** home 27×, /vs/lindy 11×, pricing 6×; blog post is literally titled "What is an AI teammate?" — but the sitemap slug for that same post is `what-is-an-ai-employee`.
- **The docs say "colleagues"**: 02/03/04/06 standardize on "AI colleagues, not agents" as the category phrase Kohai is supposed to own — it appears 3× on the homepage, mostly in meta.
- **Searchers say "AI employees"**: every money phrase in 04-content-strategy uses it — "AI employees for small business", "hire AI employees". Live homepage occurrences of "AI employee(s)": **0**. Of "workforce": **0**. Of "hire": **0**.

Nobody searching "AI employees for small business" — or an AI engine deciding how to describe Kohai — gets a consistent signal. The relevance for the highest-volume phrase family is split three ways and each variant under-serves it.

**Concretely:** pick **"AI colleagues" as the brand phrase** (docs are right — it's the differentiator) but treat **"AI employees" as the ranking vocabulary**: it belongs in the homepage title (Fix 2), the H1 or first paragraph, and one dedicated money page. Cheapest first move that serves both: write the `/ai-colleagues` explainer (still 404) with question-H2s — "What is an AI colleague?", "AI colleagues vs AI agents", "AI employees for small business: what to look for" — so one page carries both vocabularies and links them.

**Also logged:** `/blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business` targets the money phrase with the word "agents" in the URL — a direct glossary violation (03: never "AI agents" for Kohai's category) on the exact page meant to win "AI employees for small business". Retitle/re-slug when the next blog slot opens; don't break the URL casually.
