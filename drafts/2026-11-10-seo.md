## Fix 1 — [HIGH] Homepage `<title>` and `<meta name="description">` target zero keyword share

**Severity:** High — the `<title>` is the first thing Google reads and the primary ranking signal for brand-adjacent queries. The current title is all brand, no keyword.

**Current homepage `<title>`:**
```
Kōhai | Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.
```

**Problem:** Every competitor in the set (Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism) leads with a category + differentiator in their title (e.g., "AI Employees" / "AI BDR"). Kohai's title buries the category signal — searchers for "AI employees," "AI workforce platform," or "AI teammates for small business" see zero keyword match in the title tag. Per the strategy doc's money-page targets (04-content-strategy.md: "AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees"), this is a 1-line fix that costs nothing.

**Recommended fix — option A (category-first):**
```html
<title>AI Teammates &amp; Workforce Platform for Small Business | Kōhai</title>
<meta name="description" content="Onboard AI teammates that finish work across sales, marketing, ops, and support. Open-weight, private by default. Start on Kōhai Free — $0, no card." />
```
Hits: "AI teammates", "AI workforce platform", "small business", "open-weight", "private", plus the free-tier CTA in the description.

**Recommended fix — option B (comparison-query capture):**
```html
<title>Kōhai — The AI Workforce Platform That Doesn&#39;t Make You Build Agents</title>
<meta name="description" content="Kōhai is the Lindy alternative that gives you AI teammates, not agent builders. Describe the outcome. Finished work lands in your Library. Open-weight &amp; private." />
```
Hits: "AI workforce platform", "Lindy alternative", "AI teammates", plus the anti-builder differentiator that the strategy doc identifies as Kohai's core win.

**Installation:** Edit `index.html` (or the `Helmet`/`Head` component if using Next.js/Remix) — one-line change. The `meta description` goes in the same component just below the title.

**SEO value calculation:** According to the GTM playbook (06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md § Stage 2), "comparison or buyer guide per week" plus "structure pages for lifting: question as H2, answer in first 2 sentences" are the priority. At minimum, the homepage title must carry category keywords — currently it carries zero.

## Fix 2 — [MEDIUM] No "Alternatives / vs" pages exist, handing Lindy the entire comparison-query traffic

**Severity:** Medium (build effort) — but Lindy is already running competitor pricing takedowns (lindy.ai/blog/sintra-ai-pricing) that rank for "Sintra pricing" and, by extension, "Lindy alternative" and "AI employees pricing." Kohai's strategy doc lists "Lindy alternative" and "Artisan alternative" as priority money pages (04-content-strategy.md). These pages do not exist on kohai.co today.

**Problem:** Lindy's aggressive SEO play — publishing head-to-head pricing comparisons — means anyone searching "best AI employees for small business" or "Lindy vs X alternatives" finds Lindy's own content first. Kohai has no counter-page. The strategy doc says these should exist ("money pages"). This is a structural gap, not a tag tweak.

## Keyword-Gap Observation

**Gap:** **"AI Chief of Staff"** and **"AI Strategy Lead"** are zero-competition, high-relevance long-tail queries that no competitor owns and Kohai's product literally delivers.

**Data:** A search for "AI Chief of Staff" returns generic career-advice content and task-management tools — none of the named competitors (Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism, Relevance AI) rank for this term or have a dedicated page targeting it. Meanwhile, Kohai's product has a named "Chief of Staff" role template that is the *first teammate listed* on the homepage and the core orchestrator of the 3-step workflow. The term appears nowhere in Kohai's page title, meta description, or H1s.

**What to do:** Create a dedicated landing page at `/ai-chief-of-staff` (or as an anchor section on the homepage with its own URL slug) that:
- H1: "AI Chief of Staff — Your Strategy, Orchestrated"
- First 2 sentences: "Kōhai's first teammate is your Chief of Staff. It listens to your brief, assigns the right leads, and tracks every deliverable — so you never manage the work, just the outcome."
- Lists what the Chief of Staff does: meeting prep, note-taking, follow-ups, team assignment, status tracking
- Links to the pricing page with a "Try for free" CTA

Same logic applies to **"AI Sales Lead"** and **"AI Marketing Lead"** — these are named roles in the product that no one else is SEO-targeting, and they're the kind of phrase a solo operator types when they're looking for help but don't know the category name yet.

**Supporting evidence:** The strategy doc (04-content-strategy.md) lists "AI chief of staff" as a long-tail target. The product has it. The competitors don't. Every day without a page for this term is a day the zero-competition query sits unserved.
