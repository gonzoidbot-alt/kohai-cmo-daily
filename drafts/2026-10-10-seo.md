## Fix 2 — P1: /pricing FAQ answers contradict the live plan cards — reconcile the copy BEFORE any FAQPage schema ships

**Severity: high, and newly urgent.** The 10-04/10-06 drafts carried a copy-paste FAQPage JSON-LD for /pricing built from the live FAQ. Today's crawl shows the FAQ itself is now stale: the page sells a 4-tier unlimited-seats model while three of its answers still describe the old per-seat/Team-plan model:

- "All prices in USD, **per seat**." (intro line of the FAQ block) — contradicts "unlimited seats on every plan" in the cards and intro.
- "**What does the Team plan add over Pro?** A shared credit pool across your organization, plus the admin controls to manage it…" — **no Team plan exists on the page.**
- Trial answer: "Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial" vs. the cards' Free tier ("Start without a card") and the FAQ's own other answer ("Your trial runs its full seven days, then your workspace moves to Kōhai Free") — the trial story changed shape and the two answers disagree.

Why this is the priority over shipping more schema: answer engines quote FAQ text nearly verbatim. Right now an AI engine lifting the /pricing FAQ would tell buyers Kohai has per-seat pricing and a Team plan — both false on the live page. Shipping the FAQPage JSON-LD that previous drafts prepared would **freeze the wrong copy into structured data** and make the wrong story more authoritative, not less.

**Copy-paste (replacements, match to live cards):**

```text
1. "All prices in USD, per seat."
→ "All prices in USD, per workspace. Every plan includes the full team and unlimited seats — only the monthly credits change between plans."

2. "What does the Team plan add over Pro? A shared credit pool across your organization, plus the admin controls to manage it: set per-member credit limits so one teammate can't drain the pool, top up with add-on credits when you need more capacity, and keep billing centralized under the org owner."
→ DELETE this question entirely (no Team plan exists), or replace with a question that matches the live model, e.g.:
"What do unlimited seats mean? Every plan — including Kōhai Free — includes the whole AI team and unlimited team seats in one shared workspace. Plans differ only in monthly credits (8,000 / 40,000 / 80,000), so you pick capacity, not seats."

3. Trial answers — pick one story and use it in both places:
→ "Every plan starts with a 7-day free trial on the Pro tier. When the trial ends, your workspace moves to Kōhai Free — credits refresh monthly, no card needed — and you can upgrade any time."
(Adjust to whatever the product actually does; the current page states two different trial outcomes.)
```

**Then** ship the FAQPage JSON-LD from the 10-04 draft, regenerated from the corrected answers (the old snippet's "Is there an annual plan?" answer — $220/$1,050/$2,100 — was re-verified live today and remains correct). This ordering is the difference between schema that helps and schema that launders a contradiction into every answer engine.
