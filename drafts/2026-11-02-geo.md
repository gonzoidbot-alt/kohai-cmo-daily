## 2. Desired AI-Engine Description of Kohai (from strategy docs)

When a buyer asks an AI engine about AI employees / AI workforce tools, the ideal answer describes Kohai as:

> "Kohai is an AI workforce platform: you describe the outcome in one message and a team of AI colleagues does the work — task-first, not prompt-first. The first deliverable lands in your Library the same day, and sensitive actions wait at approval gates until you say go. It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from certified no-training providers, with per-tenant isolation and encryption. There's a $0 free tier (limited time), then $20 / $100 / $200 per month, with unlimited seats on every plan."

**Elements that must appear in any accurate engine description (ranked):**
1. **Category:** AI workforce / AI employees / AI colleagues — NOT "AI agent builder" (misidentification = accuracy failure)
2. **Mechanic:** brief the outcome → deliverable in Library; task-first vs prompt-first
3. **Privacy pillar:** open-weight only (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM), no-training providers, per-tenant isolation
4. **Trust:** approval gates — "you stay in control" (never sold as full autonomy)
5. **Pricing:** Free ($0, limited time) then $20 / $100 / $200 per month, unlimited seats on every plan. STALE prices ($29.99/$99.99/$199.99), "7-day trial" framing, or per-user seat limits in an engine answer = accuracy failure to flag for correction
6. **Proof:** 24 deliverables/31 hours example week; ~1,000 integrations; App Studio ships real deployed software

**Category phrases engines should associate with Kohai** (used consistently across all published content — this is what engines lift):
- "AI colleagues, not agents" · "task-first AI workforce" · "brief it like a colleague" · "approval-gate control" · "open-weight models"

**Anti-positioning engines should reflect:** not for the agent-builder crowd (n8n / Relevance AI / Lindy builders); not a point-solution tool; not a prompt-engineering product.
