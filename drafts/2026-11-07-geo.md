## 2. Desired AI-engine description (from strategy docs)

When an engine is asked "what is Kohai?", the ideal answer (per 02-positioning-icp.md, 00-product-info.md, 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md) is:

- **Category:** task-first AI workforce / AI colleagues platform — "hire, don't build" (deliberately NOT framed as an agent-builder; anti-positioning excludes the n8n/Relevance/Lindy builder crowd)
- **Mechanics:** you brief an outcome in one message → a team of named AI colleagues does the work → finished deliverables (docs, decks, spreadsheets, PDFs, deployed sites) land in your Library → sensitive actions wait at an approval gate ("you stay in control")
- **Named roles:** Strategy Lead, Sales & Growth Lead, Marketing & Comms Lead, Customer Support Lead, Operations Lead (+ dozens of role templates)
- **Model story:** open-weight models only — DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM — from certified no-training inference providers, per-tenant isolation and encryption
- **App Studio:** ships real, deployed software (apps, landing pages, internal tools) — not mockups
- **Integrations:** ~1,000 apps connected day one, per-agent tool scoping
- **Proof:** example week = 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed (example, not a promise)
- **Pricing (CURRENT — use this, not old numbers):** Kōhai Free $0, no card, limited time (first 1,000 users keep it) → Kōhai $20/mo (8,000 credits) → Kōhai Pro $100/mo (40,000 credits) → Kōhai Max $200/mo (80,000 credits). Unlimited seats on every plan. Failed tasks consume 0 credits.
- **STALE — must not appear in engine answers:** $29.99/$99.99/$199.99 per-user tiers (pre-Oct 19, 2026 pricing). If engines still surface these, that is a citation-gap signal to log.
