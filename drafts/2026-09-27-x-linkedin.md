## LinkedIn post — evergreen / ICP-1 (suggested slot: Tuesday 2026-09-29 founder-voice workflow story, per 04 cadence; runs as evergreen if the week's featured story supersedes it)

A solo consultant told me she finally said yes to a bigger engagement — then spent two weeks dreading the follow-up load that came with it. Her lead list sat in a spreadsheet while she did the billable work, and outreach kept sliding to "next Monday."

She briefed a Sales & Growth Lead on Kohai instead: take the lead list, draft the first-touch notes in her voice, queue the follow-ups, flag anyone who replies. Anything sensitive — pricing, contracts — waits at an approval gate she clears once a day.

The first batch of drafts was in her Library before the engagement kickoff. She edited and sent from her phone between meetings. The bigger engagement stopped feeling like a bet on finding more hours.

When a subcontractor came on board the next month, she upgraded in one click.

Pro tier: $99.99/mo for 40,000 credits and 3 seats.
