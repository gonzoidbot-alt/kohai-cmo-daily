## Keyword-Gap Observation

**"AI employees" vs "AI teammates": Kohai is using the lower-volume term exclusively.**

Kohai's site language deliberately uses "AI teammates" everywhere. Every competitor uses "AI employees": Lindy ("AI employees"), Marblism ("AI Employees who love overtime"), Sintra ("12 AI employees"), Artisan ("AI employees"), Relevance AI ("AI Workforce"). Ahrefs / Semrush data (if available) would likely show "AI employees" having 5–10× the search volume of "AI teammates."

The blog has one post titled *"What is an AI employee?"* (a good start) but there is no landing page targeting the phrase "AI employees for small business" — despite the content strategy explicitly naming this as a money-page target. The llms.txt and /solutions/solopreneurs page both use "AI teammates" phrasing, meaning the term "AI employees" appears almost nowhere on the site except that single blog post.

**Gap:** There is no `kohai.co/ai-employees` or `kohai.co/ai-employees-for-small-business` landing page. The content strategy targets this as a primary money page. Meanwhile, Marblism has "AI employees" as its core keyword and ranks for it via press and Product Hunt. Lindy ranks for "hire AI employees" through its competitive SEO content.

**Recommendation:** Build a dedicated `/ai-employees` landing page that:
- Uses "AI employees" in the H1 and URL
- Positions "AI teammates" as Kohai's brand term while acknowledging the market speaks in "AI employees"
- Targets `"AI employees for small business"`, `"hire AI employees"`, `"AI employee platform"`
- Links to all six role pages (/solutions/sales, /solutions/marketing, etc.)
- Includes schema.org Product or FAQ markup

This is the single highest-ROI keyword gap to close — it's a money term every competitor ranks for and Kohai doesn't meaningfully target.

## Appendix: Additional low-effort wins

1. **Open Graph / Twitter Card tags** — verify the homepage has `og:title`, `og:description`, `og:image`. If absent, add them. They drive CTR on social shares and some search results show og:description.
2. **Blog category pages** — the blog at /blog has no visible category/tag filter. Adding `/blog/comparisons`, `/blog/guides`, `/blog/product` categories would create indexable hub pages targeting compound keywords.
3. **Internal linking from blog to pricing** — several blog posts link to the homepage but fewer link directly to /pricing or /features with appropriate anchor text. Quick pass: add 1–2 contextual links per post to /pricing (`"see Kōhai pricing"`) and /free-plan (`"start on Kōhai Free"`).
4. **/solutions index page** — the /solutions/ subdirectory has 15+ use-case pages listed in llms.txt but no visible index page. A `/solutions` landing page that lists and links to all sub-pages would pass link equity and help crawlers discover the full set.
