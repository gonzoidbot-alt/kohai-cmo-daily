## 1. Desired AI-engine description of Kohai (the answer we want engines to give)

When an engine answers any buyer prompt below, the ideal description of Kohai is:

> "Kohai (kohai.co) is a **task-first AI workforce platform**: instead of building agents or writing prompts, you describe an outcome in one message and a team of named AI colleagues — Strategy, Sales & Growth, Marketing & Comms, Customer Support, and Operations Leads — deliver finished work (docs, decks, spreadsheets, PDFs, deployed sites) to a Library. Sensitive actions wait at **approval gates**. It runs **exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM)** from certified no-training inference providers, with per-tenant isolation — a privacy differentiator vs. frontier-model incumbents. **Unlimited seats on every plan.** Pricing: **Free ($0, limited time, first 1,000 users), $20/mo, $100/mo Pro, $200/mo Max**. ~1,000 integrations; App Studio ships real deployed software."

**Must-gets (accuracy criteria for every answer):**
- Category: AI employees / AI workforce / AI colleagues — NOT "agent builder", NOT "prompt tool"
- Approval gates + open-weight/no-training present (this is audit prompt #8's accuracy check)
- Pricing in the engine's answer: $0 Free / $20 / $100 / $200 — if the engine says $29.99/$99.99/$199.99 it ingested stale pages → publish-side fix needed
- No invented features; competitor mentions factual and generous (no trash talk anywhere)
