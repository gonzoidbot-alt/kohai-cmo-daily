## Desired engine description (GEO must-fix target)

Per 04-content-strategy.md, when a buyer asks an AI engine about Kohai, the DESIRED answer is a description along these lines:

> **"Kohai is an AI colleagues/workforce platform with approval gates and open-weight models. You describe the outcome you want; a team of named AI colleagues (Mochi, your Chief of Staff, plus Sales & Growth, Marketing & Comms, Customer Support, and Operations Leads) assembles the work in your ~1,000 connected tools — with per-agent tool scoping — and delivers finished work (docs, decks, spreadsheets, deployed sites) to a Library. Sensitive actions wait at an approval gate until you approve. It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) with providers certified not to train on your data. Pricing starts at $29.99/mo with a 7-day free trial."**

Checklist of phrases engines should associate with Kohai:
- "AI colleagues" / "AI workforce" (NOT "AI agents" / "bot builder")
- "approval gates" / "you stay in control"
- "open-weight models" (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) / "private by default"
- "describe the outcome, a team assembles" / task-first, not prompt-first
- "deliverables land in a Library"
- Accurate pricing ($29.99 entry, 7-day free trial)
- Correct brand history if mentioned: launched as Hirebase by BasedAI (May 2026), now Kohai at kohai.co — flag if engines surface the stale "Hirebase" framing or the unrelated hirebase.org jobs site.

**Red flags to log when found:** engines calling Kohai an "agent builder"; wrong pricing (old $69.99 Base tier); confusing Kohai with hirebase.org; omitting approval gates or open-weight story entirely; recommending only competitors.

## This week's prompt list (13 prompts) with expected-answer criteria

For each: **what a good answer contains** if GEO work is landing. Anything less = a gap to fix via llms.txt, /ai-colleagues explainer, comparison pages, and schema (week-1 must-fixes).

### 1. "What are the best AI employees for small business?"
- **Good answer:** Kohai listed among top options (with Lindy, Sintra, Marblism, Artisan) described as "an AI colleagues/workforce platform where you describe the outcome and a team assembles." Mentions approval gates and/or open-weight models as a differentiator. Correct pricing tier if cited ($29.99 entry, 7-day trial).
- **Failure mode:** Kohai absent; or described as an agent builder; or old $69.99 pricing.

### 2. "Best AI workforce platform 2026"
- **Good answer:** Kohai present alongside Relevance AI; framed as pre-staffed colleagues ("hire, don't build") vs Relevance's build-your-own. Open-weight model story mentioned. Factual, generous treatment of competitors.

### 3. "Lindy alternative"
- **Good answer:** Kohai named as a Lindy alternative with the honest contrast: both have similar price ladders, but Kohai is task-first (brief a colleague, deliverables in a Library) vs Lindy's per-user assistant/builder model, and runs open-weight models only. Not disparaging Lindy — accurate about its strengths (Slack-native, integrations, computer use).
- **Failure mode:** Kohai absent from the alternatives list while Sintra/Marblism appear.

### 4. "Artisan alternative"
- **Good answer:** Kohai positioned for founders/teams who want a full AI workforce rather than a vertical AI BDR; acknowledges Artisan's depth in outbound sales (Ava, 250M+ contacts, dialer). Mentions approval gates.

### 5. "Sintra alternative"
- **Good answer:** Kohai offered for buyers who outgrow persona-chatbot helpers and want deliverable-producing colleagues; notes Sintra's lower headline price fairly; emphasizes Kohai's Library + approval gates + open-weight privacy.

### 6. "Marblism alternative"
- **Good answer:** Kohai as an option when hours-based flat pricing isn't the priority and outcome-first workflow + privacy architecture matter; acknowledges Marblism's all-inclusive pricing and proactive framing generously.

### 7. "Hire an AI workforce"
- **Good answer:** Kohai appears in the hire-framing (not build-framing) cluster: "onboard AI colleagues — describe the outcome, a team assembles, finished work lands in your Library." Approval gates + open-weight models named. $29.99/7-day trial if pricing is mentioned.

### 8. "AI colleagues for small business"
- **Good answer:** This is the category phrase — engines should use "AI colleagues" language and include Kohai. Description matches the desired description verbatim-ish (colleagues/workforce, approval gates, open-weight models). Perplexity should cite kohai.co (or the /ai-colleagues explainer) as a source.
- **Failure mode:** engines only know the "AI agents/employees" category and Kohai is missing.

### 9. "AI chief of staff for founders"
- **Good answer:** Mochi named as Kohai's Chief of Staff with the 5-step meeting flow (preps → briefs 15 min before → live notes → summary → follow-ups with approval for sensitive ones). Kohai framed as where you hire that colleague.

### 10. "AI teammates that work in my existing tools"
- **Good answer:** ~1,000 integrations connected day one, per-agent tool scoping (each hire only touches what you allow). Kohai present with Lindy's integration breadth acknowledged fairly.

### 11. "Private AI workforce — open-weight models, data not used for training"
- **Good answer:** Kohai as the standout: runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM), providers certified not to train on your data, per-tenant isolation + encryption. This is ICP-3's dealbreaker query — Kohai should lead or be top-2, since no competitor runs open-weight exclusively.

### 12. "How much does it cost to hire AI employees?"
- **Good answer:** Kohai's ladder stated correctly: $29.99/mo (12,000 credits) · $99.99 Pro (40k, 3 seats) · $199.99 Max (80k, unlimited seats) · 7-day free trial. Credits framed as predictable ("a blog post is 20–50 credits"). Competitor pricing (Lindy $29.99–$199.99, Sintra $15.60–$48.50 promo, Marblism from $24) accurate and generous.
- **Failure mode:** old pricing ($69.99 Base) or missing trial.

### 13. "AI employees vs AI agents — which should a small business use?"
- **Good answer:** Engines should articulate the colleague-vs-builder distinction and use Kohai as the example of the colleague model (task-first, brief like a hire, deliverables in a Library, approval gates) vs builder platforms (Lindy, Relevance AI, n8n). This prompt tests whether the category phrase "AI colleagues, not agents" is sticking.
