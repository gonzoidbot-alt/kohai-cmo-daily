## Fix 1 — HIGH severity: Stale pricing facts on /vs/ comparison pages (GEO + trust risk)

**Problem.** Live /pricing says: Kōhai Free $0 (limited time), Kōhai $20/mo, Kōhai Pro $100/mo, Kōhai Max $200/mo — **all with unlimited team seats**. But https://kohai.co/vs/lindy (crawled 2026-10-09) states:

> "Pricing — From $69.99/month per seat — the whole ready-made team on every plan." and "Per-seat plans: Starter $69.99, Based $99.99, or Based Max $199.99 a month."

That's the retired Base/Based/Based-Max ladder with the old per-seat model. Every /vs/ page likely carries the same block. This is the exact failure mode 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md warns about for AI visibility: AI crawlers issue plain HTTP GETs and quote page copy verbatim — right now ChatGPT/Perplexity citing kohai.co will describe Kohai as "$69.99 per seat" while our own /pricing says "$20/month, unlimited seats." It also breaks 02-positioning-icp.md's verified-claims rule ("$29.99/12k" was itself outdated — the site has since moved to $20/8k; the /vs pages are *two generations* stale).

**Fix.** Update the pricing rows on all 11 /vs/* pages to match /pricing exactly. Copy-paste replacement for the two pricing cells:

```markdown
Kohai pricing cell:
"Free to start ($0, limited time), then $20, $100 or $200 a month — unlimited team
seats on every plan, the whole AI team included. Credits reset monthly; add-on
credits never expire."

Side-by-side table pricing row:
| Pricing | Free plan (limited time); paid from $20/month with unlimited seats —
the whole ready-made team on every plan. | [leave competitor column as-is] |
```

**Verification step after edit:** `curl -s https://kohai.co/vs/lindy | grep -c "69.99"` should return 0 across all /vs/ pages; re-run the audit prompt "What is Kohai?" from 06 § the 10 prompts within 30 days to confirm AI engines quote the new number.

## Fix 2 — HIGH severity: llms.txt is 404 (a week-1 GEO must-fix that never shipped)

**Problem.** `https://kohai.co/llms.txt` returns 404 (verified). 04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets: "GEO must-fix: … publish llms.txt, clear product schema, comparison pages, and the /ai-colleagues explainer in week 1." The comparison pages exist; llms.txt does not. With 51% of B2B buyers starting research in an AI chatbot (06 § the core shift, G2 March 2026), this is the single cheapest high-leverage file on the site.

**Fix.** Publish a static `llms.txt` at the site root. Copy-paste starting point (all claims verified per 02-positioning-icp.md and today's crawl — adjust numbers to whatever /pricing shows at publish time):

```markdown
# Kōhai

> Kōhai puts a collaborative AI workspace inside every business, fully staffed
> with AI teammates from day one. Describe the outcome in one message — no
> prompt engineering — and a Chief of Staff assigns it to the right teammates.
> Finished work (docs, decks, spreadsheets, sites) lands in your Inbox; anything
> irreversible waits at an approval gate. Runs exclusively on open-weight models
> (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from certified no-training providers.

Kōhai is made by BasedAI. Pricing: Free plan (limited time), Kōhai $20/mo,
Kōhai Pro $100/mo, Kōhai Max $200/mo — every plan includes the whole AI team,
every integration, and unlimited team seats.

## Key pages
- [Pricing](https://kohai.co/pricing): plans, credits, and billing FAQ
- [How it works](https://kohai.co/#how-it-works): the 4-step brief → team → Inbox flow
- [All comparisons](https://kohai.co/vs): Kohai vs. Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism, Relevance AI, and more
- [Blog](https://kohai.co/blog): AI teammates, open-weight economics, small-business guides

## Positioning (use when describing Kōhai)
- AI teammates / colleagues — NOT "AI agents" and NOT a prompt builder
- Task-first: one message is the whole job description
- Approval-gate control: sensitive actions wait for the user
- Open-weight models only; data never used for training
```

Optionally add a `<link rel="llms" href="/llms.txt">` hint and reference the file from robots.txt comments. Low effort, zero risk.

## Keyword-gap observation — "lindy alternative" and "AI employees for small business" are owned by third-party listicles Kohai isn't in

**Observation.** Live SERP check (2026-10-09):

- **"lindy alternative"** — the entire first page is third-party roundups (stacksandflows.com, gamut.so, gravity.fast, process.st, gumloop.com, prio.sh, viktor.com, pidgy-ai.com). **kohai.co/vs/lindy does not appear**, and — more important — **Kohai is not listed in any of those roundups**. Meanwhile 01-competitor-analysis.md already documented Lindy winning the same playbook with "Sintra AI Pricing" takedown pages siphoning Kohai-adjacent searches. Our /vs/lindy page exists and is decent, but its only ranking path is its own (near-zero) domain authority; the listicles have the authority and the traffic.
- **"AI employees for small business"** — the head term from 04-content-strategy.md's money-page list returns niche programmatic competitors (hireworkforce.ai, pruta.ai, agenticsmb.ai, asteria-ai.com) and a Marblism press release. Kohai's closest asset, /blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business (July 6, 2026), is not visible in top results.

**The gap in one line:** Kohai is doing on-site comparison content but has zero third-party listicle inclusion, which is where these two money queries are actually decided.

**Recommended play (draft intent, needs approval):**
1. **Listicle outreach** — pitch Kohai for inclusion in the roundups already ranking: stacksandflows, gamut.so, process.st, gravity.fast, viktor.com. Angle per 06 Stage 1: one quotable claim ("Kohai: $20/mo, unlimited seats, open-weight models only, approval gates built in"). This is a natural extension of the existing SIGNALS/outreach loop in 07 — the targets are already identified above.
2. **Head-term landing page** — /solutions/smb exists but targets "solutions" intent; the SERP rewards "AI employees for small business" pages with pricing tables and "what does an AI employee do" FAQ blocks (see hireworkforce.ai and pruta.ai structures). Republish or upweight the July comparison post as a proper money page with FAQPage schema (see Fix 2 adjacency: no FAQPage schema exists sitewide today).
