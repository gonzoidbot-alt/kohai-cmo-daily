## Fix 2 (Severity: HIGH — new finding, deeper than the prior schema flags) — every blog post ships with zero Article schema and no publish dates; pricing FAQs remain invisible as structured data

**Evidence (crawled 2026-09-19):**
- Sampled posts (`/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee`, `/blog/what-is-an-open-weight-model`, `/blog/never-download-the-model`, `/blog/predictable-ai-bill`, `/blog/what-an-ai-job-actually-costs`): each returns **exactly two JSON-LD blocks — Organization + WebSite, identical to the homepage.** No `Article`/`BlogPosting`, no `datePublished`/`dateModified`, no `author`. This holds across all 17 blog URLs in sitemap-0.xml (same template).
- sitemap-0.xml contains **zero `<lastmod>` entries** for any of its 52 URLs — AI engines and Google have no freshness signal anywhere on the site.
- Why this matters for this specific competitor set: Lindy's SEO engine is its blog (01-competitor-analysis.md: aggressive head-to-head pricing content), and per 06, AI engines quote structured, dated, attributable content at higher rates. Kohai's blog is the right strategic asset ("Predictable AI Bill", "What an AI Job Actually Costs", "What Is an Open-Weight Model?" are exactly the pillar-3 economics content the HN/Reddit crowd cites) — but to a citation engine every post is an undated, authorless page indistinguishable from marketing copy. The content exists; the machine-readable wrapper doesn't.
- Compounding, unchanged from prior flags: /pricing has 8 visible FAQ accordion items (What's a credit / difference between plans / run out of credits / switch mid-cycle / free trial / …) and no FAQPage schema; the plan cards have no Product/Offer schema. Sitewide JSON-LD count: 2.
