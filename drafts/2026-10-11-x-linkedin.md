## LinkedIn post — Monday pillar slot (the security-review surface-area story; ICP-2 primary, ICP-3 readable; claim #3; 120–180 words)

When we evaluated platforms for client work, the objection wasn't capability. It was surface area — every new tool is another system in the security review.

That instinct is right. A new login, a new permissions page, another vendor questionnaire. Most AI tools add one.

Kohai works the other direction. The colleagues work in the tools you already run. ~1,000 integrations connected day one, with per-agent tool scoping — the Operations Lead touches the project board and nothing wider. The review covers one new platform, not one per task.

You brief it like a colleague, and the work happens inside controls you already understand. For a team that answers to clients, that is the difference between adding a vendor and adding a risk.

Where does a new tool land in your security review?
