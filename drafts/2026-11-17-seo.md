## Fix 2 — MEDIUM: 117 of 118 homepage images have empty alt text

**The problem.** Audited `/tmp` copy of the homepage: **118 `<img>` tags, 117 with `alt=""`** (one exception — the brand mark). Empty alt on the repeated integration-logo wall tiles is defensible (decorative repetition), but the same `alt=""` is applied to *content* images: the role avatars (Mochi, Strategy Lead, Sales & Growth…), the four how-it-works panel illustrations (stage-1/2/3.svg), and the CTA art (torii.svg, wave-crest.svg). ## Keyword-gap observation

**"AI workforce platform" — a named money keyword with zero URLs targeting it.**

04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets lists "AI workforce platform" as a money phrase, and 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md uses it as a consistent category phrase. I checked every URL in sitemap-0.xml: **no page title, slug, or section targets "workforce" anywhere on kohai.co.** The closest coverage is oblique — `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee`, `/blog/how-ai-employees-help-every-company-size`, `/solutions/smb`. Meanwhile competitors own the adjacent real estate: Relevance AI's entire positioning is "AI Workforce" (01-competitor-analysis.md §6), and Lindy/Marblism rank for workforce-language queries from their category pages.

Cheapest path: the homepage title is deliberately "AI teammates" (brand-protected — don't touch it), but the phrase can live on a page of its own without breaking the naming convention, exactly the way `what-is-an-ai-employee` carries the "AI employee" keyword while the site says "teammate". Suggested URL: `/blog/what-is-an-ai-workforce` — question-as-H2 structure per 06 § Stage 2, one comparison table (AI workforce platform vs. agent builders vs. point-solution AI tools), target queries: "AI workforce platform", "AI workforce for small business", "AI workforce vs AI agents". Internally link from /solutions/smb and the homepage "Connected on day one" section. This also preempts Relevance AI's head term instead of conceding it.
