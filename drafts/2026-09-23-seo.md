## Keyword-gap observation

**Kohai owns the "Kōhai vs. X" comparison-intent SERP position but has zero "X alternative" switcher-intent coverage, and the two highest-intent long-tails from the strategy are untargeted.**

The sitemap has 11 `/vs/*` pages (lindy, artisan, sintra, marblism, relevance-ai, 11x, claude, hermes, openclaw, viktor, virtual-assistant) — genuinely good coverage that matches competitor-analysis pillar 5. But "Lindy alternative" and "Artisan alternative" (both named money keywords in 04-content-strategy.md) are a **different query class**: searchers typing "vs." are still comparing; searchers typing "alternative" have already rejected the incumbent and are shopping for a replacement — typically higher conversion, and Lindy is already publishing head-to-head takedown content that siphons this traffic (01-competitor-analysis.md § Visible marketing channels). Related gaps with no matching page in the sitemap: "AI chief of staff" (Kohai literally ships a named Chief of Staff, Mochi — the product-feature-to-query fit is exact and no `/solutions/*` or blog page targets it) and "how to delegate ops to AI" (no operator-playbook page; content pillar 4 plans these but none are in the sitemap yet).

**Recommended action (in priority order):**
1. Ship `/lindy-alternative` and `/artisan-alternative` pages (or fold "alternative" intent into the existing /vs pages' titles + an H2 like "Why teams choose Kōhai as a Lindy alternative" — faster, reuses existing authority).
2. Ship an "AI chief of staff" page — Mochi's 5-step meeting flow (preps → briefs 15 min before → live notes → summary → follow-ups) is the answer; anchor it to the verified example-week numbers (24 deliverables, 31 hours reclaimed).
3. First operator-playbook post targets "how to delegate ops to AI" with the Monday-report workflow; add to sitemap on publish.
4. **Terminology note:** the site oscillates between "AI teammates" (homepage title/H1), "AI employees" (/features, /solutions), and "colleagues" (brand phrase). The money keywords are "AI employees" and "AI workforce platform." Keep "colleagues, not agents" as the brand line, but ensure each money page's `<title>`, H1, and first paragraph contain the exact keyword phrase — right now only /features and /solutions/smb (post-Fix-2) would.
