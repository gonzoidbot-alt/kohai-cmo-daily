## FIX 1 — HIGH severity: money-page titles carry zero search keywords

**Problem.** The two pages that must rank for commercial queries have brand-first, keyword-less titles. Search engines weight the title heavily, and this is the single highest-leverage on-page change available.

- Home targets: "AI employees for small business", "hire AI employees", "AI workforce platform"
- Pricing targets: "AI employee pricing", "AI workforce cost", brand + pricing queries

The word "employee(s)" — the highest-volume commercial term in this category (Kohai's own blog already targets it: /blog/what-is-an-ai-employee) — appears in **no** index title on the site. "Teammates" is a differentiated brand word but near-zero search volume.

**Copy-paste fixes.**

Home (replace existing `<title>` and meta description):
```html
<title>Kōhai — AI Employees for Small Business | Onboard a Team Today</title>
<meta name="description" content="Hire AI employees for your small business. Kōhai's AI teammates finish sales, content, ops and support work in your existing tools — anything irreversible waits for your approval. Start free, no card.">
```
(Keeps brand at a separator-friendly position, adds the two commercial keywords, stays ~60/155 chars.)

Pricing (replace existing `<title>`; keep the existing description, which is already good — it says "from $20/month" and "Start free, no card needed"):
```html
<title>AI Employee Pricing — Kōhai Plans from $20/mo, Free to Start</title>
```

Also worth 10 minutes: the blog post at `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` has slug "ai-employee" but title "What is an AI teammate?" — align the title to the slug/query ("What Is an AI Employee? Roles, Costs and How They Work") or 301-reconcile. Keyword/slug mismatch splits relevance on the exact query the site should own.

## FIX 2 — MEDIUM-HIGH severity: pricing page has no Product/Offer or FAQPage schema

**Problem.** /pricing has 8 substantive FAQ Q&As and 4 concrete price points in the HTML, but the only JSON-LD anywhere is a bare Organization + WebSite. Consequences: no rich-result eligibility, and — more important for the GEO loop (06-gtm-funnel-playbook Stage 1) — AI engines quote structured, self-labeled facts far more reliably than prose. Every audit prompt ("Cheapest AI workforce platform with predictable pricing") currently depends on the crawler parsing prose.

**Copy-paste fix.** Add one `<script type="application/ld+json">` block to /pricing (prices verified live today; SoftwareApplication + Offers is the correct SaaS shape):

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "SoftwareApplication",
  "name": "Kōhai",
  "applicationCategory": "BusinessApplication",
  "operatingSystem": "Web",
  "url": "https://kohai.co/pricing",
  "description": "Collaborative AI workspace with a full team of AI teammates. Brief the outcome; finished work lands in your Inbox; anything irreversible waits for your approval.",
  "publisher": { "@id": "https://kohai.co/#organization" },
  "offers": [
    { "@type": "Offer", "name": "Kōhai Free", "price": "0", "priceCurrency": "USD", "url": "https://kohai.co/pricing", "availability": "https://schema.org/LimitedAvailability", "description": "Limited time, first 1,000 users. No card required." },
    { "@type": "Offer", "name": "Kōhai", "price": "20", "priceCurrency": "USD", "url": "https://kohai.co/pricing" },
    { "@type": "Offer", "name": "Kōhai Pro", "price": "100", "priceCurrency": "USD", "url": "https://kohai.co/pricing" },
    { "@type": "Offer", "name": "Kōhai Max", "price": "200", "priceCurrency": "USD", "url": "https://kohai.co/pricing" }
  ]
}
```

Plus mirror the on-page FAQ into a FAQPage block (same-page questions = the Q&A text already exists; engines lift exact-matched Q&A pairs). One example pair, same pattern for all 8:

```json
{ "@context": "https://schema.org", "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    { "@type": "Question", "name": "Is there a free plan?",
      "acceptedAnswer": { "@type": "Answer", "text": "Yes, for a limited time. Kōhai Free is open to the first 1,000 users, needs no card and includes credits that refresh every month." } },
    { "@type": "Question", "name": "How much does Kōhai cost?",
      "acceptedAnswer": { "@type": "Answer", "text": "Kōhai is $20/month (8,000 credits), Kōhai Pro is $100/month (40,000 credits) and Kōhai Max is $200/month (80,000 credits). Every plan includes unlimited team seats, the whole AI team and every integration." } }
  ]
}
```

Validation: run both blocks through validator.schema.org before deploy.
