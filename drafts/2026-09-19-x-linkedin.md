## LinkedIn post (weekend founder-voice — Colleague thesis, pillar 1, ICP-2, 120–180 words)

A founder I know spent September interviewing for a part-time ops coordinator. Three weeks in, no hire, and the reporting still lands on her desk every Friday.

The alternative she didn't consider: brief an Operations Lead the way she'd have briefed the hire. Where the data lives, what the Friday report needs, what it's never allowed to touch. The team assembles itself, the report lands in her Library, and anything sensitive — payroll edits, invoice sends — waits at an approval gate until she says go.

The hire she was budgeting: part-time salary, onboarding, ramp time. The alternative: Pro tier: $99.99/mo for 40,000 credits and 3 seats. Three functions covered, cancel anytime.

You don't build agents. You hire colleagues and brief them like people.

That's the whole shift: hire, don't build.
