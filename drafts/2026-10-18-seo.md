## Fix #2 — Severity 2: comparison and pricing pages have quote-ready FAQ content but no FAQPage / Product schema

**Evidence (verified):** grep of fetched HTML shows every page ships only `Organization` + `WebSite` + `ImageObject` JSON-LD. Meanwhile:

- /pricing has **10 real FAQ questions** rendered in-page ("What's a credit?", "What's the difference between the plans?", "Is there a free plan?", "Do unused credits roll over?", …) — no FAQPage schema.
- Every /vs page has **3 real FAQ questions** ("Is Kōhai a good Lindy alternative?", "Can I customize Kōhai like Lindy?", "Which is faster to get going?") — no FAQPage schema.
- Blog posts ship no Article/BlogPosting schema.
- /pricing ships no Product/Offer schema for the $20/$100/$200 tiers.

The content 06 § Stage 2 says gets "quoted more than paragraphs" (tables, Q&A) already exists — it's just invisible to machines as structured data. Schema is exactly how ChatGPT/Perplexity citations and Google rich results pick these up.

**Snippet — FAQPage JSON-LD for /pricing (skeleton with the verified question texts; fill `<answer>` verbatim from the live page's answer paragraphs):**

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{"@context":"https://schema.org","@type":"FAQPage","mainEntity":[
 {"@type":"Question","name":"What's a credit?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"<answer>"}},
 {"@type":"Question","name":"What's the difference between the plans?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"<answer>"}},
 {"@type":"Question","name":"Is there a free plan?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"<answer>"}},
 {"@type":"Question","name":"Do unused credits roll over?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"<answer>"}},
 {"@type":"Question","name":"Can I switch plans mid-cycle?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"<answer>"}}
]}
</script>
```

Same pattern for /vs/lindy with `"name":"Is Kōhai a good Lindy alternative?"` etc. For blog posts, add Article schema (headline, datePublished, author → `@id` of the existing Organization). Since the site is Astro, this is one shared component — implement once, apply everywhere. (06 § month-2 plan already schedules "pricing/alternatives page rewrite for citation (schema markup)" — this is that work item, moved up because the content already exists and only the markup is missing.)

## Keyword-gap observation (vs 04 § SEO/GEO targets)

04 names **"AI employees for small business"** and **"hire AI employees"** as money keywords. Verified live: **zero page titles on kohai.co contain "AI employee(s)."** The site's vocabulary is uniformly "AI teammates" / "AI teams":

- Homepage title/H1: "Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished."
- /blog/what-is-an-ai-employee → title/H1: "What is an AI teammate?" (slug targets the keyword; title swaps the term)
- /blog/how-ai-employees-help-every-company-size → title: "How AI teammates help companies of every size"
- /blog/ai-employee-vs-virtual-assistant-vs-freelancer → title: "AI teammates vs. virtual assistants vs. freelancers…"
- "AI teams for small business" appears once (/blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business), still not "AI employees."

This is a deliberate brand-voice choice ("colleagues/teammates, not agents" — 02/03 glossary), and it's defensible in body copy. But the buyer's search vocabulary is "AI employees" — it's what Artisan, Lindy, Marblism and Sintra all rank on, and 04 explicitly targets it. The three URL slugs already named `ai-employee…` are paying the exact-match cost for nothing.

**Minimal-fix recommendation:** keep "teammates" in H1s and body (voice preserved); put "AI employee(s)" into the `<title>`/meta description of the three posts whose slugs already target it, e.g.:

- `What is an AI employee (AI teammate)? | Kōhai Blog`
- `AI employees vs. virtual assistants vs. freelancers: which is right for your business? | Kōhai Blog`
- `How AI employees help companies of every size | Kōhai Blog`

Cheapest possible exact-match coverage for the money keyword without touching brand voice anywhere else. Flag for Gabe: this bends 03's glossary in titles only — his call.
