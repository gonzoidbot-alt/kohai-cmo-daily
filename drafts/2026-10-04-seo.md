The blog covers it (/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee, /blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business — both in the sitemap ✓), but the homepage — the strongest authority signal — never bridges from the brand vocabulary to the category vocabulary. Comparison coverage is genuinely strong (11 /vs/* pages in the sitemap), so the gap isn't competitive pages — it's the head terms. **Low-risk move:** keep "colleagues, not agents" as the brand phrase, but work "AI employees for small business" into the home title/description and one above-the-fold sentence (e.g. title: "Kōhai | Hire AI employees — onboard AI teammates that finish the work"), and cross-link home → /blog/what-is-an-ai-employee. No competitor-mentions needed; this is vocabulary, not comparison content. *(Factual and generous framing: Lindy/Artisan/Sintra/Marblism each lead their own "AI employees" narrative — the term is the category's common language, which is why Kohai's absence from it on money pages costs queries.)*
