## LinkedIn post — Tuesday founder-voice workflow slot (the Monday queue; ICP-1 primary, ICP-2 readable; claim #1 + re-issued #2 pricing line; 120–180 words, numbers-led)

The queue used to set the tone for my Mondays.

Weekend tickets stacked up overnight — triage, duplicates, the two that couldn't wait. Before the first meeting, half the morning was gone just deciding what mattered.

Now the Customer Support Lead runs the triage before I'm at the desk: tickets sorted, duplicates merged, drafts of the replies waiting. Anything sensitive or client-facing sits at the approval gate until I read it. My Monday starts on the ones that need judgment, not the routine ones.

Kohai's example week: 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed. Support triage alone accounts for 5 of those hours.

The queue still exists. It just stopped being the first thing I do.

Kōhai Free is $0, no card. The first paid tier is $20/month for 8,000 credits.
