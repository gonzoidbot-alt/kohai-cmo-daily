Switching AI vendors shouldn't mean starting over.

You brief colleagues, not a vendor.

## LinkedIn post — Monday pillar-slot founder-voice post (the two-column audit; ICP-1 primary, ICP-2 readable; claim #1; 120–180 words, numbers-led)

Before you decide what to hand off, write down what you actually did last week. Two columns.

Column one: work only you can do. Column two: work that just has to happen — the growth report, the pipeline sweep, the invoice chases, the support queue.

For most operators I talk to, column two is most of the week. That's the list to delegate — not the strategy, the recurring work.

I briefed my Kohai colleagues on column two and left column one alone. Finished reports and follow-ups land in the Library; anything client-facing waits at the approval gate until I read it.

Kohai's example week: 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed. The breakdown: sales outreach 11 hours, content and SEO 8, ops and reporting 7, support triage 5.

Pull up your own column two. How many hours is it holding?
