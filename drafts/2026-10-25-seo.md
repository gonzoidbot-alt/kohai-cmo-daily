## Fix 1 (highest severity): Homepage title + H1 contain zero money keywords

**Severity: High.** The homepage is the site's highest-authority page and its title is brand-voice only:

- Current: `Kōhai | Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.`
- Meta description: also keyword-free ("collaborative AI workspace… fully staffed with AI teammates").

The money-keyword cluster is "AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees". Competitors own the exact vocabulary: Lindy leads with "AI employees", Relevance AI owns "AI Workforce" in search. Kohai's entire site says "teammates" — a word searchers do not use — so the brand's most authoritative page cannot rank for any commercial query in the set. "AI teammates" as a query is dominated by Slack/Teams and MS Teams content.

**Copy-paste fix (title tag):**
```html
<title>Kōhai — AI Employees for Small Business | Hire Your AI Workforce</title>
```
**Copy-paste fix (meta description):**
```html
<meta name="description" content="Hire AI employees for your small business. Kōhai is the AI workforce platform that comes fully staffed — a Chief of Staff plus a whole team of AI employees. Describe the outcome, get finished work back. Free to start.">
```
Keep "AI teammates" in body copy for brand voice; titles/H1s carry the search terms. This is worth doing before any new content: every internal link currently points its anchor equity at a keyword-free title.

## Fix 2 (second priority): /vs/ pages don't target "X alternative" queries — Lindy is already running this exact playbook against the category

**Severity: High.** The site already has 12 comparison pages (good instinct — Lindy's most visible channel is competitive SEO content like its "Sintra AI Pricing" takedown pages), but the titles are non-commercial:

- Current `/vs/lindy`: `Kōhai vs. Lindy: which should you choose?` — no "alternative", no "pricing", no "review". Money keyword **"Lindy alternative"** is unserved.
- Same pattern on all 12 (`Kōhai vs. Sintra: which should you choose?` etc.). "Artisan alternative" likewise unserved despite `/vs/artisan` existing.

Buyers comparing tools type "lindy alternative" / "sintra vs marblism" / "lindy pricing", not "which should you choose". These pages are the closest thing Kohai has to capturing competitor-brand search demand and they're invisible to it.

**Copy-paste fix (title pattern for /vs/lindy, repeat for each competitor):**
```html
<title>Kōhai vs. Lindy (2026): The Lindy Alternative That Finishes the Work</title>
<meta name="description" content="Looking for a Lindy alternative? Kōhai gives you a ready-made team of AI employees instead of per-user agents you build yourself. Compare features and 2026 pricing side by side.">
```
**On-page addition:** add a pricing comparison table to each /vs/ page with both vendors' *current* tiers. Kohai's column is verified: Free $0, $20/mo (8,000 credits), $100/mo (40,000), $200/mo (80,000), unlimited seats on every plan. Competitor columns must be re-verified at publish time — the Sept doc's figures (Lindy $29.99/$99.99/$199.99 per user; Sintra promo pricing) predate known pricing churn and are stale. Lindy's pricing has changed repeatedly in 2025–2026, so date-stamp the competitor side of the table ("Lindy pricing checked [date]").

*Secondary note while in there:* the pricing page has Organization/WebSite JSON-LD but no Product/Offer or FAQPage schema — a cheap add for a page whose SERP competitors all show rich results.

## Keyword-gap observation: "AI chief of staff" — a money-adjacent long-tail Kohai owns in product but not in search

Of the target long-tails ("how to delegate ops to AI", "AI chief of staff", "AI team for solo founder"), **"AI chief of staff" is the standout gap because Kohai has a named Chief of Staff teammate (the Mochi roster character) as a core product concept — it's on the homepage hero flow — yet no page on the site targets the phrase.** Sitemap check: zero URLs contain "chief-of-staff"; no blog post or solutions page targets it.

- The searcher intent ("what is an AI chief of staff" / "AI chief of staff for founders") maps exactly to ICP-1 Solo Operator and to existing product behavior: it splits your brief into tasks, assigns teammates, preps meetings, runs follow-ups. The homepage's meeting-prep section is this keyword's content, mislabeled.
- None of the six analyzed competitors lead with "Chief of Staff" as a product concept — this is an ownable phrase rather than a contested one, unlike "AI employees" (Lindy) and "AI Workforce" (Relevance AI).
- Suggested move: one pillar page (`/solutions/ai-chief-of-staff` or `/blog/what-is-an-ai-chief-of-staff`) targeting the head term, interlinked from the homepage roster section and the solopreneurs solutions page (whose current title, `Solopreneurs | Kōhai`, also carries no keyword and could become `AI Team for Solo Founders | Kōhai` to catch the third long-tail).
- Secondary gap noted for the backlog: "how to delegate ops to AI" has no targeting content either; `/solutions/operations` exists but its title wasn't keyword-bearing in the pattern of the rest of the site.
