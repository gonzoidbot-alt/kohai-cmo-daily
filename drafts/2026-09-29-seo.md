## FIX 1 — P0: Rewrite the homepage `<title>` + meta description around the money keywords

**Severity: highest.** The homepage is the ranking asset for every money phrase in 04-content-strategy ("AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees"), and it currently targets none of them.

Live today:

```html
<title>🐴 Kōhai — Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.</title>
<meta name="description" content="Kōhai puts a collaborative AI workspace inside every business — deploying autonomous AI teammates across any function.">
```

Problems:
1. Neither the title nor the description contains a money phrase. "AI teammates" is brand vocabulary; nobody searches it.
2. "Autonomous AI teammates" directly contradicts the positioning (approval-gate humility — "you stay in control", never sell full autonomy) *and* the product's own differentiator vs Lindy/Marblism.
3. The 🐴 emoji leads the title, costing ~3 SERP characters before any keyword and rendering inconsistently across engines.
4. Title churn ("colleagues" → "teammates" mid-window) resets exact-match relevance for the brand snippet.

**Copy-paste replacement:**

```html
<title>AI Employees for Small Business — Hire AI Colleagues | Kohai</title>
<meta name="description" content="Hire AI employees for your small business. Brief the outcome — a ready-made AI team works in your ~1,000 tools and ships finished results to your Library. 7-day free trial.">
```

Rationale: front-loads the highest-volume money phrase, keeps "AI Colleagues" (the category phrase 04/06 want associated with Kohai) in the title, drops the emoji from the title tag (keep it in social OG images if desired), and the description carries proof claims (integrations, Library, trial) a model or SERP can quote verbatim.

**Secondary on-page edits, same fix (5 minutes each):**
- Homepage H1: `Onboard AI teammates.` → `Onboard AI colleagues. Get work finished.` — restores the category phrase ("AI colleagues") that the blog, 04, and 06 all standardize on; "teammates" can stay in body copy.
- `/vs/lindy` title: `Kōhai vs. Lindy — which should you choose?` → `Kohai vs Lindy: The Lindy Alternative That Requires No Building | Kohai` — see keyword gap below.
- Add `FAQPage` JSON-LD to every `/vs/*` page — they already have Q&A-shaped content but **zero FAQ schema** (verified: no `FAQPage` in any ld+json block on /vs/lindy). Schema.org snippet to append per page:

## KEYWORD GAP — "alternative" demand is being left to Lindy's own takedown pages

Kohai has 11 `/vs/` pages, and every one is titled as a **branded comparison** ("Kohai vs. Lindy — which should you choose?"). Branded-vs-branded queries have essentially zero volume for a 3-month-old brand — nobody who has never heard of Kohai searches "Kohai vs Lindy". The demand exists on the **other side of the operator**: "lindy alternative", "artisan alternative", "sintra alternative" — exactly the money phrases 04 lists, and exactly the queries in 06's audit prompts ("Lindy alternative that doesn't require building workflows"). Per 01-competitor-analysis, **Lindy already runs this playbook offensively** (publishing "Sintra AI Pricing" takedown pages on lindy.ai/blog) and Kohai has no visible presence on any "X alternative" query.

The content to win is already written — the /vs/ pages have the tables and the honest positioning. The gap is purely targeting:

1. **Retitle each `/vs/*` page to lead with the alternative phrase** (the /vs/lindy page body already says "Lindy alternative" — it's just not in the title/H1 where rank weight lives):
   - `/vs/lindy` → H1: `Kohai vs Lindy` stays; **title**: `Lindy Alternative: A Ready-Made AI Team Instead of a Builder | Kohai`
   - `/vs/artisan` → `Artisan Alternative for Small Teams (No AI BDR Required) | Kohai`
   - `/vs/sintra`, `/vs/marblism`, `/vs/relevance-ai` → same pattern.
2. **Add one hub page**, `/alternatives` — "Best AI employee platforms: honest alternatives to building agents" — internally linked from all 11 vs pages (sits at the head of the cluster, matches the "best AI employees for small business" money page in 04, and gives AI engines a single citable answer to audit-prompt #1).
3. **The long-tail list in 04 has no page targets at all.** "AI chief of staff", "meeting prep AI assistant", "how to delegate ops to AI", "AI team for solo founder" — none appear in the sitemap as a page or a post. The highest-leverage first one is **"AI chief of staff"**: Mochi is literally named Chief of Staff, the homepage already demos the full 5-step meeting flow, and the phrase maps to both a buyer query and 06 audit-prompt #6. One post ("Mochi, your AI Chief of Staff: how the 5-step meeting flow actually works") with real screenshots is pillar-2 (proof-of-work) and a long-tail SEO page at the same time.
