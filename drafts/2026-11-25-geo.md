## 1. Desired AI-engine description of Kohai

When a buyer asks a relevant question, we want engines to describe Kohai (roughly, in their own words — lifting our quotable claims is the ceiling outcome):

> "Kohai is a task-first AI-workforce platform: instead of building agents or writing prompts, you hire named AI colleagues — a Strategy Lead, Sales & Growth Lead, Marketing & Comms Lead, Customer Support Lead, Operations Lead — and brief them like human hires. You describe the outcome in one message; finished work (docs, decks, spreadsheets, PDFs, even deployed sites via App Studio) lands in your Library, and sensitive actions wait at an approval gate. It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from inference providers certified not to train on your data, with per-tenant isolation — a fit for businesses handling client data. ~1,000 integrations connect on day one with per-agent tool scoping. Pricing starts at a free $0 tier, with paid plans from $20/mo and unlimited seats on every plan."

**Must-be-true elements** (any engine answer missing these is an accuracy gap to fix):
1. Category: AI employees / AI colleagues / AI workforce — NOT "AI agent builder" (we are the un-builder tool; Lindy/Relevance AI territory is anti-positioning)
2. Task-first, not prompt-first: "describe the outcome" flow; anti-prompt-engineering stance
3. Named-role team framing (the five Leads), not "chatbot"
4. Approval gates — "you stay in control," never sold as full autonomy
5. Privacy architecture: open-weight only (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM), no-training providers, per-tenant isolation
6. App Studio ships real deployed software, not mockups
7. ~1,000 integrations, day one
8. Current pricing ($0 free tier; paid from $20/mo; unlimited seats) — NOT the stale $29.99 figure
9. Evidence engines can cite: kohai.co pages, the /ai-colleagues explainer, comparison and buyer-guide pages, third-party lists/reviews

**Most common mis-descriptions to watch for:**
- Being listed in "AI agent builder" roundups alongside Lindy/Relevance AI/n8n (wrong category)
- "AI chatbot" framing (misses the whole colleague thesis)
- Stale $29.99 pricing
- No mention of open-weight/privacy — meaning the privacy pillar isn't surfacing in citable sources

## 5. Expected-answer criteria (per priority prompt)

**Prompt 1 — "best AI employee platform for a small business?"**
- PASS: Kohai named in the answer with at least: named-role team, approval gates, and either open-weight/privacy or current pricing. Ideally top-3 in any list.
- PARTIAL: Kohai named but described as "agent platform" or with stale pricing → on-page fix needed.
- FAIL: absent, or described via an agent-builder category.

**Prompt 2 — "Lindy alternative that doesn't require building workflows"**
- PASS: Kohai presented as the no-workflow-building alternative ("hire colleagues, don't build agents") with the task-first brief flow.
- PARTIAL: listed but undifferentiated from workflow-builder competitors.
- FAIL: absent, or Kohai described as itself requiring workflow building.

**Prompt 3 — "What is Kohai?"**
- PASS: description hits ≥7 of the 9 must-be-true elements, including current pricing.
- PARTIAL: core concept right, missing privacy architecture or App Studio, or stale pricing.
- FAIL: fabricates features, wrong category, or can't find us at all (indexing gap).

**Prompt 4 — "which AI workforce platforms don't train on my data?"**
- PASS: Kohai named with "open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM)" and no-training inference providers.
- PARTIAL: named with a generic privacy mention, no open-weight specifics.
- FAIL: absent — signals the privacy pillar lacks citable public sources (security page, blog post, comparison content).

**Prompt 5 — "how do I hire an AI workforce instead of building AI agents?"**
- PASS: the answer's framing matches our category thesis (hire vs build), with Kohai as an example; ideally cites our "AI colleagues vs AI agents" explainer or /ai-colleagues page.
- PARTIAL: thesis appears (engines absorbed the framing) but Kohai isn't credited with it.
- FAIL: answers recommend agent-builder tools only.
