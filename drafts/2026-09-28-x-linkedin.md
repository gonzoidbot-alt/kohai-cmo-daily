An agency owner asked me the question every founder asks before their first AI hire: what stops it from sending something embarrassing?

The answer is a gate, not a promise. Her Marketing & Comms Lead drafts the client newsletter, then it waits at an approval gate. She reads it with her morning coffee, edits two lines, says go. Nothing leaves the building on its own.

She told me the habit changed how she manages her human team, too — one review queue at 8:30 instead of a week of checking in.

That's the design principle underneath Kohai: brief it like a colleague, keep the gate for anything sensitive, and the person whose name is on the work stays the person who decides.
