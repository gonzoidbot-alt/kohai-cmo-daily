## Title

Show HN: Kohai – describe an outcome, a team of open-weight AI colleagues does the work

## Text

Hi HN, we're launching Kohai (https://kohai.co) today.

The short version: instead of building and managing AI agents, you describe an outcome — "prep Monday's growth report" or "find 50 e-commerce leads and draft the outreach" — and a team assembles itself. A Chief of Staff agent (Mochi) splits the goal and hands tasks to specialist roles (research, outreach, content, ops), which work inside the integration stack you already use (Gmail, Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, ~1,000 apps). Finished work — docs, sheets, PDFs, even deployed sites — lands in a Library. Actions that touch the outside world (sending email, moving CRM stages, deploying sites) pause at approval gates you configure.

A few things we think are genuinely different, and that HN will want to push on:

1. Open-weight models only. Kohai runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) through inference providers certified not to train on your data, with per-tenant isolation and encryption. No frontier-API dependency, no data-for-training trade.

2. Credits instead of tokens. Pricing is a flat monthly credit pool ($29.99/12k, $99.99/40k, $199.99/80k) and we publish what work costs: a short research task is ~5–10 credits, a blog post 20–50, a deck or market-research job 100–300. The goal is that you can price work before you assign it — no surprise token bills.

3. Task-first, not prompt-first. There's no agent builder or workflow canvas. That's a deliberate product stance, not a limitation — we think most small teams want to brief a colleague, not architect a workflow.

Happy to answer anything about the architecture, the model stack, or why we think "hire, don't build" is the right side of this market. Roast away.
