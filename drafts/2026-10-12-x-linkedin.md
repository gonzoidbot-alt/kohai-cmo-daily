## LinkedIn post — Tuesday slot (the AI line item that didn't grow; ICP-2 primary; claim #5; 120–180 words)

A client asked last month why our AI spend hadn't moved, even though the workload had.

The honest answer: the work outgrew the format, not the budget. We started by briefing a weekly summary of the numbers. A few iterations later, the same request had become a small internal app the team still opens every Monday. App Studio ships deployed software, not mockups.

That's what a task-first AI workforce changes in a budget review. You're not buying a new tool per job; one credit pool spans a drafted document at one end and a running application at the other. A blog post runs 20–50 credits — an application sits higher — and the finished work lands in your Library either way.

The line item didn't grow. The value per credit did.

When your workload grows, does your AI budget have to?
