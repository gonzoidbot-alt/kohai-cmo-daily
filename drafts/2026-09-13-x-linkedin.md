> The first AI tool bill I couldn't explain was the moment I started building Kohai.
>
> Token pricing feels like a utility bill written in a language you don't speak. You can't budget it, can't predict it, and can't answer "what did last month's work actually cost?"
>
> So we went the other direction. Open-weight models only — DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM — from certified no-training providers. That choice does two things at once: your work stays yours, and the cost curve stays sane.
>
> Then we made pricing legible. Credits, not tokens. A blog post: 20–50 credits. A deck: 100–300. You see what a job costs before you assign it — the same way you'd estimate hours for a human hire.
>
> Your first AI hire: $29.99/month, 12,000 credits, 7-day free trial. Flat, known, cancellable.
>
> If you run a business on AI tools: can you state what last month's AI work cost you? Most operators can't.
