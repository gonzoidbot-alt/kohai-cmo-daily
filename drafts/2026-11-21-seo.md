## Fix 2 — SEVERITY: HIGH. robots.txt blocks the AI retrieval crawlers the GEO strategy depends on

**Evidence (curl, 2026-11-21):** robots.txt carries the Cloudflare managed content block with hard `Disallow: /` for **GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Claude-Web, anthropic-ai, Google-Extended, CCBot, KimiBot, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, Bytespider, Diffbot, omgili, meta-externalagent, GoogleOther** and more, plus `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference`.

**Why it matters:** 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md Stage 1 is AI visibility ("being quotable by models is a separate job"), and there's a monthly AI-visibility audit planned. But the crawlers that power ChatGPT Search (GPTBot / OAI-SearchBot ecosystem) and Claude retrieval (ClaudeBot) are globally disallowed — the site can't appear in the very engine answers the audit measures. This also contradicts our own `llms.txt`, which explicitly invites language models to read the sitemap. PerplexityBot is *not* blocked (good).

**Nuance for Gabe's call:** privacy is a brand pillar, and `ai-train=no` is the right stance and worth keeping. The conflict is only between *training* crawlers (fine to block) and *retrieval/search* crawlers (blocking them silences us in AI answers). Recommended: keep every training bot blocked, but allow the retrieval/user-prompted ones by adding explicit allows BEFORE the Cloudflare managed block:

```
# Allow AI retrieval & search crawlers (they ground answers, they don't train)
User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /
User-agent: ChatGPT-User
Allow: /
User-agent: ClaudeBot
Allow: /
User-agent: PerplexityBot
Allow: /
User-agent: Googlebot
Allow: /
```

(Then confirm the Cloudflare managed block that follows doesn't re-disallow them — user-agent-specific Allow after a group `User-agent: *` Disallow does not override, so these must precede the block or the managed ruleset must be edited in Cloudflare.) Also: `https://kohai.co/sitemap.xml` returns the 404 page — harmless (robots.txt points to `sitemap-index.xml`, which works), but cheap to redirect for tools that guess the default path.
