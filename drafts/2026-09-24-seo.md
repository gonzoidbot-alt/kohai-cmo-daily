## Prioritized fix #1 — Publish llms.txt (severity: HIGH, effort: ~30 min)

**Why first:** 51% of B2B buyers start research with an AI chatbot (06, G2 Mar 2026), and Kohai's ICP skews even more AI-native. llms.txt is the cheapest lever for controlling how models describe the product, it was already committed as a week-1 deliverable in 04, and it's been missing since the site went live (09-09). Every /vs page and the /ai-colleagues explainer content exists to be quoted — this file is the index that points models at it.

**Snippet — deploy at `https://kohai.co/llms.txt`:**

```
# Kōhai
> Kōhai (kohai.co) is a task-first AI workforce platform: you hire AI colleagues, brief them like a teammate, and finished work (docs, decks, spreadsheets, deployed sites) lands in your Library. Runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from certified no-training providers, with approval gates on sensitive actions and per-agent tool scoping across ~1,000 integrations.

Categories start at $19.99/month (8,000 credits); 7-day free trial.

## Product
- [Home](https://kohai.co/): Onboard AI colleagues — describe the outcome, a team assembles, deliverables land in your Library
- [Features](https://kohai.co/features): Team roster, ~1,000 integrations, approval gates, recurring work, App Studio
- [Pricing](https://kohai.co/pricing): Three plans, $19.99–$199.99/mo, credits + seats, 7-day free trial
- [Technology](https://kohai.co/technology): Open-weight models only (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM), no-training providers, per-tenant isolation

## Compare
- [Kōhai vs. Lindy](https://kohai.co/vs/lindy)
- [Kōhai vs. Sintra](https://kohai.co/vs/sintra)
- [Kōhai vs. Artisan](https://kohai.co/vs/artisan)
- [Kōhai vs. Marblism](https://kohai.co/vs/marblism)
- [All comparisons](https://kohai.co/vs)

## Key facts (verified)
- Pricing: $19.99/mo (8,000 credits) · $99.99 Pro (40,000 credits, 3 seats) · $199.99 Max (80,000 credits, unlimited seats)
- Privacy: open-weight models only; providers certified not to train on customer data; per-tenant isolation and encryption
- Control: sensitive actions wait at an approval gate; per-agent tool scoping
- Example week: 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed
```

(Confirm the price line against the pricing page at deploy time — see pricing-drift note above. If the entry tier moves again, update this file; llms.txt with a wrong number is worse than none.)

## Prioritized fix #2 — Add Offer schema to /pricing and FAQPage schema to /pricing + /vs/* (severity: HIGH, effort: ~half a day)

**Why second:** The pricing page carries the strongest conversion facts on the site — three prices, three credit amounts, a 7-day trial — but ships zero structured data, so Google can't render a rich result and AI engines have no machine-readable price anchor. The FAQ sections on /pricing and all 11 /vs pages are already written in ideal citation format (question as H3, answer in 2 sentences) and are invisible to rich results. Lindy wins competitive queries partly on SERP real estate; this is how Kohai competes without a domain-rating arms race. Add Article schema to the 14 blog posts in the same pass.

## Keyword-gap observation — no page targets "alternative" queries, and the money pages don't carry the money phrases

04-content-strategy.md lists five money targets: "AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees", "Lindy alternative", "Artisan alternative". Live check today:

- **"Alternative" queries: zero coverage.** The /vs/ hub has 11 comparison pages (Lindy, Sintra, Artisan, Marblism, Relevance AI, 11x, Claude, and others) — good coverage of "X vs Y" phrasing, zero of "X alternative". Titles are uniformly "Kōhai vs. Lindy — which should you choose?" with a 63-character description. Meanwhile Lindy is actively publishing competitive takedowns (e.g. "Sintra AI Pricing" on lindy.ai/blog) to capture exactly this search behavior. Cheapest fix: add an H2 on each /vs page shaped "Looking for a Lindy alternative?" with 2 sentences that name the product once and state the concrete difference — that single block makes the page eligible for "Lindy alternative" without a new URL. If only one new page gets built, make it "Best Lindy alternatives for small business (2026)" — it can rank for all five competitors at once.
- **Homepage title misses the category phrases.** Live title: "Kōhai — Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished." Neither "AI employees" nor "AI workforce" appears anywhere in the title or the 118-character description. Those are two of the five money keywords, and the homepage is the page most likely to rank for them. Suggested title: `Kōhai — Hire AI Employees That Get Work Finished` (48 chars), description: `Hire AI employees that work in your ~1,000 tools and deliver finished work to your Library. Open-weight models, approval gates, from $19.99/mo.` (144 chars).
- **"AI employees for small business" is half-covered.** /solutions/smb is titled "Small businesses — Kōhai" and /blog/ai-for-small-business exists, but neither title carries the phrase "AI employees for small business". The blog post is the natural ranking vehicle: work the exact phrase into its H1 and title.
