## Fix #1 — HIGH severity: missing llms.txt + zero page-level schema (GEO visibility)

**Problem.** 04-content-strategy.md lists this as a GEO must-fix: "publish llms.txt, clear product schema, comparison pages." As of today llms.txt is a 404, and the only structured data on the whole site is a sitewide Organization + WebSite block. The /vs/* comparison pages — the exact asset AI engines quote for "Lindy alternative" queries — carry no FAQPage, Article, or BreadcrumbList schema, and the pricing page has no Product/Offer schema. Lindy is running competitive SEO content; Relevance AI owns "AI Workforce" in enterprise search. Kohai's quotable pages are structurally invisible to answer engines even though the HTML is ungated.

**Fix A: llms.txt.** Serve a static file at https://kohai.co/llms.txt (one-time add to the Astro `public/` dir):

```
# Kohai
> Kohai is an AI workforce platform: you brief AI colleagues in plain language, a team assembles itself, and finished work lands in your Library. Open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM), certified no-training providers, and approval gates on every sensitive action.

- Home: https://kohai.co/
- Pricing: https://kohai.co/pricing ($20/mo entry, 8,000 credits, unlimited seats; free tier available)
- Features: https://kohai.co/features
- What is an AI teammate?: https://kohai.co/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee
- Kohai vs Lindy: https://kohai.co/vs/lindy
- All comparisons: https://kohai.co/vs
- AI for small business: https://kohai.co/blog/ai-for-small-business
- Blog: https://kohai.co/blog
```

(Adjust the summary line to whatever claim-check approves; keep it under ~1,024 chars so models lift it whole.)

**Fix B: FAQPage schema on every /vs page.** Each /vs page already has Q&A-shaped content; wrap the top 3 questions in JSON-LD. Snippet for /vs/lindy (repeat pattern per page):

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "What is the difference between Kohai and Lindy?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Lindy is a builder: you assemble AI assistants and workflows yourself. Kohai is task-first — you brief it like a colleague, a team assembles itself around the outcome, and finished deliverables land in your Library. Both have approval gates; Kohai runs exclusively on open-weight models."
      }
    },
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "Is Kohai a good Lindy alternative?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Yes, if you don't want to build agents. Kohai includes the whole team on every plan with unlimited seats, starting at $20/month with 8,000 credits, and a free tier with no card required."
      }
    }
  ]
}
```

**Fix C: Product + Offer schema on /pricing** (three `Product` blocks, one per plan, `offers` with `price: "20"/"100"/"200"`, `priceCurrency: "USD"`, `priceSpecification: monthly`). Pricing pages with Offer schema are the most-cited source type for "cheapest AI workforce platform"-class prompts.

**Effort:** llms.txt ~30 min; schema template + apply to 10 /vs pages + pricing ~half a day. Highest GEO leverage per hour available.

## Fix #2 — MEDIUM-HIGH severity: title tags miss the money keywords

**Problem.** Titles are well-formed (canonicals, OG, Twitter cards all clean — better than average) but they brand-first instead of intent-first, and they use "AI teammates" where the strategy (04, 06 Stage 1) says establish "AI colleagues" as the category phrase. Current vs. suggested (copy-paste):

| Page | Current title | Suggested title | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| / | `Kōhai \| Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.` (48ch) | `Kōhai — AI Colleagues That Finish the Work` (43ch) | Plants "AI colleagues" in the strongest title slot on the site; current one spends it on "teammates," which appears nowhere in the money-keyword list. |
| /pricing | `Pricing \| Kōhai` (15ch) | `Pricing — AI Colleague Plans from $20/mo \| Kōhai` (49ch) | 15 characters is a wasted title on a money page; "$20/mo" is a quotable number and undercuts Sintra/Marblism comparisons in the SERP. |
| /vs/lindy | `Kōhai vs. Lindy: which should you choose?` | `Kōhai vs. Lindy — the no-build Lindy Alternative` (48ch) | "Lindy alternative" is a named money keyword (04); the word "alternative" appears exactly once on the whole page today. Lindy's playbook is exactly this head-to-head content. |
| /vs/* (pattern) | `Kōhai vs. {X}: which should you choose?` | `Kōhai vs. {X} — {X} Alternative Without the Building` | Same fix across all 10 /vs pages; each is a commercial-intent query Kohai currently doesn't name in its title. |

Meta descriptions are acceptable lengths (127–197 chars) — no changes needed there this pass.

**Effort:** ~1 hour including the /vs pattern sweep. Pairs with Fix #1 (title + FAQPage schema on the same pages in one PR).

## Keyword-gap observation — "AI chief of staff" (long-tail, zero coverage)

**The gap.** 04-content-strategy.md lists "AI chief of staff" as a long-tail target, and Kohai has the single strongest possible asset for it: **Mochi, your Chief of Staff**, with a fully specified 5-step meeting workflow (prep → 15-min brief → live notes → summary → follow-ups). Yet a sitemap crawl of all 52 URLs shows zero pages targeting the phrase — it appears only as product copy on the homepage. No blog post, no landing page, no FAQ.

**Why it matters.** The searchers behind "AI chief of staff" are self-qualified ICP-1/ICP-2 buyers describing the exact role they want to hire — and unlike "AI workforce platform," no competitor in the set (Lindy, Relevance AI, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism) owns the phrase in search either. It's an open long-tail where Kohai has a native product answer nobody else can name.

**Recommended play (per 04 cadence — Friday SEO post):** one ~1,000-word ungated page, e.g. `kohai.co/blog/ai-chief-of-staff` — "What does an AI Chief of Staff actually do? Mochi's week, task by task" — structured with question-H2s, the 5-step meeting flow as a numbered list, real screenshot of the 9:45 brief, and an internal link to / and /solutions/solopreneurs. Then add a "Chief of Staff" FAQ entry to the homepage schema so models can quote the role definition. Secondary gaps worth queueing after this one: "hire AI employees" (no page speaks in "hire" language outside the homepage hero) and "Lindy alternative" (page exists but see Fix #2 — it never says the words).
