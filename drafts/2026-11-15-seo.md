## Prioritized SEO Fixes

### Fix 1 (HIGH) — Homepage meta description exceeds SERP cutoff

**Problem:** The homepage meta description is ~280 characters. Google truncates to ~155–160 in SERPs, meaning the last sentence ("and anything irreversible waits for your approval.") gets cut off entirely. This sentence contains the approval-gate differentiator, which is the single strongest wedge against competitors (Lindy, Artisan, Sintra).

Current meta: `Kōhai puts a collaborative AI workspace inside every business, fully staffed with AI teammates from day one. They finish work across any function, and anything irreversible waits for your approval.`

**Fix — replace with ≤155 chars:**

```html
<meta name="description" content="Onboard AI teammates who finish work across every function. Anything irreversible waits for your approval. Start free, no card needed.">
```

Character count: 154. Preserves: "AI teammates" (category term), "every function" (breadth), "approval" (differentiator), "Start free" (CTA hook). Drops the verbose "Kōhai puts a collaborative AI workspace inside every business..." framing that doesn't survive truncation anyway.

**Same fix applies to og:description and twitter:description** — they currently mirror the same 280-char string.

---

### Fix 2 (HIGH) — No dedicated competitor comparison landing pages

**Problem:** Lindy.ai publishes aggressive head-to-head SEO content (e.g., "Sintra AI Pricing" takedown). Kohai's strategy targets /lindy-alternative and /artisan-alternative as money pages, but:
- `https://kohai.co/lindy-alternative` → 404
- `https://kohai.co/artisan-alternative` → 404
- `https://kohai.co/vs` exists (200) but is a single generic comparison page — no dedicated, competitor-specific landing pages exist.

These queries ("Lindy alternative", "Artisan alternative", "best AI workforce platform") are high-intent — the searcher is actively evaluating and has already heard of a competitor. Lindy is competing here. Kohai is not.

**Fix — create at minimum two dedicated comparison pages, structured per GTM funnel Stage 2 guidance:**

1. `/lindy-alternative` — target: "Lindy alternative", "Kohai vs Lindy"
2. `/artisan-alternative` — target: "Artisan alternative", "Kohai vs Artisan"

Each page should follow the H2-as-question format from 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md:
- H2: "How does Kohai compare to Lindy?"
- Answer in first 2 sentences, then detail
- Table comparing: pricing per seat, credit economics, model policy (open-weight vs frontier), approval gates vs building workflows, privacy posture
- End with: "Start free on Kohai — your first AI teammate delivers today."

These pages serve double duty: SEO ranking AND the comparison/buyer-guide content cadence from Stage 2 of the funnel.

## Keyword-Gap Observation

### Missing: Dedicated "AI workforce" category landing page

**Gap:** The strategy explicitly targets "AI workforce platform" as a money keyword (04-content-strategy.md), but no page on kohai.co has this phrase in its title, H1, or meta description. The closest is the homepage H1: "Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished." — but this does not contain "AI workforce" or "AI employees".

**Competitor context:**
- **Relevance AI** positions heavily around "AI Workforce" (LinkedIn-heavy GTM, GTMAE positioning per 01-competitor-analysis.md)
- **Artisan** brands its AI agents as "AI employees" with named roles (Intern, Employee)
- **Sintra** uses "AI Helpers"

Kohai uses "teammates" / "colleagues" language intentionally (per positioning guide), but this creates a lexical disconnect: the searcher typing "AI workforce platform" into Google won't match anything on kohai.co directly. The term "workforce" does not appear in any page title, H1, or meta description.

**Recommendation:** Create a dedicated `/ai-workforce` or `/ai-employees` landing page that:
- Title: "AI Employees & AI Workforce Platform | Kōhai"
- H1: "Hire an AI workforce without adding payroll"
- Naturally incorporates the "teammates" language lower on the page, but surfaces the search-mapped terms in headings and meta
- Includes pricing table ($0/$20/$100/$200 — unlimited seats) and the example-week stats (24 deliverables, 31 hours)
- Links to /pricing, /features, and the solutions pages

This is the single highest-ROI content page Kohai could ship: it maps to a category search term, serves ICP-1 and ICP-2 intent directly, and none of the solution subpages (solutions/solopreneurs, solutions/smb) currently own this keyword.
