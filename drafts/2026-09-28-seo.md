## Keyword-gap observation (vs. competitors — updated for today's crawl)

**The 11 /vs pages target "Kohai vs. X" but nothing on the domain targets "[competitor] alternative" or "[competitor] pricing" — the exact queries Lindy uses to siphon this space, and the bigger pool: people who know the competitor but have never heard of Kohai.**

Per 01-competitor-analysis.md, Lindy *"publishes head-to-head pricing takedowns of competitors (e.g. 'Sintra AI Pricing' on lindy.ai/blog)"* and competitive SEO content is named as Kohai's highest-leverage gap. Today's crawl confirms the gap is still fully open: all 11 /vs pages are titled "Kōhai vs. X — which should you choose?" (comparison intent from people who already know Kohai), and all 17 blog posts are first-principles explainers or generic listicles ("Best AI agents for small business") — **zero "[competitor] alternative" or "[competitor] pricing" pages**. "Lindy alternative" and "Artisan alternative" are both listed money keywords in 04-content-strategy.md and in 06's AI-visibility audit prompts; nothing on kohai.co answers them. Low-hanging sub-fix: /vs/lindy's meta description and an H2 phrased "Is Kohai a good Lindy alternative?" would let the existing page rank for the alternative query with no new page.

**Recommendation (draft-page recommendations only — nothing published):** one alternatives/pricing post per high-traffic competitor, starting with the price-twin: "Lindy alternatives for solo founders" and "Lindy pricing explained (and what you get for it)" — factual and generous per pillar 5 in 04-content-strategy.md, each internally linked to its /vs page and /pricing. Structure per 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md Stage 2: question as H2, answer in the first two sentences, pricing table (tables get quoted by AI engines). Secondary: "Sintra alternative" (Kohai's exact ICP; Sintra ranks its own discount pages but nobody owns the alternative query honestly) and "Artisan alternative" (Artisan's own pricing page is sales-scoped, leaving a factual self-serve-pricing opening).
