## Fix 1 — HIGH: robots.txt blocks the AI-retrieval crawlers our strategy depends on

**The problem.** `https://kohai.co/robots.txt` (Cloudflare-managed) blocks, among others: **Google-Extended** (powers Gemini grounding + AI Overviews citations), **anthropic-ai / Claude-Web** (Claude retrieval), **omgili**, **Diffbot**, **KimiBot**, plus training-only bots (GPTBot, CCBot, ClaudeBot, Bytespider, GoogleOther). The block list conflates *training* crawlers with *retrieval* crawlers. This directly undermines the Stage-1 goal in 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md (be quotable by AI engines) and makes the GEO channel's monthly visibility audit partially unwinnable — some of the engines we audit can't read the site at all.

**Why it's the top fix.** Every other SEO/GEO investment (vs/ pages, llms.txt, blog) is capped until retrieval bots are allowed in. Unblocking costs nothing and does not weaken the privacy brand: the brand promise is "no training on your data" — blocking *training* bots can stay. The fix is to unblock only *retrieval* bots.

**Copy-paste change** — in the Cloudflare Managed Content block list, delete these entries (keep the training bots):

```
# REMOVE from blocklist (retrieval/grounding, not training):
User-agent: Google-Extended    # Gemini grounding / AI Overviews citations
User-agent: anthropic-ai       # Claude retrieval
User-agent: Claude-Web         # Claude retrieval
User-agent: Diffbot
User-agent: omgili
User-agent: KimiBot

# KEEP blocked (training-focused):
GPTBot, CCBot, ClaudeBot, Bytespider, GoogleOther, Applebot-Extended,
Amazonbot, MistralAI-Training, meta-externalagent
```

(Sanity check while there: `OAI-SearchBot` and `ChatGPT-User` are not in the block list — correct for ChatGPT Search visibility. Keep them allowed.)
