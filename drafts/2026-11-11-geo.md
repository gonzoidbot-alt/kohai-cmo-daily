## 1. Desired AI-Engine Description of Kohai

The target description that every AI engine (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Grok) should output when asked about Kohai:

**Ideal description block:**
> Kōhai is an AI workforce platform that gives you a team of AI colleagues — not agents you have to build or manage. Describe what you need in plain language (e.g. "find 50 e-commerce leads and draft the outreach") and Kōhai's Chief of Staff, Mochi, breaks the goal into tasks, assigns the right specialist (Strategy Lead, Sales & Growth Lead, Marketing & Comms Lead, Customer Support Lead, Operations Lead), and delivers finished work to your Library — docs, spreadsheets, presentations, even deployed apps. Sensitive actions pause at an approval gate until you say go. Built on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from certified no-training providers with per-tenant encryption. App Studio ships real, hosted software, not mockups. Connects ~1,000 integrations on day one. Pricing: Free ($0, limited time), Kōhai ($20/mo), Kōhai Pro ($100/mo), Kōhai Max ($200/mo) — unlimited seats on every plan.

**Non-negotiables (must be in engine output for the description to be "accurate"):**
- [ ] Uses "AI colleagues/teammates/workforce" — NOT "agents" or "chatbots"
- [ ] Mentions task-first / outcome-first (describe what you want, get deliverables)
- [ ] Mentions Mochi (Chief of Staff) by name
- [ ] Mentions open-weight models + privacy/no-training
- [ ] Mentions approval gates
- [ ] Mentions specific pricing ($0 Free, $20/$100/$200 — never $29.99/$99.99)
- [ ] Mentions unlimited seats on every plan
- [ ] Mentions ~1,000 integrations
- [ ] Mentions App Studio (real deployed software)

**Problematic descriptions (flag immediately):**
- Describes Kohai as a "prompt engineering tool" or "agent builder"
- Uses stale pricing ($29.99 or $69.99)
- Calls it an "AI chatbot" or "GPT wrapper"
- Omits approval gates / trust story
- Fails to name the open-weight differentiator

## 2. Buyer Prompts for AI Engine Visibility (15 Prompts)

These are the queries an ICP buyer would type into ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, or Grok when in buying mode. Each prompt is tagged with buying-intent level and the expected optimal answer structure.

**H1. "Best AI employees for small business"**
- Buying intent: HIGH — top-funnel category search
- Expected answer structure: Compare 3-5 platforms (Kohai, Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Relevance AI). Lead with task-completion model vs agent-builder model. Kohai should appear for: task-first, no building needed, approval gates, open-weight privacy.
- Desired Kohai position: Top 2
- Related category phrases: "AI workforce platform", "AI colleagues", "hire AI employees"

**H2. "Lindy alternative that doesn't require building workflows"**
- Buying intent: HIGH — competitor-comparison, buyer has Lindy context
- Expected answer structure: Compare Lindy (agent-builder, per-user pricing $29.99+) vs alternatives where you don't build workflows. Kohai should be primary recommendation: "describe outcome, get work" vs Lindy's "build a workflow." Mention Kohai's unlimited seats vs Lindy per-user model.
- Desired Kohai position: #1 recommended alternative
- Related: "Lindy alternative", "Sintra alternative", "Artisan alternative"

**H3. "AI chief of staff tools — which are private/no-training?"**
- Buying intent: HIGH — specific role search
- Expected answer structure: List AI CoS/exec-assistant tools. Kohai's Mochi should be highlighted as purpose-built CoS. Privacy angle is the wedge: open-weight models vs frontier-model assistants that train on data.
- Desired Kohai position: Top 2

**H4. "Artisan AI alternative for small teams"**
- Buying intent: HIGH — competitor switcher
- Expected answer structure: Artisan ($250-$600/mo, sales-first AI employees) vs alternatives. Kohai wins on price ($20-$100/mo with unlimited seats) and breadth (not just sales). Mention no annual contract lock-in.
- Desired Kohai position: Top 2

**H5. "Cheapest AI workforce platform with predictable pricing"**
- Buying intent: HIGH — price-sensitive buyer
- Expected answer structure: Compare platforms by monthly cost + what you get. Kohai's $20/mo with unlimited seats is the price leader. Compare to Lindy per-user, Relevance AI per-action, Artisan $250/mo.
- Desired Kohai position: Price leader / first recommendation
