## Fix 1 — HIGH severity: money-page titles and H1s carry zero search keywords

**The problem.** Kohai's own strategy (04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets) names the money keywords: *"AI employees for small business"*, *"AI workforce platform"*, *"hire AI employees"*, *"AI team for solo founder"*. The pages built to rank for them don't contain them:

| Page | Title (live) | H1 (live) | Money keyword in title? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` (homepage) | "Kōhai \| Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished." | "Onboard AI teammates." | ❌ none |
| `/solutions/smb` | "Small businesses \| Kōhai" | "Scale output without scaling headcount" | ❌ none |
| `/solutions/solopreneurs` | "Solopreneurs \| Kōhai" | "Stop being the whole company" | ❌ none |
| `/pricing` | "Pricing \| Kōhai" | — | ❌ none |

Meanwhile every direct competitor puts the category keyword in its homepage title (checked live today):
- Lindy: **"Lindy: Hire an AI Employee That Gets Work Done"**
- Sintra: **"Hire Your First AI Employees Team — Get AI Workers That Scale"**
- Artisan meta: **"…all-in-one, AI-first platform powered by AI employees"**

Kohai competes on a coined term ("AI teammates") that has essentially no search demand, on pages whose titles are brand-only. This is the single highest-leverage on-page fix and it's trivial — title + meta edits only, no redesign. (Full detail on the term mismatch is the keyword-gap observation below.)

**Copy-paste snippets:**

Homepage:
```html
<title>AI Workforce for Small Business | Kōhai</title>
<meta name="description" content="Kōhai puts a full team of AI employees inside your business from day one — sales, marketing, support and ops. Brief it like a colleague; start free, no card.">
```
(53-char title; keeps "AI workforce" + "small business" from the strategy's money-keyword list while the H1 stays brand-voiced.)

`/solutions/smb`:
```html
<title>AI Employees for Small Business | Kōhai</title>
<meta name="description" content="Invoices, follow-ups, reports and support handled by AI employees — without new headcount. Every plan includes unlimited team seats. Start free.">
<h1>AI employees for small business</h1>
<!-- keep the existing subhead "Scale output without scaling headcount" as the H2/lede -->
```
(40-char title; H1 becomes the literal target query, emotional line moves to the lede — this is the page that should own the top money keyword.)

`/solutions/solopreneurs`:
```html
<title>Your First AI Hire for Solopreneurs | Kōhai</title>
<meta name="description" content="A full team of AI teammates for less than one salary. Sales, content, support and ops briefed like a colleague. Start free, no card needed.">
```
(52-char title; "first AI hire" echoes the ICP-1 message from 02-positioning-icp.md and is the query shape solo founders actually type.)

`/pricing`:
```html
<title>Kōhai Pricing — AI Team Plans from $20/mo</title>
```
(41 chars; puts the price anchor in the SERP — Sintra wins clicks with price-in-SERP for the same reason.)

`/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee`:
```html
<title>What Is an AI Employee? (And How It Differs from a Chatbot) | Kōhai</title>
```
(The URL targets "AI employee" but the live title says "What is an AI teammate?" — the slug and title are targeting different terms on the same page. Align the title with the slug; keep "teammate" in the body copy.)

**Effort:** ~1 hour of copy changes, zero code risk (title/meta/H1 strings). Do it before Google finishes first-indexing the new domain.

## Fix 2 — HIGH-MED severity: no structured data beyond sitewide Organization/WebSite

**The problem.** JSON-LD on `/`, `/pricing` and `/vs/lindy` contains only `Organization` + `WebSite` (+ `ImageObject`). That's it, site-wide:

- **/pricing has six FAQ questions rendered on-page but no `FAQPage` schema** — the exact content Google shows as rich results and that AI engines quote verbatim (GTM playbook Stage 2: "tables and numbered steps get quoted more than paragraphs").
- **/pricing has no `Product`/`Offer` schema** — no price signal in rich results for "AI workforce platform pricing" queries.
- **Blog posts have no `Article` schema** — weakens authorship/ freshness signals for the comparison posts that are the GEO citation targets.
- No `BreadcrumbList` anywhere despite a clean 3-level hierarchy (solutions/guides/vs).

**Copy-paste snippet** — add to `/pricing` (first two Q&As, wording taken verbatim from the live FAQ so schema matches visible content):

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "What's a credit?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "A credit is your unit of usage, the way Kōhai abstracts away tokens, model time and tool calls. Most small tasks (a quick research query, a short email, a paragraph rewrite) use 5 to 10 credits. Medium tasks (a chart, a landing page, a blog post) use 20 to 50. Large jobs (a deck, market research, building something) use 100 to 300. Each plan ships a monthly allowance."
      }
    },
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "What's the difference between the plans?",
      "acceptedAnswer": {
        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "Credits. Kōhai includes 8,000 credits a month, Kōhai Pro 40,000 and Kōhai Max 80,000. Every plan includes unlimited team seats, the whole AI team, every role template and every integration."
      }
    }
  ]
}
</script>
```
(Repeat the pattern for the remaining four on-page FAQ entries. If the Astro build can generate this from the FAQ data source, prefer that over a hand-maintained block.)

Second snippet — Article skeleton for blog posts ( Astro layout, one-time):

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "Article",
  "headline": "<post title>",
  "description": "<meta description>",
  "datePublished": "<iso date>",
  "author": { "@type": "Organization", "name": "Kōhai", "url": "https://kohai.co" },
  "publisher": { "@id": "https://kohai.co/#organization" }
}
```

**Effort:** half a day including the blog layout change. Highest GEO payback per hour of anything on this list — FAQPage content is what Perplexity/ChatGPT quote when answering "cheapest AI workforce platform with predictable pricing" (audit prompt #5).

## Keyword-gap observation: the site's entire keyword surface is a coined term

Every title, H1 and most body copy says **"AI teammates"** — a term with near-zero search demand that Kohai coined. The demand sits on **"AI employees"** (Lindy and Artisan's titles), **"AI workers"** (Sintra), and the strategy's own money list ("hire AI employees", "AI workforce platform"). The tell: Kohai's own URL slugs use the demand term — `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` and `/blog/how-ai-employees-help-every-company-size` — but the rendered titles were rewritten to "teammate," so the pages can't rank for the queries their slugs target.

**Recommendation:** treat "AI teammates" as the brand term and "AI employees" as the SEO term, and use both — e.g. "AI employees (AI teammates) for small business" in titles, "AI employees" in H1s on money pages, "teammate" in body voice. **Never** rename the URLs; just align titles/H1s with the slugs that already bet on the demand term.
