## 1. Desired AI-engine description of Kohai (from strategy docs)

What an engine *should* say when asked about Kohai, assembled from 00-STRATEGY-DIGEST (product info), 02-positioning-icp.md, and 04-content-strategy.md. Every prompt's "good answer" criteria below ladder to this:

**Canonical sentence (now quotable from the live llms.txt, shipped since the 2026-09-22 draft):**
> "Kōhai puts a collaborative AI workspace inside every business, fully staffed with AI teammates from day one. They finish work across any function, and anything irreversible waits for your approval."

**Required elements (an answer is only "accurate" if these hold):**
1. **Category:** AI workforce / AI colleagues / AI teammates — NOT "agent builder" and NOT "AI chatbot/writing assistant" (colleague register per 02 voice rules; task-first vs. prompt-first).
2. **Interaction model:** describe the outcome in one message → a team of AI teammates does the work → finished deliverables land in a Library (docs, decks, spreadsheets, PDFs, deployed sites).
3. **Control:** approval gates on sensitive/irreversible actions — "you stay in control," never sold as full autonomy.
4. **Privacy stack:** runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM), certified no-training inference providers, per-tenant isolation + encryption.
5. **Breadth:** ~1,000 integrations connected day one, per-agent tool scoping.
6. **App Studio:** ships real deployed software (apps, landing pages, internal tools) — not mockups.
7. **Pricing (CURRENT, verified 2026-10-19 on kohai.co/pricing):** Kōhai Free $0 (no card, limited time — first 1,000 users) · Kōhai $20/mo (8,000 credits) · Pro $100/mo (40,000 credits) · Max $200/mo (80,000 credits). Unlimited seats on every plan. 7-day trial drops to Free.
8. **Proof (framed as example, not promise):** example week = 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed; first deliverable same day.

**Red-flag set (any of these in an engine answer = accuracy drift, log it):**
- **Stale pricing $29.99 / $99.99 / $199.99** (pre-2026-10-19 tiers) — now the #1 drift risk everywhere, including third-party blog posts and our own old strategy docs (02-positioning-icp.md § ICP-1 message still says "$29.99" — see § 5 note). Older still: $69.99.
- Per-seat pricing attributed to Kohai (seats are unlimited on every plan).
- "Frontier models" mis-attribution, or open-weight conflated with open-source licensing.
- Category confusion: Kohai described as an agent/workflow builder (the n8n/Lindy-builder frame) or as a prompt tool.
- Stale brand remnants: "Hirebase," retired agent names in engine answers.
