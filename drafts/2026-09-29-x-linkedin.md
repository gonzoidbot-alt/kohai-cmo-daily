## LinkedIn post — PRIMARY SLOT (founder-voice workflow story, numbers-led; ICP-2 anchor; claim #5)

Your ops lead's Friday: five tabs, one spreadsheet, two hours of copy-paste before the status report even starts.

A five-person agency ended that differently last month. Their ops lead described the dashboard she kept rebuilding — pipeline by stage, blockers, owner — and asked for it as a brief instead of a task. App Studio ships deployed software, not mockups. Built, deployed, hosted the same afternoon, pulling from the tools they already used, refreshing itself every Friday at 9.

The first version wasn't perfect. She left two comments at the approval gate, and Monday's version had both fixes.

Two hours of copy-paste became ten minutes of reading. Build time: one message.

What would your team stop rebuilding by hand if the tool took one brief?
