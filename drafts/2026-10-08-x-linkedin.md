## LinkedIn Post — Mochi workflow story (claim #2)

**Target:** ICP-1 / ICP-2 · **Word count: ~150**

Monday, 8:47am. I brief Mochi — my Chief of Staff on Kohai — the way I'd brief a new hire: "Pull last week's pipeline numbers, flag the stalled deals, and have a draft follow-up ready for each one."

No workflow builder. No prompt to engineer. Mochi assembles the teammates it needs, works inside the tools we already use, and the finished follow-ups land in our Library.

Here's the part I didn't expect to matter: the approval gate. When Mochi was ready to send anything to an actual client, it stopped, showed me the exact message, and waited. One glance, one approve. I stayed in control without reading a single status update.

A week of this runs about $29.99. That's less than the coffee I bought while reviewing the deliverables.

First AI hire: $29.99/month, 12,000 credits, 7-day free trial.

What's the first task you'd hand to a colleague who never forgets a follow-up?
