## Priority Fix 1 (CRITICAL): Missing standalone alternative/keyword landing pages

**Severity:** High — directly blocks ranking for the highest-intent buyer terms

**Problem:** The strategy doc (04-content-strategy.md) identifies "hire AI employees", "AI workforce platform", "Lindy alternative", "Artisan alternative", "AI employees for small business" as target money pages. The site has a `/vs` comparison hub (16 comparisons, well-written) but:

1. **No standalone `/<competitor>-alternative` pages.** Each comparison is one level deep at `/vs/lindy` etc. — `/lindy-alternative` returns 404. Search queries like "Lindy alternative" won't match the URL or title reliably.
2. **No `/ai-employees` or `/ai-workforce` or `/ai-colleagues` page.** These return 404. These are the category-defining keyword phrases the product itself uses ("AI teammates", "AI workforce").
3. **No canonical/title optimization.** Each `/vs/*` page's `<title>` says "Kōhai vs. Lindy: which should you choose?" — this doesn't include "Lindy alternative" or "Lindy competitor".

**Fix:** Create thin-layer redirect pages or duplicate the comparison content to standalone URLs, OR update the `/vs/*` pages' `<title>` and H1 to include the "alternative" keyword. Recommended approach:

**Option A (low effort, high impact) — rewrite `/vs/*` titles:**
```
Current: "Kōhai vs. Lindy: which should you choose?"
Proposed: "Kōhai vs. Lindy: a Lindy alternative for a whole team, not one assistant"
```
This captures "Lindy alternative" in the title while keeping the existing content.

**Option B (medium effort, higher impact) — add redirects:**
Create `/lindy-alternative` as an alias to `/vs/lindy` with a 301 redirect, and similarly for `/artisan-alternative` → `/vs/artisan`, `/marblism-alternative` → `/vs/marblism`.

**Option C (highest effort, best payoff) — dedicated landing pages:**
Build `/ai-employees`, `/ai-workforce`, `/ai-colleagues` as proper GEO-friendly landing pages with:
- H2-structured Q&A ("What is an AI workforce?", "How much does an AI employee cost?")
- Comparison table (inline, not a separate page)
- Schema markup (Product, FAQPage, Comparison)
- Internal linking from /pricing and home page

**Copy-paste snippet for a `/vs/lindy` title rewrite:**

```diff
- <title>Kōhai vs. Lindy: which should you choose? | Kōhai</title>
+ <title>Kōhai vs. Lindy: a Lindy alternative for a whole team | Kōhai</title>
```

## Keyword Gap Observation

**Single biggest keyword gap: "AI chief of staff"**

Kōhai's central product differentiator is **Mochi, the Chief of Staff** — it's the avatar, the orchestrator, the face of the product. Yet:

1. **No page targets the phrase "AI chief of staff"** in its title, H1, or URL.
2. The `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` post defines "AI teammate" but doesn't specifically anchor on "Chief of Staff" as a search term.
3. No blog post titled "What is an AI chief of staff" or "AI chief of staff tools for small business."
4. The home page mentions "Chief of Staff" in body text but the H1 is "Onboard AI teammates" — not the same query.

**Value:** "AI chief of staff" is a moderate-volume, high-intent search with low competition (mostly VentureBeat/Forbes articles and Lindy landing pages). Kōhai owns the concept (Mochi) and the role is the first thing a user encounters. This is a free keyword Kōhai should dominate.

**Other notable keyword gaps:**
- **"Predictable AI pricing"** — Marblism's "hours" model and Sintra's discount-led pricing are both framed as simpler than credits. Kōhai's pricing FAQ addresses this defensively (Q&A about credits), but there's no proactive content targeting "predictable AI pricing" or "cheapest AI workforce platform" as search queries.
- **"AI employee costs" / "how much does an AI employee cost"** — Blog post "what-an-ai-job-actually-costs" exists but the URL slug doesn't match the commercial-intent query. A `/pricing/how-much-does-an-ai-employee-cost` or similar page would capture comparison shoppers.
- **"No-prompt AI" / "stop prompt engineering"** — Kohai's anti-prompt-engineering stance is central to the positioning, but no page targets these keywords directly.

**Recommended 1-week quick hit:**
Create `/ai-chief-of-staff` as a landing page with:
- Title: "AI Chief of Staff for Small Business | Kōhai"
- H1: "An AI Chief of Staff that works across your whole business"
- Content: describe Mochi's role, the meeting workflow (prep→brief→notes→follow-ups), comparison to human EA ($20/mo vs $40k+/yr)
- Internal link from home page hero and /pricing
- Schema: Product + FAQPage

This single page could rank for "AI chief of staff" within weeks — it's a low-competition, high-relevance term that maps exactly to the product's signature feature.
