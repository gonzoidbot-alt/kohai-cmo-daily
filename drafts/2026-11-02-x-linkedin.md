## LinkedIn Post — pillar: stop building agents, start briefing colleagues (claim #4)

One concrete workflow story, 120–180 words:

If you've spent a weekend in an agent builder, you know the ending: a half-finished workflow, three YouTube tutorials open, and the actual work still waiting.

The builder crowd optimizes for control over the plumbing. Most operators don't want the plumbing — they want the outcome. "Find 50 e-commerce leads and draft the outreach" is the whole job description. One message.

That's the difference between building and hiring. A colleague takes the brief, does the work, and puts the finished version in your Library. The sensitive steps — the send, the payment, the publish — wait at an approval gate until you say go. You keep the control you were building workflows to get; you just stop maintaining them.

One more thing for teams handling client data: Kōhai runs on open-weight models only — DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM — from certified no-training providers. Your work stays yours.

Kōhai Free is $0, no card, for a limited time. What would you hand your first hire?
