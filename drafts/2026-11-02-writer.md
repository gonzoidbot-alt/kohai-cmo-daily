# Sintra alternative for small business: what each subscription actually buys

Sintra sells twelve named AI helpers on a heavily discounted monthly subscription. Kohai sells a smaller team of AI colleagues who ship finished work into your Library. Both are honest products; they answer different jobs, and this post is the side-by-side a buyer needs before picking either one.

You're probably here because the entry price caught your eye — $15.60–$48.50 a month is genuinely low, and we'll say so. The question worth asking before you subscribe is not "which is cheaper" but "what does the money turn into." Here's that comparison, done fairly.

## What is Sintra?

Sintra is a subscription service giving small businesses twelve named AI helpers — Soshie for social media, Cassie for customer support, Penn for copywriting, Vizzy as an executive assistant, and so on. Each helper works through chat: you message it, it responds, it produces drafts and suggestions. The company reports more than 50,000 business owners using it and over 100 million prompts processed in 2025.

Three plans exist, all promotional on terms: $48.50/month for a 1-month plan, $23.60/month for a 3-month plan, and $15.60/month for a 12-month plan — against list prices of $97, $59, and $52. Every plan includes all twelve helpers, 250 monthly credits, 15+ integrations, and a 14-day money-back guarantee. (Pricing checked live on 2026-11-02.)

## What does Sintra do well?

Three things, and they deserve real credit:

1. **The entry price is the lowest in the category.** The discount ladder gets a full helper roster below every competitor we've measured.
2. **The setup is near zero.** Pick a helper, start chatting. There's no build phase and no learning curve to speak of.
3. **The community is large.** Fifty thousand claimed users, an active affiliate program, and visible social presence mean you can find other owners' experiences quickly.

If your need is light, occasional help — a social caption, a quick draft, a second opinion — a helper at $15–48 a month is a reasonable buy. We're not going to pretend otherwise.

## Helpers or colleagues: what's the actual difference?

The difference is what comes out the other end. A helper answers in chat; a colleague produces a finished deliverable and puts it where your work lives.

| | Sintra's helpers | Kohai's colleagues |
|---|---|---|
| Unit of work | Chat replies and drafts | Deliverables — documents, decks, spreadsheets, deployed sites |
| Where work lands | The chat log | Your Library, ready to use or forward |
| How you start a job | Pick a helper, write prompts | Describe the outcome in one message — the brief is the job description |
| Sensitive actions | Your judgment in chat | Approval gates — you review and say go before anything external sends |
| Seats | Per-subscription | Unlimited seats on every plan |
| Models | Proprietary/frontier | Open-weight only — DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM |

The middle rows matter most. "Pick a helper, write prompts" means the quality of your output still depends on your skill as a prompter, every time, for every task. Kohai's task-first model exists precisely to remove that dependency: you brief like you'd brief a hire, once, and the same request doesn't need re-engineering tomorrow.

And on the first row — chat replies end where the reply ends. A deliverable is a thing you can send, ship, or hand to a client. That distinction decides whether the subscription saves you time or just moves the typing from one window to another.

## What does each one cost?

| | Sintra | Kohai |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | $15.60–$48.50/mo (promotional; list $52–$97) | Kōhai Free — $0, no card (limited time, first 1,000 users) |
| Standard | 250 credits/month across all 12 helpers | Kōhai — $20/mo, 8,000 credits |
| Higher tiers | — | Pro $100/mo (40,000 credits), Max $200/mo (80,000 credits) |
| Credits | One pool for all helpers | Small tasks 5–10, medium (chart, landing page, blog) 20–50, large (deck, research) 100–300 |
| Failed tasks | Credit terms not published | Consume 0 credits |
| Seats | Included with subscription | Unlimited on every plan |

Two lines deserve your attention. First, **credits**: 250 across twelve helpers is thin if you're working daily — a single medium task on Kohai's meter (20–50 credits) is the kind of thing you'll do many times a week, and the $20 plan's 8,000-credit pool is sized for it. Second, **failed tasks**: Kohai's failed tasks consume 0 credits, so a task that misses costs you the retry, not the retry plus the spend. Ask any usage-priced vendor that question before you subscribe — it's the one that determines whether the cheap headline survives contact with a real month.

## Which one fits your business?

Run this check in order:

1. **What do you need produced?** If it's advice, captions, and short drafts, and volume is low, a helper roster covers it.
2. **Where should the work end up?** If you want finished deliverables — decks, spreadsheets, full documents, deployed pages — you need a colleague, not a chat.
3. **Who else touches it?** If a partner, VA, or teammate will use the same subscription, Kohai's unlimited seats on every plan removes the per-seat math entirely.
4. **What happens when a task misses?** Check the failed-task policy. Ours is zero-credit; verify anyone else's before you commit.

## The honest verdict

Sintra is the right buy for a specific job: low-volume, chat-shaped help at the lowest headline price in the category. That job is real, and they serve it well.

If the job is actual output — a content calendar that ships, an ops report that lands on schedule, a client deliverable that exists as a file rather than a chat thread — then the cheaper-looking subscription costs more per finished thing. And the ceiling is different in kind, not just degree: in Kohai, **App Studio ships deployed software, not mockups** — a working landing page or internal tool built, deployed, and hosted from a brief, which no chat helper produces at any price.

You don't have to take the comparison on faith. Start on Kōhai Free — $0, no card — brief one real task, and see what lands in your Library. If a helper at $15.60 covers your actual week, subscribe to the helper with our blessing. If the Library fills up with finished work by Friday, you've found the difference.
