## Fix 1 — Cloudflare is 403-blocking GPTBot and all Claude crawlers (severity: HIGH — site-wide, silent, blocks the GEO mission)

**Finding (verified today by user-agent test):**

| Crawler (UA tested) | Result on kohai.co/ |
|---|---|
| GPTBot (`GPTBot/1.0`) | **403** ("Your request was blocked", Cloudflare, text/plain) |
| ClaudeBot (`ClaudeBot/1.0`) | **403** |
| Claude-Web (`Claude-Web/1.0`), `anthropic-ai/1.0` | **403** |
| OAI-SearchBot / ChatGPT-User | 200 (pass) |
| PerplexityBot / Perplexity-User | 200 (pass) |
| Googlebot, meta-externalagent | 200 (pass) |

Two of the highest-value AI crawlers — the ones behind ChatGPT's training/browsing corpus and Claude's web index — cannot read any page on kohai.co. This directly undermines the stated goal of 04 (GEO presence: "AI engines describe Kohai accurately") and 06 § Stage 1 (share-of-AI-answers). It is invisible in analytics because the crawlers never appear at all — they just get a 403 and move on. Note the inconsistency: Perplexity and OAI-SearchBot get through, so Perplexity answers can cite Kohai while ChatGPT and Claude answers structurally cannot. That asymmetry also means the monthly AI-visibility audit (07 § audit step) will show a Perplexity-shaped bias nobody asked for.

**Why it matters:** 51% of B2B buyers start research with an AI chatbot (06 § core shift, G2 March 2026) — and for Kohai's AI-native ICP that share skews higher. A 403 for GPTBot/ClaudeBot means the site is absent from the two biggest answer engines' source material regardless of content quality. This is the single largest unowned leak in the funnel right now.

**Fix — allowlist the AI crawlers in the Cloudflare WAF/bot-fight rule.** The block is coming from a Cloudflare rule (server: cloudflare, plain-text 403 body), not from robots.txt (which says `Allow: /` for all). In Cloudflare → Security → WAF → custom rules (or Bot Fight Mode settings), add an Allow rule ahead of the bot rules:

```
# Cloudflare WAF custom rule expression (Security → WAF → Custom rules → Create rule)
# Name: "Allow AI crawlers (GEO)"
(cf.client.bot) and
(http.user_agent contains "GPTBot" or
 http.user_agent contains "OAI-SearchBot" or
 http.user_agent contains "ChatGPT-User" or
 http.user_agent contains "ClaudeBot" or
 http.user_agent contains "Claude-User" or
 http.user_agent contains "Claude-Web" or
 http.user_agent contains "anthropic-ai" or
 http.user_agent contains "PerplexityBot" or
 http.user_agent contains "Perplexity-User" or
 http.user_agent contains "Google-Extended")
Action: Allow (Skip → skip all remaining rules)
```

Then verify the same way this draft did: `curl -A "GPTBot/1.0" https://kohai.co/` must return 200 with the real HTML (not "Your request was blocked"). If the team prefers to keep Claude's training crawler out but let search in, the minimum acceptable set is GPTBot + OAI-SearchBot + ClaudeBot — but per 04 the goal is accurate AI-engine descriptions, which argues for allowing all of the above.

## Keyword-gap observation — "Lindy alternative" is now a vendor-farmed SERP with ~6 self-promoting listicles, and Kohai is on none of them

Today's live SERP check for "Lindy alternative / Lindy AI alternatives" returned ten results; **at least six are vendor-written listicles ranking their own product #1** (Cyndra, Viktor, Novekai Workforce, Junior, Craze, Molted), plus two aggregator pages. **Kohai appears in none of them** — including Viktor's, despite kohai.co already publishing `/vs/viktor`. Recurring angles across those listicles: "no credit math / predictable pricing," "managed, nothing to build," "chat-native." Kohai's genuine counters exist on-site (brief-like-a-colleague flow, open-weight privacy, approval gates) but no `/lindy-alternative` money page exists anywhere in the 53-URL sitemap — the gap first flagged 10-11 and re-verified 10-12 stands, and the field is getting more crowded weekly.

**Two refinements to the standing recommendation, from reading what actually ranks:**
1. **The winning page shape is a decision table + "three questions" intro** (build vs. delegate; how many tools; who maintains it) — that's what Cyndra, Viktor, and junior.so all lead with, and it's what gets quoted. Kohai's page should adopt that structure rather than a straight feature comparison.
2. **Do not compete on the "no credits" frame.** Multiple ranking pages attack Lindy specifically on credit unpredictability, and Kohai is itself credit-metered — entering that frame loses. Kohai's honest wedges per 01 § where-Kohai-wins: the team assembles itself (no builder), open-weight/no-training privacy (unique in this SERP — none of the six mention model transparency), and approval gates. One factual, generous page — "Kohai vs. Lindy: hiring a team vs. building agents," ungated, H2s as questions, decision table, FAQPage schema from 10-11 § Fix 2, sitemap entry — covers both this gap and the audit-list prompt.
