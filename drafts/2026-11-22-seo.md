# SEO Audit — 2026-11-22

## Summary

kohai.co has solid technical SEO fundamentals (canonical URLs, schema.org Organization/WebSite markup, OG/Twitter meta tags, GTM + Meta Pixel, clean Astro HTML). The core vulnerability is **on-page keyword targeting**: the site consistently avoids the high-intent search terms defined in the content strategy's SEO/GEO target list. Every page uses brand-safe "AI teammates" and "AI workspace" language but never surfaces "AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees", or competitor comparison terms where they'd be indexed by search engines.

---

## Fix #1 — CRITICAL: Rewrite homepage title and meta description to target money keywords

**Current (live):**

```html
<title>Kōhai | AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates</title>
<meta name="description" content="Kōhai is a collaborative AI workspace, fully staffed with AI
teammates from day one. Brief the work; finished deliverables land in your Inbox.">
```

**Problem:** Neither the title nor the meta description contains any of the SEO/GEO target queries from the content strategy ("AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees"). The term "AI workspace" has lower commercial intent than "AI employees" or "AI workforce platform". Competitors like Lindy and Artisan actively target these phrases — Lindy's blog runs head-to-head pricing takedowns ranking for comparison queries, and Artisan's whole positioning is "AI employee."

**Severity:** High. The homepage is the highest-authority page on the domain and does nothing to rank for the ICP's primary search terms.

**Recommended replacement:**

```html
<title>Kōhai | AI employees for small business — an AI workforce that starts today</title>
<meta name="description" content="Kōhai gives you AI employees for small business: a full
team of AI colleagues for sales, marketing, support, and ops. No prompt engineering.
Start free, no card needed. First deliverable today.">
```

**Rationale:** Puts "AI employees for small business" in the title tag (exact match for a primary GEO target query) and "AI team" / "AI colleagues" in the meta description. Preserves brand name (Kōhai) at the front. Meta description includes the free-trial CTA and promise of speed (first deliverable today) which drives CTR.

## Fix #2 — HIGH: Create dedicated comparison landing pages (not just a thin `/vs` hub)

**Current:** The `/vs` page exists (HTTP 200, title: "Compare Kōhai: Kōhai vs. the alternatives") but lists comparison topics as accordion items with one-liner descriptions. There are no individual URLs like `/vs/lindy`, `/vs/artisan`, `/vs/relevance-ai` that could each be optimized for a specific comparison query. The content is too thin to rank.

**Problem:** The SEO/GEO target list explicitly calls out "Lindy alternative" and "Artisan alternative" as money pages. The GTM playbook (06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md) says: "1 comparison or buyer guide per week — structure pages for lifting: question as H2, answer in first 2 sentences, then detail. Tables and numbered steps get quoted more than paragraphs."

Lindy publishes dedicated blog posts (e.g., "Sintra AI Pricing" on lindy.ai/blog) that rank for comparison queries — this is a proven play Kohai is not running.

**Recommended action:**

Create individual subpages at `/vs/lindy`, `/vs/artisan`, and `/vs/relevance-ai` with:

```
URL: /vs/lindy
Title: Kōhai vs Lindy: Which AI workforce platform is better for small business?
Meta description: Compare Kōhai vs Lindy: pricing, features, credit systems, and privacy.
Kōhai offers unlimited seats, open-weight models, and a $0 free tier.
H1: Kōhai vs Lindy — a side-by-side comparison
H2: What does Kōhai cost compared to Lindy?
    Table: Kōhai Free $0 vs Lindy Plus $29.99/user, Kōhai $20/mo vs Lindy Pro $99.99/user...
H2: How does the credit system compare?
H2: Which platform has better privacy?
H2: Which platform is easier to get started with?
H2: Who is Kōhai for vs who is Lindy for?
```

**Key comparison data for the table** (from competitor analysis in digest):

| Feature | Kōhai | Lindy | Artisan |

| Entry price | Free ($0) | $29.99/user/mo | Free (300 credits) |

## Keyword Gap Observation: Zero on-page content for "AI employees"

**Observation:** The site's entire public surface (homepage, pricing, features, blog, /vs) uses the language "AI teammates" and "AI workspace" exclusively. The term "AI employee" (or "employees") appears nowhere in page titles, meta descriptions, H1s, H2s, or body copy — despite being the #1 listed money page target in the SEO/GEO strategy: "AI employees for small business" and "hire AI employees."

Meanwhile:
- **Artisan (artisan.co)** centers its entire positioning on "AI employees" — their core product is literally called "AI Sales Employee" (Ava). Their blog runs essays like "The AI sales org chart."
- **Search intent:** A solo operator searching "AI employees for small business" or "hire AI employees" has high purchase intent and maps directly to Kohai's ICP-1 (the solo operator) and ICP-2 (lean team lead). The page that answers this query doesn't exist.
- The blog's "What is an AI workspace?" post is a good foundational piece but doesn't use "AI employee" language either.
- The `/solutions/smb` page (linked in the footer) is a promising URL to target with "AI employees for small business" content, but its current content was not crawled in this audit.

**Recommendation:** Create a dedicated landing page at `/solutions/smb` or `/ai-employees` structured as the "AI employees for small business" buyer guide called for in the content strategy. Use H2s as questions (per the GTM playbook: "question as H2, answer in first 2 sentences"). Include a pricing comparison table, link to `/vs/lindy` from the page for internal SEO flow, and use the phrase "AI employees" naturally in the H1 and body copy while preserving the brand's "AI teammates" terminology as a synonym in the text.
