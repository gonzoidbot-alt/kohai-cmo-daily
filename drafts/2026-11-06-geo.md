From 04-content-strategy.md § GEO must-fix, refined by the running daily drafts (most recent: 2026-11-05):

> **Kōhai** is an AI workforce platform where you describe an outcome and a team of AI colleagues assembles, does the work in your existing tools, and delivers finished results — no prompt engineering, no agent-building. It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from certified no-training providers, with per-tenant isolation and encryption.
