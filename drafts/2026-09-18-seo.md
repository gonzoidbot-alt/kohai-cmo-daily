## Fix 1 (Severity: HIGH) — Pricing-page schema/meta contradicts live prices; JSON-LD is site-generic and has no Offer data

**Evidence (crawled 2026-09-18):**
- kohai.co/pricing renders **Base $69.99 · Based $99.99 · Based Max $199.99** per seat, but the page's `<title>`/meta description/OG tags all still say: *"Kohai, Kohai Pro and Kohai Max — from $29.99 a month…"* — the old/new staging plan names and price are stale in the SERP snippet.
- The pricing page's JSON-LD is the **same generic Organization + WebSite block as every other page**. No `Product`/`Offer`/`AggregateOffer`, no `priceCurrency`/`price`, no FAQPage — while the page itself contains a full price ladder and a credit-cost explainer.
- /pricing page title is "Pricing — Kōhai"; home is "Kōhai — Onboard AI colleagues. Get work finished." — but /pricing's title as served to some fetchers ("Your Instant AI Workforce") differs across templates (blog pages also render "— Kōhai Blog" with mojibake `â` in the title/meta seen in extraction, an encoding defect in the title template).
- This matters commercially: **Lindy is a near-price-twin ($29.99/$99.99/$199.99 ladder, per 01-competitor-analysis.md)** and is already running head-to-head pricing SEO content. Buyers comparing "kohai pricing" in the SERP or asking an AI engine "what does Kohai cost" get a different number ($29.99) than the page shows ($69.99) — a trust hit at exactly the consideration stage the funnel playbook (06, Stage 2) says preference hardens.

**Copy-paste fix — pricing page `<head>`:**
```html
<title>AI Workforce Pricing — Plans from $69.99/mo | Kohai</title>
<meta name="description" content="Kohai plans: Base $69.99/mo (28,000 credits), Based $99.99/mo (40,000 credits), Based Max $199.99/mo (80,000 credits). All plans include the full AI-employee team. 7-day free trial, cancel anytime.">
```
And add a second JSON-LD block alongside the existing Organization one:
```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{"@context":"https://schema.org","@type":"Product","name":"Kohai AI Workforce","brand":{"@type":"Brand","name":"Kohai"},"description":"A team of AI colleagues — describe the outcome and finished work lands in your Library.","offers":{"@type":"AggregateOffer","priceCurrency":"USD","lowPrice":"69.99","highPrice":"199.99","offerCount":3,"offers":[{"@type":"Offer","name":"Base","price":"69.99","priceCurrency":"USD"},{"@type":"Offer","name":"Based","price":"99.99","priceCurrency":"USD"},{"@type":"Offer","name":"Based Max","price":"199.99","priceCurrency":"USD"}]}}
</script>
```
*Note: prices/names above mirror what the page currently renders — update the snippet in the same PR if the plan names/prices themselves change, so the two never drift again. Also fix the title-template encoding (the `â` mojibake on blog titles suggests a UTF-8 miss in the meta pipeline).*

## Keyword-gap observation (1 observation, not a fix yet)

**"AI employees for small business" is the money query from 04-content-strategy.md — and Kohai's best-matching asset targets the wrong head term.** The site's flagship comparison post is titled *"The Best AI **Agents** for Small Business in 2026 (Honest Comparison)"* and its own copy positions Kohai **against** agents ("Stop Building AI Agents. Hire a Team Instead", "Colleagues, Not Coworkers") — yet competitors own both sides of the semantic field while Kohai's term set is internally split: the blog competes on "AI agents for small business" (head term, dominated by Lindy/Relevance-grade content) while the category bet from 02/06 is "AI colleagues, not agents". **Gap:** nobody in the competitor set has seriously claimed the **"AI employees/colleagues for solopreneurs"** cluster — Sintra and Marblism (closest ICP overlap per 01) rank on persona/price pages, not on a definition-grade "what is an AI employee" money page; Kohai already has /blog/what-is-an-ai-employee (11 May 2026) but it's a blog post, not a conversion-oriented pillar. **Observation:** consolidating the comparison post + /blog/what-is-an-ai-employee + a new "AI employees for small business" pillar into one interlinked cluster — and pointing internal links from the homepage Solutions nav (which already lists "Solopreneurs" and "Small businesses" pages, both 200) at that cluster — would give Kohai the one cluster where the competitor set is weakest, instead of contesting "AI agents" where Lindy's domain gravity (per 01) wins. Draft a pillar-page outline before writing copy; do not rewrite the existing post's title until the cluster plan is approved.
