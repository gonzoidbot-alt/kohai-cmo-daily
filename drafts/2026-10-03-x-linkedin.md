That's the trade. Your week for your work — theirs for everything else.

## LinkedIn post — Tuesday 2026-10-06 slot (founder-voice tool-sprawl story; ICP-2 lean team lead; claim #3; 120–180 words)

Six people, nine tools, and the Monday update still landed on one desk.

A GTM lead at a small agency told me about her sweep: CRM, project tracker, two inboxes, a reporting spreadsheet nobody trusted by Wednesday. Every Monday she pulled all nine sources into a leadership update — two hours of copy-paste before the actual week began.

She briefed a Kohai Operations Lead instead: pull the numbers from the tools we already use, draft the Monday update, flag anything that looks off. Nothing got migrated. Nothing got rebuilt.

~1,000 integrations connected day one, with per-agent tool scoping — the Ops Lead touches the CRM and the tracker, not the client inboxes. She drew those lines herself.

The first Monday update was waiting in her Library, at the approval gate, before standup. She read it, fixed one number, said go.

Her tool count didn't drop. Her Monday did.

What does your Monday sweep cost you?
