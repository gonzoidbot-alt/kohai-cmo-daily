## Keyword-gap observation: the site's own vocabulary is out of register with category demand — "teammates" vs "employees"

Every direct competitor sells "**AI employees**": Artisan ("Artisans = AI employees"), Lindy ("AI employees"), Sintra ("12 AI employees"), Marblism ("AI Employees who love overtime"). Category search demand follows them — "AI employee", "AI employees for small business", "AI employee platform". Kōhai's titles, h1s and body copy say "**AI teammates**" almost exclusively, so the brand is exact-matching zero category head terms. Sharpest symptom: the explainer post lives at `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` (slug = the demand phrase) but its title and h1 read "What is an AI **teammate**?" — the URL and the title tag target different vocabularies on the same page, splitting the exact-match signal. Compounding it: `/ai-employees` and `/ai-colleagues` both still 404, so there is still no anchor page for the demand phrase internally (re-flagged from 11-02).

**Recommendation:** publish the `/ai-colleagues` (or better, `/ai-employees`) anchor as the "AI employee vs AI teammate vs chatbot" explainer with "AI employee" in the title, and use "AI teammates" as the brand differentiator *inside* the copy — win the category phrase, then differentiate on it. Do not silently swap sitewide vocabulary; make the anchor page carry the demand term.
