Every Wednesday at 2pm, my pipeline used to get an hour I never had.

Leads from the week before had gone quiet. Follow-ups were owed. The CRM told the story, but someone still had to read it and act on it.

Kohai's example week: 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed. Sales outreach accounts for 11 of those hours.

The sweep still happens every Wednesday. I'm just not the one doing it anymore.

Where does your pipeline hour actually go — the reading, or the doing?
