## P1: Missing FAQPage Structured Data on Pricing and Comparison Pages

**Severity: HIGH**

**What:** The `/pricing` page and all `/vs/*` comparison pages contain human-readable FAQ sections but lack FAQPage schema markup. Google renders FAQ rich results from this schema — it's the single highest-ROI structured data fix available.

**Evidence:**
- `/pricing` has 7+ Q&A pairs: "What's a credit?", "What's the difference between the plans?", "Is there an annual plan?", "Can I switch plans mid-cycle?", "Is there a free plan?", "I started a trial. What happens now?"
- `/vs/lindy` has 3+ Q&A pairs: "Is Kōhai a good Lindy alternative?", "Which is faster to get going?"
- Both pages return zero FAQPage schema on audit.

**Why it matters:** The strategy digest notes Lindy aggressively publishes "head-to-head pricing takedowns of competitors" for SEO traffic. FAQ rich snippets are the most effective way to claim SERP real estate for comparison queries. Missing this schema leaves money-page impressions on the table.

**Fix — Add this JSON-LD block to the `<head>` of `/pricing`:**

        "@type": "Answer",
        "text": "A credit is your unit of usage, the way Kōhai abstracts away tokens, model time and tool calls. Most small tasks use 5 to 10 credits. Medium tasks (a chart, a landing page, a blog post) use 20 to 50. Large jobs (a deck, market research, building something) use 100 to 300."

## P2: Comparison Pages Lack Cross-Linking from Content Pages

**Severity: MEDIUM**

**What:** The `/vs/` comparison pages are the primary money pages for "X alternative" search queries, but they are only linked from the footer's "Compare" index page. Blog posts and the features page do not cross-link to relevant comparison pages.

**Evidence:**
- Homepage footer: "Compare" link goes to `/vs` (a list page)
- Blog post "Stop Building AI Agents" — no link to `/vs/lindy` or `/vs/artisan` despite directly addressing the same audience
- Blog post "What Is an AI Employee" — no link to `/vs/relevance-ai` or `/vs/virtual-assistant`
- `/features` page — no links to any `/vs/` page despite being a natural hub
- Internal link count to each `/vs/*` page: likely 1 (from `/vs` index)

**Why it matters:** Google distributes PageRank through internal links. Pages with 1 inbound link are treated as orphans. Cross-linking from the 18+ blog posts and the features page would:
1. Distribute link equity to comparison pages
2. Signal topical relevance (blog about "AI agents" → `/vs/lindy` is a natural editorial link)
3. Improve user flow (reader learns about an alternative in a blog post, clicks to compare)

**Fix — Add editorial cross-links from relevant blog posts and the features page:**

| Source Page | Target | Anchor Text |
|---|---|---|
| `/blog/stop-building-ai-agents` | `/vs/lindy` | "See how Kōhai compares to Lindy" |
| `/blog/stop-building-ai-agents` | `/vs/artisan` | "See how Kōhai compares to Artisan" |
| `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` | `/vs/relevance-ai` | "Kōhai vs Relevance AI: teams vs tooling" |
| `/blog/ai-employee-vs-virtual-assistant-vs-freelancer` | `/vs/virtual-assistant` | "AI employee vs VA: the full comparison" |
| `/features` (below each role section) | `/vs/claude` | Side-by-side comparison with Claude |

## Keyword-Gap Observation

**"AI teammate" is the dominant in-house brand term (used extensively on every page) but is not targeted as a search query.**

The strategy digest positions Kohai's language around "colleagues / teammates / hires / workforce" — deliberately NOT "agents" or "prompts." The site body copy uses "AI teammate" ~20+ times across homepage, features, and pricing pages. But:

- No blog post or pillar page exists targeting "AI teammates for small business"
- No page targeting "AI teammates platform"
- No page targeting "best AI teammates" or "AI teammate software"
- Competitor analysis shows no rival is owning this exact query cluster either

**Why it matters:** This is an unguarded keyword cluster where Kohai has natural topical authority (the site uses the term heavily). As search engines move toward entity-based ranking, owning "AI teammate" as an entity is defensible long-term SEO moat. It also directly supports the "colleague" positioning pillar from the content strategy.

**Recommendation:** Create a pillar page at `/ai-teammates` targeting:
- Primary: "AI teammates for small business"
- Secondary: "AI teammates platform", "AI colleague", "virtual teammates", "AI team members"
- Content: Expand the current homepage/features "roster" section into a full pillar page describing each AI teammate role, use cases per ICP, and cross-linking to relevant comparison pages and industry solutions.

This competes for a head term no one owns yet, using site copy that already proves relevance.
