## Fix 2 — Title tags: emoji prefix + weak meta descriptions on money pages (MEDIUM-HIGH)

**Why #2:** Every page title starts with an emoji (🐴 home/pricing/blog, 🔴 on /vs pages). In a SERP the emoji consumes ~2 characters of the ~60 shown before truncation and reads as noise against the design guide's "Japanese restraint, calm competence" brand. Worse, Google rewrites titles at will and the emoji invites it. The bigger loss is on the money pages: `/vs/lindy`'s meta description is "Build your own assistant vs. a ready-made team that just works." — it never contains the search phrase the page is actually answering ("Lindy alternative"), even though the page's own H2 is "Is Kohai a good Lindy alternative?". Google lifts snippet text from meta descriptions that match the query; a description without the query phrase gets replaced with arbitrary page text.

**Copy-paste fixes (60-char budget, sentence case, no hype):**

| Page | Current title | Proposed title | Proposed meta description |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` | 🐴 Kōhai — Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished. | Kōhai — Hire AI colleagues. Get work finished. | Brief AI colleagues like a hire — describe the outcome and a team assembles, works in your tools, and delivers finished work. Free 7-day trial. |
| `/pricing` | 🐴 Pricing — Kōhai | Kohai Pricing — Plans from $19.99/month | Three plans from $19.99/month. Every plan unlocks the whole team of AI colleagues, ~1,000 integrations, and a 7-day free trial. Credits explained. |
| `/vs/lindy` | 🔴 Kōhai vs. Lindy — which should you choose? | Kohai vs. Lindy: Colleagues, Not Agents | Kohai is a Lindy alternative with no workflow building: brief AI colleagues like a hire and get finished deliverables. Compare pricing, credits, and setup. |
| `/vs` | 🔴 Compare Kōhai — Kōhai vs. the alternatives | Compare Kohai — AI Colleague Platforms Compared | Side-by-side comparisons of Kohai with Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism, Relevance AI, and human virtual assistants — pricing, privacy, and setup. |

Also drop the emoji from the title template sitewide (keep it in favicons/social images where it works). `/solutions/solopreneurs`' meta description is truncated mid-sentence ("Tell Kohai what's eating…") — rewrite to a full sentence under 160 chars.

## Keyword-gap observation (vs. competitors)

**The `/vs` hub targets "X vs. Kohai" but nothing targets the "[competitor] alternative" and "[competitor] pricing" queries — the exact playbook Lindy uses against this space.**

Per 01-competitor-analysis.md, Lindy publishes head-to-head pricing content ("Sintra AI Pricing" on lindy.ai/blog) that siphons Kohai-adjacent searches, and competitive SEO content is named there as Kohai's highest-leverage gap. Kohai's live /vs/lindy page is built for the "Kohai vs Lindy" query — that's comparison intent from people who already know Kohai. It misses the bigger pool: "Lindy alternative" (a listed money keyword in 04-content-strategy.md) and "Lindy pricing", where searchers know the competitor but have never heard of Kohai. The blog confirms the gap: all 19 posts are first-principles explainers ("What is an AI employee", "Stop building AI agents") — zero alternative/pricing-takedown posts.

**Recommendation:** one "alternatives" post per high-traffic competitor, starting with the price-twin: "Lindy alternatives for [ICP-1's job-to-be-done]" and "Lindy pricing explained (and what you get for it)" — factual, generous per pillar 5 in 04-content-strategy.md, each internally linked to its /vs page and to /pricing. Structure per 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md Stage 2: question as H2, answer in the first two sentences, pricing table (tables get quoted by AI engines). Secondary: "Sintra alternative" (Kohai's exact ICP searches it — Sintra ranks its own discount pages but nobody owns the alternative query with an honest comparison) and "Artisan alternative" (listed money keyword; Artisan's page is sales-scoped, so a factual self-serve-pricing page has an opening). These are draft-page recommendations only — nothing published.
