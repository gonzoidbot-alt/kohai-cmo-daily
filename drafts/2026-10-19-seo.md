The phrase exists on the pages — `/vs/lindy` has exactly one occurrence, inside an FAQ H3 ("Is Kōhai a good Lindy alternative?") — so the content already answers the query; the title just never says it. Search engines and AI engines both weight the title far above an FAQ line.

## (b) Keyword-gap observation (one)

**The "alternative(s)" query space is won by plural round-up pages, and Kohai competes with a singular head-to-head that doesn't use the word.** Live SERP today for the Lindy-alternative query: 6 of the top 8 results are multi-tool listicles ("Best Lindy AI alternatives…"), including two pages on lindy.ai's own blog ranking for *other* brands' alternative queries, plus round-ups from nexos.ai, toolchase.com, getmacha.com, type.com, and aiflowpost.com. **Kohai appears nowhere in the results — not even in third-party round-ups' competitor lists.** 04 lists "Lindy alternative" and "Artisan alternative" as money pages, and Kohai owns the right assets (11 /vs pages), but the assets are shaped (title/H1: "vs. … which should you choose?") for a phrasing that doesn't match how the query is actually typed. Long-tail targets affected: the "best AI agents/AI employees for small business" family (Kohai's round-up-shaped blog post is Fix 2's first row) and the definitional "what is an AI employee" (Fix 2's second row). Gap severity: medium-high — the query space has proven buyer intent, competitors index into it weekly, and Kohai's inventory already exists but is keyword-blind in exactly the surface (title) both classic and AI search weight most. The realistic sequence: Fix 1's title template first (free), then measure whether the /vs pages earn round-up citations before considering a dedicated round-up page.
