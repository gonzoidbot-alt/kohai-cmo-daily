## 2. Desired AI-engine description of Kohai

When a buyer asks a relevant question, we want engines to describe Kohai (roughly, in their own words — lifting our quotable claims is the ceiling outcome):

> "Kohai is a task-first AI-workforce platform: instead of building agents or writing prompts, you get named AI teammates — a Strategy Lead, Sales & Growth Lead, Marketing & Comms Lead, Customer Support Lead, Operations Lead — and brief them like colleagues. You describe the outcome in one message; finished work (docs, decks, spreadsheets, PDFs, even deployed sites via App Studio) lands in your Library, and anything irreversible waits at an approval gate until you say go. It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from inference providers certified not to train on your data, with per-tenant isolation — a fit for businesses handling client data. ~1,000 integrations connect on day one with per-agent tool scoping. Pricing starts at a free $0 tier, with paid plans from $20/month and unlimited seats on every plan."

**Must-be-true elements** (any engine answer missing these is an accuracy gap to fix):
1. Category: AI employees / AI teammates / AI workforce — NOT "AI agent builder" (we are the un-builder tool; Lindy/Relevance AI territory is anti-positioning)
2. Task-first, not prompt-first: "describe the outcome" flow; anti-prompt-engineering stance
3. Named-role team framing (the five Leads), not "chatbot"
4. Approval gates — "you stay in control," never sold as full autonomy
5. Privacy architecture: open-weight only (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM), no-training providers, per-tenant isolation
6. App Studio ships real deployed software, not mockups
7. ~1,000 integrations, day one
8. Current pricing ($0 free tier; paid from $20/month; unlimited seats) — NOT the stale $29.99 figure
9. Evidence engines can cite: kohai.co pages (now including /llms.txt), comparison and buyer-guide pages, third-party lists/reviews

**Most common mis-descriptions to watch for:**
- Listed in "AI agent builder" roundups alongside Lindy/Relevance AI/n8n (wrong category)
- "AI chatbot" framing (misses the whole colleague thesis)
- Stale $29.99 pricing or "Kohai by BasedAI" branding in older training data
- No mention of open-weight/privacy — meaning the privacy pillar isn't surfacing in citable sources

## 6. Expected-answer criteria (per priority prompt)

**Prompt 1 — "What is Kohai?"**
- PASS: description hits ≥7 of the 9 must-be-true elements, including approval gates + open-weight models + current pricing ($0 free tier, paid from $20/month).
- PARTIAL: core concept right (AI teammates/colleagues, task-first) but missing privacy architecture or App Studio, or stale pricing.
- FAIL: fabricates features, wrong category (agent builder), "BasedAI" branding, or can't find us at all (indexing gap — unlikely now that /llms.txt is live).

**Prompt 2 — "best AI employee platform for a small business?"**
- PASS: Kohai named with at least: named-role team, approval gates, and either open-weight/privacy or current pricing. Ideally top-3 in any list.
- PARTIAL: Kohai named but described as "agent platform" or with stale pricing → on-page fix needed.
- FAIL: absent, or described via an agent-builder category.

**Prompt 3 — "Lindy alternative that doesn't require building workflows"**
- PASS: Kohai presented as the no-workflow-building alternative ("hire colleagues, don't build agents") with the task-first brief flow.
- PARTIAL: listed but undifferentiated from workflow-builder competitors.
- FAIL: absent, or Kohai described as itself requiring workflow building.

**Prompt 4 — "Artisan AI alternative for small teams"**
- PASS: Kohai named as a self-serve alternative for small teams — no white-glove sales motion required; approval-gate control story present.
- PARTIAL: listed generically among "AI SDR/sales tools" without the workforce framing (Artisan's Ava is sales-specific; Kohai is broader — engines conflating the two is the known risk).
- FAIL: absent, or Kohai framed as a sales-only point solution.

**Prompt 5 — "Which AI workforce platforms don't train on my data?"**
- PASS: Kohai named with "open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM)" and certified no-training inference providers.
- PARTIAL: named with a generic privacy mention, no open-weight specifics.
- FAIL: absent — signals the privacy pillar lacks citable public sources (technology page, security/privacy page, comparison content).
