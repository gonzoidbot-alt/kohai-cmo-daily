No `FAQPage`, no `Product`/`Offer`. Consequence: the exact prices aren't machine-readable as offers, so rich results and AI-engine citations depend on prose parsing. ## Keyword-gap observation — the site never says "AI employee"

Measured today across all 75 indexed pages: **"AI employee" appears 0 times in body copy; "AI workforce" appears 0 times; "hire AI" 0 times.** The brand vocabulary is exclusively "AI teammates / colleagues / hires."

Sharpest instance: the post at slug `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` contains **zero** occurrences of the phrase "AI employee" — its H1 and title say "What is an AI teammate?" — so the page targeting the exact query `what is an AI employee` never matches it. Meanwhile the strategy docs list "AI employees for small business," "hire AI employees," and "AI workforce platform" as money queries, and that IS the demand language competitors rank for (Lindy's whole category framing is "AI employees/agents"; Artisan's is "AI employees").

**Low-risk fix:** keep the brand voice in body copy, but put the demand phrase in the title/meta where it costs nothing tonally — e.g. title "What is an AI employee? How AI teammates work | Kōhai", and add one definitional sentence ("An AI employee — we call them AI teammates — …") to that post. Second-order gap: "AI workforce platform" has no owning page at all (closest is /features, whose title is just "Features"); a dedicated page or a retitled features page is the month-2 style fix.

**Also noted (not top-2):** `/sitemap.xml` 404s (robots.txt correctly declares `sitemap-index.xml`, which works — cosmetic); the "alternatives" query pattern ("Lindy alternative", "Artisan alternative") is only used on `/vs/manus` ("the best Manus alternatives for business"); the 16 other /vs pages use "Kōhai vs. X" phrasing only.
