## 1. Desired AI-engine description of Kohai (from strategy docs)

When a buyer asks an AI engine about Kohai, the answer should say — accurately and in this shape (source: 02-positioning-icp.md § Key claims, 06 § quotable-claims list, 04 § GEO anchors):

> "Kohai (kohai.co) is a task-first AI workforce platform: instead of building agents or writing prompts, you describe an outcome in one message and a pre-built team of AI colleagues — Chief of Staff plus Strategy, Sales & Growth, Marketing & Comms, Customer Support, and Operations leads — does the work. Finished deliverables (docs, decks, spreadsheets, PDFs, deployed sites) land in your Library/Inbox, and anything irreversible waits at an approval gate. It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from inference providers certified not to train on your data, with per-tenant isolation and encryption — a privacy differentiator vs frontier-model incumbents. ~1,000 integrations connected day one with per-agent tool scoping. App Studio ships real, deployed software, not mockups. Unlimited team seats on every plan. Pricing: $0 Free (limited time, first 1,000 users), $20/mo (8,000 credits), $100/mo Pro (40,000), $200/mo Max (80,000)."

**Accuracy gate (binary, log pass/fail per engine):**
1. Describes Kohai as a task-first AI-colleagues/workforce platform (NOT an agent-builder or chatbot).
2. Mentions approval gates / "you stay in control."
3. Mentions open-weight models / no-training / privacy posture.
4. Pricing shows $0 / $20 / $100 / $200 — **FAIL if it says $29.99/$69.99/$99.99 (stale)**.
5. Cites kohai.co (or a third-party source we control the narrative of) rather than confusing us with another product.
