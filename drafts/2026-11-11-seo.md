# SEO Channel Draft — 2026-11-11

## Method

Extracted kohai.co homepage + pricing page via web_extract (both responded <1s). Extracted competitor homepages: lindy.ai, artisan.co, sintra.ai, marblism.com, relevanceai.com. Compared page titles, H1s, nav structure, blog presence, and pricing-page comparison tactics.

## Fix 1 — CRITICAL: Title Tag & H1 Carry Zero Target Keywords

**Current state:**

| Page | Title tag | H1 |
|---|---|---|
| kohai.co | `Kōhai \| Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.` | `Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.` |
| kohai.co/pricing | `Pricing \| Kōhai` | `One team. Free to start.` |

**Competitor titles (for comparison):**

| Competitor | Title tag |
|---|---|
| sintra.ai | `Hire Your First AI Employees Team - Get AI Workers That Scale` |
| marblism.com | `Hire AI Employees - AI Workers That Run Your Business 24/7` |
| lindy.ai | `Lindy: Hire an AI Employee That Gets Work Done` |
| artisan.co | `Boost Your Outbound Sales with an AI BDR from Artisan` |
| relevanceai.com | `Specialist AI Agents for Every Task \| Relevance AI` |

Every competitor leads with a searchable keyword phrase ("Hire AI Employees", "AI Employee", "AI BDR"). Kohai's title tag contains zero high-volume terms. The brand name "Kōhai" (with diacritic) is also a potential SEO friction point — users searching "Kohai" (without diacritic) may not match perfectly depending on search engine normalization.

**Proposed fix — update kohai.co `<title>` tag:**

```
<title>Kōhai AI Employees & Teammates | AI Workforce Platform for Small Business</title>
```

And update the H1 from `Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.` to something that includes a secondary keyword:

```
<h1>Kōhai AI Teammates. Describe outcomes. Get finished work.</h1>
```

This keeps the brand voice ("teammates") while surfacing the keyword "AI teammates" and "AI workforce platform" in the title. The meta description (currently unverifiable from extraction) should also echo target phrases from 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md § SEO targets: *"AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees"*.

**Severity:** Critical — this blocks all SERP discovery for the primary category keywords.

## Fix 2 — HIGH: No Blog / Content Hub / Resource Pages

**Current state:** The kohai.co nav (homepage extraction) shows no `/blog`, `/resources`, `/case-studies`, or `/guides` section. The only content pages visible are the homepage and pricing page.

**Competitor state:**

| Competitor | Content hub | What they host |
|---|---|---|
| sintra.ai | `/blog`, `/case-studies`, `/help` | Blog posts, customer stories, help guides |
| lindy.ai | `/blog` | SEO head-to-head pricing takedowns, feature essays |
| artisan.co | `/blog`, customer stories | Outbound sales strategies, AI insights |
| marblism.com | `/stories` | Customer case studies with real numbers |
| relevanceai.com | `/resources`, `/customers`, `/events` | Whitepapers, webinars, case studies |

**Why this matters:**
1. **Long-tail traffic** — content pages are the only path to rank for queries like "how to delegate ops to AI", "AI chief of staff tools", "Lindy alternative" (per 04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets). Without pages, those queries yield zero Kohai results.
2. **GEO citations** — AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini) extract answers from published content. No content = no citation surface.
3. **Comparison pages** — Sintra has a direct feature comparison table on its homepage pitting itself against OpenAI/Claude/Lindy. Kohai has no comparison surface at all. The GTM funnel (06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md) calls for "1 comparison or buyer guide per week" — but there's nowhere to host it.

**Proposed fix — launch a `/blog` or `/resources` section hosting:**
- Comparison pages: `/lindy-alternative`, `/artisan-alternative` (keyword-aligned slugs)
- Operator playbook posts matching long-tail queries from 04-content-strategy.md
- A pricing comparison page with a table vs Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism (mimicking Sintra's on-page comparison table)

**Severity:** High — blocks the entire content-driven SEO/GEO strategy.

## Keyword-Gap Observation: "AI Employees" is the Category Search Term — Kohai Avoids It

**Data:**

| Site | Uses "AI employees" on homepage | Uses "AI teammates/workers" |
|---|---|---|
| kohai.co | ❌ Not visible anywhere | ✅ "teammates" throughout |
| sintra.ai | ✅ "AI employees team", "AI workers" | ❌ |
| marblism.com | ✅ "AI employees", "AI workers" | ❌ |
| lindy.ai | ✅ "AI employee" | ✅ "teammate" |
| artisan.co | ✅ "AI employee" (Ava the AI BDR as employee concept) | ❌ |
| relevanceai.com | ❌ ("AI agents") | ❌ |

**Observation:** "AI employees" is the dominant category keyword — all three direct competitors (Sintra, Marblism, Lindy) use it in their title tags and homepage copy. It's almost certainly the highest-volume search term in this category. Kohai's deliberate choice to use "teammates" instead of "employees" is a defensible brand move (less transactional, more collaborative), but there's no on-page bridge between the two vocabularies.

**Gap:** A user searching "hire AI employees" lands on Sintra or Marblism — Kohai doesn't appear because the phrase doesn't exist on the site. Similarly, "AI workers" (Sintra, Marblism) yields zero Kohai results.

**Recommendation:** Add a single sentence on the homepage and pricing page that bridges the vocabulary, e.g.: *"Kōhai gives you AI employees — teammates, not agents — that finish real work across every function."* This surfaces the keyword without compromising "teammates" as the primary brand noun. A dedicated `/ai-employees` page (or section in the pricing page FAQ) would capture the search traffic directly and serve as an SEO landing page.

**Additional observation:** The "Kōhai Free — $0, no card, for a limited time" offer (first 1,000 users) is a powerful SEO hook if it appears in snippet text. Currently it's only on the pricing page — adding it to the homepage metadata or FAQ section would improve featured-snippet eligibility for queries like "free AI employees" or "AI employee free plan".
