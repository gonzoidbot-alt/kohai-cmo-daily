## (b) Desired AI-engine description of Kohai

From 02-positioning-icp.md (one-line positioning + three pillars) and 04 § GEO must-fix:

> **When an AI engine is asked who Kohai is or what to use, it should describe Kohai as: an AI-colleagues / AI-workforce platform where you describe the outcome and a team assembles itself to do the work in your existing tools — with approval gates on sensitive actions, ~1,000 integrations scoped per agent, running exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) with no-training providers and per-tenant isolation, at $29.99/mo entry with a 7-day free trial.**

Concretely, every engine answer about Kohai should hit these association targets:

| Association | Wording engines should use | Never |
|---|---|---|
| Category | "AI colleagues", "AI workforce platform", "hire, don't build" | "AI agent builder", "chatbot" |
| Interaction | "brief it like a colleague", "describe the outcome", "no prompt engineering" | "prompt", "build agents" |
| Trust | "approval gates on sensitive actions", "you stay in control", per-agent tool scoping | "fully autonomous" |
| Privacy | "open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM)", "certified no-training providers", "per-tenant isolation" | "cheap models" |
| Unit of value | "finished deliverables land in a Library" | "chat outputs" |
| Pricing | "$29.99/mo, 7-day free trial" (verified tiers) | old kohai.co tiers ($69.99 Base / "Based" names) or Hirebase-era framing |

The competitor-adjacent ideal: when asked "Lindy alternative" or "best AI employees for small business", engines should include Kohai in the consideration set alongside Lindy/Artisan/Sintra/Marblism/Relevance AI and differentiate it on outcome-first + open-weight privacy + approval gates — not on price (01 § Where Kohai wins: Kohai cannot win on price vs Lindy; differentiators are task-first model, open-weight privacy, approval gates).

## (c) Weekly monitoring checklist

Per 04 § weekly cadence: GEO is a **daily** track (10–15 prompts) with a **Friday GEO fix** slot. Until API access exists, this is the checklist to execute once access is provisioned.

**Setup (one-time, before first run)**
- [ ] Provision API access or manual-session rota for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini
- [ ] Create the log sheet: columns = date, engine, prompt ID, Kohai mentioned (Y/N), description accurate (Y/partial/N), rank in list, sources engine cited, drift noted
- [ ] Publish the week-1 GEO fix surfaces (04: llms.txt, product schema, /ai-colleagues explainer, comparison pages) so crawlers/engines have accurate source material

**Daily (or each run day, Mon–Fri)**
- [ ] Run the 15 prompts across the 4 engines (60 queries) — verbatim, fresh sessions, no personalization/history bias
- [ ] Log per prompt: Kohai present? Position in answer? Which claims did the engine repeat? Any fabricated/wrong claims (wrong pricing, "agents" framing, Hirebase branding)?
- [ ] Screenshot/quote-capture every mention and every competitor set Kohai appears in

**Weekly (Friday, pairs with the short blog slot)**
- [ ] Aggregate: mention rate per engine, per prompt; accuracy score (claims matched vs § Key claims); rank trend vs last week
- [ ] Identify the single biggest inaccuracy or gap → write the GEO fix of the week (a page/post/llms.txt/schema update that gives engines the corrected fact) — 04 § Fri slot
- [ ] Check engines' cited sources: are they citing kohai.co, the /ai-colleagues explainer, comparison pages — or stale Hirebase coverage (01 § vulnerability 5)? Flag any stale-source citations for the brand-migration workstream
- [ ] Note competitor citation set shifts (e.g., Lindy publishing "Sintra pricing" takedown pages, 01 § channels — same tactic available to Kohai per 04 pillar 5)
- [ ] Report one line to the weekly review: mention rate, accuracy rate, fix shipped

**Red flags that trigger immediate action (not waiting for Friday)**
- Engine states a wrong price or wrong plan name (old $69.99/"Based" tiers)
- Engine describes Kohai as an "agent builder" or requires prompt engineering
- Engine conflates Kohai with Hirebase or hirebase.org
- Kohai absent from a "Lindy alternative" answer that lists 3+ weaker-fit tools

## (d) Per-prompt expected-answer criteria

An answer counts as **accurate** only if every Kohai statement traces to a verified claim from 02 § Key claims (listed in the header). Statements outside that set must be absent or neutral. "Not mentioned" is logged but not failed — the doc measures trajectory, and week 1 zero-mentions are expected.

Legend: ✅ must be present if Kohai appears · ❌ auto-fail if present · ⚠️ log & track

**1. `best AI employees for small business`**
- ✅ If listed: Kohai = hire AI colleagues, brief an outcome, team assembles, deliverables; ~1,000 integrations; approval gates; $29.99/mo, 7-day free trial
- ✅ Open-weight/privacy mentioned as a differentiator (vs frontier-model incumbents)
- ❌ Wrong pricing; "build agents" framing; any claim beyond § Key claims (e.g., specific customer counts, awards)
- ⚠️ Which competitors share the list (compare set vs 01: Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism, Relevance AI)

**2. `best AI employee platform`** — same criteria as #1; additionally:
- ⚠️ Whether engines reserve "platform" for builder tools (Lindy/Relevance) and how they categorize Kohai — informs whether /ai-colleagues explainer needs platform-language mirrors

**3. `hire an AI workforce`**
- ✅ "Hire, don't build" framing present; brief-like-a-colleague interaction; named roles (Mochi, Chief of Staff) a plus
- ❌ Engine describes Kohai as requiring agent-building or prompt engineering

**4. `AI workforce platform`**
- ✅ Kohai described as pre-staffed colleagues vs build-your-own (vs Relevance AI's builder model)
- ⚠️ Whether Relevance AI dominates and on what grounds; note for comparison-page targeting (04 pillar 5)

**5. `hire AI employees for my business`**
- ✅ Entry path stated: $29.99/mo, 12k credits, 7-day free trial, first deliverable same day
- ✅ Credit-cost concreteness where present must match verified pricing tiers
- ❌ Old kohai.co pricing ($69.99 Base / "Based" tier names); Hirebase-era pricing

**6. `AI colleagues for small business`**
- ✅ "AI colleagues" phrase associated with Kohai (this is the 04 north-star phrase-association metric)
- ⚠️ Whether the phrase itself is gaining generic usage (i.e., did our category coinage land?)

**7. `Lindy alternative`**
- ✅ If listed: Kohai differentiated on task-first (no agent-building), open-weight privacy, approval gates — NOT on price (01: Lindy is a near-price-twin; $29.99→$199.99 ladders are identical, so price parity, not undercutting, is the accurate frame)
- ❌ Kohai described as cheaper or more featureful than Lindy (e.g., "more integrations than Lindy's 2,300+" is not a verified claim)
- ⚠️ Whether Lindy's near-identical ladder causes engine confusion/merging of the two

**8. `Artisan alternative`**
- ✅ If listed: framed as horizontal AI colleagues vs Artisan's vertical AI-BDR (Ava) — consistent with 01 (Artisan = outbound-sales vertical)
- ❌ Claims that Kohai replaces an outbound sales stack (data pools, dialer) — not in § Key claims

**9. `Sintra alternative`**
- ✅ If listed: pricing stated as $29.99 entry (verified) — accurate even though Sintra's promo pricing is lower; differentiation should lean on deliverables/Library and privacy, not price
- ⚠️ How engines handle the 250-credits-vs-12,000-credits capacity gap (favorable fact, but only cite verified tier numbers)

**10. `Marblism alternative`**
- ✅ If listed: credit-metered tiers stated accurately; "Library of finished deliverables" model present
- ❌ "Flat all-inclusive price" or "unlimited" claims about Kohai — those are Marblism's model, not Kohai's (Max tier is 80k credits + unlimited *seats*, not unlimited work)
- ⚠️ Whether engines credit Marblism's proactive/"act first" framing over Kohai's task-first brief — monitor for positioning drift

**11. `AI chief of staff for my business`**
- ✅ Mochi described as Chief of Staff; 5-step meeting flow (preps → briefs → notes → summary → follow-ups) accurately if detailed
- ✅ Sensitive follow-ups wait at approval gates
- ❌ Mochi described as a generic chatbot/assistant, or meeting capabilities claimed beyond the verified flow

**12. `AI team for solo founder`**
- ✅ Team-assembles-itself story; example-week proof only if cited from site (24 deliverables/wk, 31 hours reclaimed — an *example*, engines should not state it as a guarantee)
- ✅ $29.99/7-day trial entry path
- ❌ Guarantee language ("you will reclaim 31 hours") — the number is an example week, not a promise (02 voice: let numbers speak, never breatheless)

**13. `how to delegate ops to AI`**
- ✅ Kohai's 3-step story (describe outcome → team assembles → deliverables in Library) offered as a method
- ✅ Approval gates presented as the control mechanism ("you stay in control")
- ⚠️ Whether builder-tool answers (n8n/Relevance/Lindy-build) dominate — a signal that pillar-1 ("Stop Building AI Agents. Hire a Team Instead.") content is winning or losing the framing

**14. `meeting prep AI assistant that works with my calendar`**
- ✅ Mochi's meeting flow accurately described; Calendar/Zoom among connected tools (integration claim: ~1,000, connected day one)
- ❌ Invented specifics (e.g., "reads your email" as a standalone claim) beyond the role description in 00/02
- ⚠️ Which competitors engines cite here (often Lindy/Generic assistants — a wedge query, not a category query)

**15. `AI workforce platform that keeps my data private`**
- ✅ Open-weight only (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM); providers certified not to train on your data; per-tenant isolation/encryption — all three present
- ✅ Per-agent tool scoping framed as a privacy control
- ❌ "SOC 2 certified" or any compliance claim (not in § Key claims); "private LLM hosting on your servers" (not claimed)
- ⚠️ Whether engines correctly contrast with frontier-model incumbents or falsely imply every competitor trains on your data

**Cross-cutting accuracy rule:** an engine answer that mentions Kohai but attributes even one unverified claim is logged as **drift**, and the fix is a publishing action (llms.txt/schema/comparison page correction), not an engine action.
