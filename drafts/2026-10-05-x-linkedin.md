## LinkedIn post — Monday pillar slot (control extends to your data: the ICP-3 objection story; claim #4; 120–180 words)

The objection arrived before the demo did: will our clients' data train your models?

She runs a bookkeeping practice — ledgers, payroll, bank statements. One bad data story ends the sale, so she had turned down every AI tool that came through.

What moved her wasn't a promise. It was the stack: open-weight models only — DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM — from certified no-training providers. The providers agreed in writing not to train on her files. Per-tenant isolation keeps each client's work separated and encrypted.

Then the control she liked even more: each colleague sees only the tools she scopes to it, and anything with outside impact waits at an approval gate. The payroll summary email waits for her read before it sends. The ledger reconciliation never did.

Control isn't one feature. It's access, data, and the gate.

What's the dealbreaker that has kept your practice out of AI so far?
