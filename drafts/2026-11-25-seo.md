## Fix 1 (CRITICAL) — The /use-cases hub is orphaned: money-keyword pages exist but nothing on the site links to them

**Severity:** HIGH — these are exactly the long-tail money pages 04-content-strategy.md names ("AI chief of staff", "meeting prep AI assistant", "how to delegate ops to AI"), fully built and optimized, but unreachable by any human or crawler following links.

**What's wrong (verified):**
- `sitemap-0.xml` lists 10 `/use-cases/*` pages, including `/use-cases/ai-chief-of-staff`, `/use-cases/ai-market-research`, `/use-cases/ai-proposal-writer`, `/use-cases/ai-report-generator`, `/use-cases/ai-sales-funnel`.
- There IS an index page (`/use-cases`, title: "What can AI teammates do? Use cases by job | Kōhai") that links to all 9 children.
- But **zero** pages link to `/use-cases` or any child: homepage, /features, /vs, /solutions/marketing, /blog posts, /guides, /help — all show 0 `href="/use-cases/"` matches.
- `llms.txt` does not mention /use-cases either (0 matches), so AI engines reading the curated llms.txt won't discover them; they only exist in the XML sitemap.

**Why it matters:** `/use-cases/ai-chief-of-staff` is the best-built page on the site for this term — exact-match H1, keyword title, 6-question FAQPage schema, SoftwareApplication markup — and it points into a void. An orphan page in a new domain rarely gets crawled, let alone ranked. This is the highest-ROI fix on the site right now because the asset already exists.

**Copy-paste fixes:**

1. Footer "Product" column (site-wide partial) — add one line:
```html
<li><a href="https://kohai.co/use-cases">Use cases</a></li>
```

2. llms.txt — add a section (GEO coverage; AI engines weight llms.txt lists heavily):
```markdown
## Use cases

- [AI Chief of Staff](https://kohai.co/use-cases/ai-chief-of-staff): Your inbox, calendar and meetings handled every day; anything sensitive waits for your approval.
- [AI market research](https://kohai.co/use-cases/ai-market-research): Competitor and market research with every finding cited.
- [AI proposal writer](https://kohai.co/use-cases/ai-proposal-writer): Proposals drafted from your notes and past wins, ready for your review.
- [AI report generator](https://kohai.co/use-cases/ai-report-generator): Weekly status and metrics reports assembled from your tools.
- [AI sales funnel](https://kohai.co/use-cases/ai-sales-funnel): Prospect research, outreach drafts and follow-ups, staged for your approval.
```
(Plus the remaining four children: ai-competitor-analysis, ai-newsletter-generator, ai-social-media-posts, ai-spreadsheet-generator.)

3. Contextual links (best anchors): the /features page's role sections and the relevant blog posts should each link one use-case page naturally — e.g. the Chief of Staff section on /features → `/use-cases/ai-chief-of-staff` with anchor "what an AI Chief of Staff does".

## Keyword-Gap Observation

**"AI chief of staff" is a live, winnable SERP — and Kohai's best-fit page is invisible in it.**

The query "AI chief of staff" (named explicitly in 04-content-strategy.md as a long-tail target) returns a field of niche tools: Aura (moreaura.ai), Gina (talktogina.ai), Team0, Sabi (USA Today press release), Magnus, The AI Orchestrators — plus a third-party roundup, "The best AI chief of staff tools in 2026" (trydock.ai), which lists Dock, Carly, Alfred, Tana and Reclaim. **None of the named competitors in 01-competitor-analysis.md (Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism, Relevance AI) appear** — this SERP is not owned by the big players, which makes it one of the few category queries a new domain can realistically take.

Kohai has the strongest product-to-query fit of anyone on that SERP: a Chief of Staff is literally the first role in every workspace, with inbox triage, meeting prep, notes and follow-ups — precisely what Aura/Gina/Team0 sell. But the page (`/use-cases/ai-chief-of-staff`) has no internal links (Fix 1) and Kohai is absent from every third-party roundup that currently ranks.

**Recommendation (two moves):**
1. Ship Fix 1 so the page is reachable — without links it cannot compete regardless of quality.
2. Roundup placement: the SERP rewards listicles. Draft a polite note to roundup publishers (trydock.ai is the live example) with the quotable claim they'd need — "every Kōhai workspace ships with a Chief of Staff, ready at signup" — plus the approval-gate angle no aide-tool on that list has. Note: 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md Stage 3 already covers outreach mechanics; this is a content-placement target, not a paid placement.

Also worth tracking: related SERPs ("AI executive assistant", "AI chief of staff for founders") are dominated by the same niche set, so one well-linked page + one roundup mention compounds across all three.
