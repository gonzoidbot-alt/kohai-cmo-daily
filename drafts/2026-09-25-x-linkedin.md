## LinkedIn post — operator playbook: support triage (founder-voice workflow story, ICP-2, 120–180 words, ends with the pricing line)

A five-person agency's support inbox used to eat every afternoon. Whoever had the least billable work that day got triage duty, and replies went out whenever someone surfaced for air.

Now their Operations Lead on Kohai runs it. One brief: every morning, triage the support inbox, sort by urgency, draft replies in the team's voice. Anything sensitive — refunds, account changes — waits at an approval gate. The team reviews the gate once, mid-morning, in minutes.

The queue that took afternoons now takes one review block. The drafts are in the Library before the team's first call; they edit and send. Support stopped being the thing the schedule bent around.

Pro tier: $99.99/mo for 40,000 credits and 3 seats.
