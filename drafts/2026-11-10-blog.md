**Target query:** automated weekly growth report for solo founders

You run a business. Monday morning rolls around. You need to know where things stand before you decide what to do with the rest of the week.

So you open Stripe for revenue. Google Analytics for traffic. Your CRM for pipeline. Your email provider for deliverability. Your ad platform for spend and ROAS.

You export, copy, paste. You paste again. You format the numbers into a slide or a doc. Around hour two you realize one of the sources has a different date range, so everything is off. You start over.

This is why most solo operators don't have a growth report at all. Not because the data doesn't exist — but because building it costs every Monday morning of your life.

## You're describing a job, not a tool problem

The problem isn't that you need a better dashboard tool. You can already see the numbers inside each platform. The problem is that *assembling* them into a coherent picture takes human time — the same time you could spend acting on what the numbers tell you.

The economics of this are straightforward. A solo founder billing at $150/hour spends 3–4 hours every Monday assembling a growth report. That's $600–$800 per week, or roughly $30,000 per year, just to know where the business stands. Not to fix anything. Not to grow anything. Just to *see the current state*.

This is the gap that most AI tools miss. They give you *another* place to look at data — another dashboard, another notification, another agent you need to prompt. What you actually need is the finished report, delivered, every Monday, before you sit down.

## What a growth report looks like when it's not your second job

A useful Monday report for a solo operator covers six things:

1. **Revenue last week** — total, week-over-week, month-to-date comparison
2. **New customers** — count, average order value, source breakdown
3. **Pipeline velocity** — new leads, proposals sent, deals at each stage
4. **Traffic summary** — sessions, top sources, trend vs last week
5. **Ad spend + ROAS** — spend by channel, return by channel, any underperformers
6. **Churn / at-risk accounts** — cancellations, downgrades, accounts with declining usage

That's about 50 data points pulled from six platforms. By hand, that takes hours because every platform organizes its data differently and the report needs a consistent format.

Your Operations Lead in Kōhai already connects to your ~1,000 integrated tools with per-agent scoping. It reads Stripe for revenue, Google Analytics for traffic, your CRM for pipeline, your ad platforms for spend. It doesn't need new logins or API keys. It just works against the tools you already use.

## The output is what you asked for — not a new thing to manage

When the report lands in your Library, it's a finished document. One page. Clean formatting. Numbers stand out. Week-over-week deltas are calculated. Anything that moved significantly is flagged.

You don't get a notification that says "your report is ready, click here to view." You don't get an internal link to a dashboard you have to open. You get the report — the same way an analyst would drop it on your desk.

Your Marketing & Comms Lead or Operations Lead picks it up and comes back with options. You approve or adjust. That's it.

That's the difference between a tool and a colleague. A tool waits for you to use it. A colleague owns the outcome.
