## Priority Fix 2 (HIGH): Strengthen Homepage H1 and Title Tag for High-Intent Keywords

**Severity:** High — the homepage is the highest-value page and the current title/H1 miss the keywords the ICP actually searches for.

**Current title tag:** `Kōhai | Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.`
**Current H1:** `Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.`

**Problem:** The market searches for "AI employees," "AI workforce," "AI team for small business" — not "AI teammates." The strategy digest lists these exact phrases as money-page targets. The current H1 is brand-voice-correct but SEO-weak.

**Suggestion for title tag:**
```
Kōhai: AI Employees & Workforce Platform for Small Business | Onboard AI Teammates
```

**Suggestion for H1 (keep brand voice but add keyword anchor):**
```
AI employees your small business can afford — starting at $20/mo, unlimited seats
```

This still uses the "colleague" register (plain, second-person) while front-loading the high-volume keyword "AI employees" and the price anchor that beats competitors.

## Keyword Gap Observation

**Gap: "AI employees" keyword phrase is absent from the site's visible content.**

The site uses "AI teammates" throughout, but every major competitor positions around "AI employees" (Lindy: "AI employees / a teammate", Artisan: "Artisans = AI employees", Sintra: "12 AI employees", Marblism: "AI Employees"). The strategy digest lists "hire AI employees" as a money-page target (page 7 of digest), yet the word "employee" appears zero times in the homepage hero, features page, or pricing page copy.

**On-page evidence:** "employees" only appears in competitor content on /vs/* comparison pages. The main site copy uses "teammates," "colleagues," "leads," and "Chief of Staff" — all brand-correct — but the high-volume search terms go unaddressed.

**Recommendation:** Add a sentence to the homepage hero and /features page that bridges the language gap, e.g.:
> "Your AI employees — Kōhai calls them teammates — handle sales, support, content, and ops. No building required."

This preserves the brand's "colleague" register while making the page rank for the keywords buyers actually use. The same bridge phrasing on the /pricing page would capture "AI employees pricing" queries.
