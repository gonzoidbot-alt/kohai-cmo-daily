## Fix #1 — HIGH: Homepage title tag and meta description carry zero target keywords

The current title ("Kōhai — Onboard AI colleagues. Get work finished.") is good brand voice but targets only "AI colleagues" — and the macron spelling (Kōhai) doesn't token-match the plain "kohai" queries people actually type. The meta description has no keyword, no proof, no CTA. "AI employees", "hire AI", and "small business" appear 0–2 times in home page copy; the money-page terms from 04-content-strategy.md are mostly absent from the page that has the most authority.

**Copy-paste title tag (61 chars):**
```html
<title>Kohai — Hire AI Employees for Small Business | AI Colleagues</title>
```

**Copy-paste meta description (146 chars):**
```html
<meta name="description" content="Hire AI colleagues that use your tools and deliver finished work. 1,000+ integrations, open-weight models, 7-day free trial. From $29.99/mo.">
```

**H1 — minimal verb swap, keeps the register (39 chars):**
```html
<h1>Hire AI colleagues. Get work finished.</h1>
```
(Alternately keep the current H1 and put "hire" into the subhead — but the H1 is the single highest-weight on-page element and "Onboard" is a weaker match for "hire AI employees".)

Also: pick **one** brand spelling for titles. Recommend plain "Kohai" in `<title>`/OG and keeping "Kōhai" for visual brand in page content.

## Keyword-gap observation: "Lindy alternative" and pricing-comparison queries

Kohai has a genuine `/vs/lindy` page (1,898 words, pricing covered) — that's further along than expected. The gap is **query phrasing**: nobody searching "Lindy alternative" or "Lindy pricing" is served by the current title/desc ("Kōhai vs. Lindy — which should you choose?" / "Build your own assistant vs. a ready-made team that just works."), which target branded "vs" queries only. Meanwhile Lindy's SEO engine already ranks head-to-head *pricing* pages (its "Sintra AI Pricing" post) — that exact angle is unclaimed on Kohai's side. Same pattern for "hire AI employees": the phrase appears 0 times on the homepage and no page is titled for it.

**Suggested title for /vs/lindy (63 chars):**
```html
<title>Kohai vs. Lindy: Pricing, Credits & Which Fits Your Team</title>
```
And one blog post this window targeting the phrasing directly (per pillar 5 in 04 — factual and generous): "Looking for a Lindy alternative? Compare pricing, credits, and how the work gets done" — let the $29.99/12k vs 3k-credits math and the open-weight privacy story carry it, with Lindy described fairly.
