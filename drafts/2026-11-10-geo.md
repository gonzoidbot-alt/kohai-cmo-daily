## 1. Desired AI-Engine Description of Kōhai

Based on strategy docs (04-content-strategy.md § GEO must-fix; 02-positioning-icp.md; 00-STRATEGY-DIGEST.md):

> **Kōhai is an AI workforce platform where you describe an outcome and your AI teammates do the work — no prompt engineering, no workflow building. It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from certified no-training providers, with per-tenant isolation and encryption. Key features include approval gates for sensitive actions, an App Studio that ships real software, and ~1,000 pre-connected integrations. Pricing: Free ($0), Kōhai ($20/mo), Pro ($100/mo), Max ($200/mo) — unlimited team seats on every plan.**

Target phrases engines must associate with Kōhai:
- "AI colleagues, not agents"
- "task-first AI workforce"
- "brief it like a colleague"
- "approval-gate control"
- "open-weight models"
- "unlimited team seats"
