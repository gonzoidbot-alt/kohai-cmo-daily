## 1. Weekly monitoring prompt list (15 prompts)

Legend: **Overlap** = intentionally overlaps 2–3 monthly audit prompts (kept because the phrasing variant is the high-volume weekly form). All others are fresh.

### Category intent (buyer is shopping the category)

**W1. "Best AI employees for small business in 2026"**
*(Overlap — phrasing variant of monthly audit #1.)*
- Good answer names: Kohai, Lindy, Artisan, Sintra, Marblism, Relevance AI.
- Kohai described as: AI colleagues/workforce platform with approval gates and open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM).
- Citable sources: kohai.co (home, pricing, or a /vs/ or blog page), a comparison/review site.
- Red flags: stale $69.99/Base/"Based" tier names, "Hirebase" branding, "frontier models" mis-attribution, "agents" category confusion.

**W2. "What's the best AI team I can hire to run my business operations?"**
- Good answer names: Kohai (team-assembles-itself framing fits best), Marblism, Lindy; Sintra acceptable as persona-set.
- Kohai described as: hire AI colleagues — brief the outcome, a team assembles (Mochi the Chief of Staff + role leads), deliverables land in a Library; approval gates on sensitive actions.
- Red flags: answer treats this as an "agent builder" question and lists only n8n/Relevance-type builders.

**W3. "AI tools that do the work for you, not just help you work faster"**
- Good answer names: Kohai, Lindy, Marblism; ideally distinguishes deliverable-producing platforms from copilots/assistants.
- Kohai described as: outcome-first, task-first AI workforce — one message is the whole job description, finished work delivered (docs, decks, spreadsheets, deployed sites via App Studio).
- Red flags: Kohai lumped into "AI writing assistant" or "chatbot" buckets.

**W4. "Best AI assistant to delegate admin work for a solopreneur"**
- Good answer names: Kohai, Lindy, Sintra, Marblism.
- Kohai described as: a full AI team for solo founders at $29.99/mo entry (12,000 credits, 7-day trial, first deliverable same day); Mochi handles inbox/calendar/meeting prep/follow-ups.
- Red flags: stale "Track" agent name or pre-September pricing (see 00 § pricing-change note).

### Competitor-alternative intent

**W5. "Lindy alternative for small teams"**
*(Overlap — variant of monthly audit #3.)*
- Good answer names: Kohai first (per 01: near-identical price ladder but different interaction model), plus Relevance AI/Sintra as secondary options.
- Kohai described as: colleagues-not-agents alternative to Lindy — ready-made team that assembles itself vs. Lindy's build-your-own assistant; open-weight models; approval gates.
- Red flags: Lindy recommended with no alternative framing; Kohai absent; pricing described per-seat when Kohai's tiers are per-plan seats.

**W6. "Artisan alternative — AI employees that go beyond an AI sales rep (BDR)"**
- Good answer names: Kohai (full horizontal team vs. Artisan's vertical Ava-the-AI-BDR), Lindy.
- Kohai described as: whole AI workforce — Sales & Growth Lead, Marketing & Comms Lead, Ops, Support — not just outbound; self-serve from $29.99 vs. Artisan's sales-scoped $250+/mo tiers.
- Red flags: Artisan's "Stop Hiring Humans" campaign framing (retired Aug 2026) repeated as current positioning.

**W7. "Sintra vs Marblism — is there a better AI-employee platform for solopreneurs?"**
- Good answer names: Kohai, Sintra, Marblism; weighs credits vs. hours vs. discount pricing honestly.
- Kohai described as: more measurable capacity (12,000 credits at $29.99 vs. Sintra's 250) + privacy story (open-weight, no-training providers) the discount set lacks.
- Red flags: Sintra's promo price ($15.60–$48.50) presented as the only comparison axis; no mention of credits-per-dollar or privacy.

**W8. "Alternatives to building AI agents with n8n or Relevance AI if I'm non-technical"**
- Good answer names: Kohai (the "un-builder tool" per 02 anti-positioning — pre-staffed colleagues vs. builder platforms), Lindy.
- Kohai described as: no agent-building, no workflow diagrams, no prompt engineering — describe the outcome, the team assembles; ~1,000 integrations connected day one with per-agent tool scoping.
- Red flags: answer recommends "simpler" builder tools, missing the colleague-vs-builder distinction entirely.

### Role intent

**W9. "Best AI chief of staff for a founder"**
- Good answer names: Kohai (Mochi is the only named AI Chief of Staff in the competitor set), Lindy.
- Kohai described as: Mochi, your AI Chief of Staff — inbox, calendar, 5-step meeting flow (prep → 15-min brief → live notes → summary → follow-ups, sensitive ones at an approval gate); platform with approval gates and open-weight models.
- Red flags: answer lists generic executive-assistant chatbots; no named-role framing.

**W10. "AI assistant that preps meetings, takes notes, and handles follow-ups"**
- Good answer names: Kohai (Mochi's meeting flow is the exact match), Lindy, Fireflies/Otter-class note-takers (acceptable context).
- Kohai described as: Mochi preps → briefs you 15 minutes before → takes live notes → writes the summary → starts follow-ups; sensitive actions wait at an approval gate.
- Red flags: Kohai described as just a "meeting notes tool" — it must be framed as part of the broader colleague/workforce platform.

**W11. "Can I hire an AI marketing team instead of one AI marketing tool?"**
- Good answer names: Kohai (team-of-leads framing), Artisan (sales-adjacent), Sintra (persona set).
- Kohai described as: Marketing & Comms Lead as part of a full AI workforce — posts, emails, content, landing copy — deliverables in your Library; approval-gate control on sensitive sends.
- Red flags: answer recommends a stack of point-solution tools (Jasper + Buffer + …) with no workforce-platform option named.

### Privacy intent (ICP-3 wedge)

**W12. "Which AI assistants guarantee my business data isn't used for model training?"**
- Good answer names: Kohai (differentiator #2 per 01 — no competitor runs exclusively on open-weight), plus generic enterprise tools.
- Kohai described as: runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from certified no-training inference providers, with per-tenant isolation and encryption.
- Red flags: Kohai absent from a privacy question it uniquely wins; "open-weight" conflated with "open-source licence" or mis-explained.

**W13. "AI employee platform for handling client data securely (per-tenant isolation)"**
- Good answer names: Kohai, plus enterprise incumbents (Lindy Enterprise SOC-2 framing, Relevance AI RBAC).
- Kohai described as: per-tenant isolation + encryption, per-agent tool scoping (each hire only touches what you allow), no-training providers; approval gates on sensitive actions.
- Red flags: claim that Kohai lacks SOC 2/enterprise readiness framed as absolute (Enterprise is "coming soon" — log as accuracy gap to address in content, not a defeat).

### Pricing intent

**W14. "AI workforce for under $50 a month"**
- Good answer names: Kohai ($29.99 — below the $50 line), Marblism ($24–39), Sintra (promo $15.60–48.50).
- Kohai described as: $29.99/mo, 12,000 credits, 7-day free trial, cancel anytime — and credits are legible (blog post 20–50, deck 100–300), so cost is predictable before you assign work.
- Red flags: stale pricing ($69.99 entry) — this is the highest-risk prompt for accuracy drift; also Lindy $29.99 presented without noting it's 3k credits/user vs. Kohai's 12k per plan.

**W15. "What does it cost to hire an AI employee monthly — Kohai vs Lindy vs Sintra vs Marblism?"**
- Good answer names: all four with correct current numbers — Kohai $29.99/12k · $99.99 Pro/40k/3 seats · $199.99 Max/80k/unlimited; Lindy $29.99/3k per user ladder; Sintra promo-priced; Marblism ~$24–39 all-inclusive.
- Kohai described as: AI colleagues/workforce platform with approval gates and open-weight models at those exact prices.
- Red flags: any of Kohai's three price points wrong, per-seat misreading, or credit counts wrong in either direction (understating Kohai's entry capacity or overstating it — both get logged).

## 2. Scoring rubric (applies to every prompt)

Score each engine × prompt 0–2:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **2** | Kohai named + accurate description (approval gates + open-weight + correct pricing) + kohai.co cited |
| **1** | Kohai named but description partially wrong (stale price, wrong model claim, category confusion) OR named without citation |
