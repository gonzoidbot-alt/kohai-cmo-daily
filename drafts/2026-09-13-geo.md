## 5) GEO blockers — highest-leverage actions right now

> These three issues mean AI engines **cannot currently see or correctly describe Kohai at all**. Every other GEO effort (prompts, content, monitoring) underperforms until they're fixed. Fix list goes to Gabe — nothing changes without approval.

### 🔴 Blocker 1 — robots.txt blocks the AI crawlers GEO depends on (CRITICAL)
- **Finding:** `kohai.co/robots.txt` disallows: **GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Bytespider, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, meta-externalagent** — i.e. the crawlers behind ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity-class answers, and Meta AI are all blocked at the door.
- **Impact:** Engines can't crawl kohai.co, so they learn about Kohai only from third-party mentions (Reddit, G2, reviews) — if any exist. The "What is Kohai?" accuracy check will fail or return stale/absent info regardless of content quality. This single line set likely explains any baseline-audit failures tomorrow.
- **Recommended fix:** Remove the disallow rules for the AI-user-agent crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Google-Extended, CCBot, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, meta-externalagent, Bytespider — the last one Gabe may choose to keep blocked, trade-off is ByteDance-derived answer engines). Keep any anti-scraping rules only for bots that don't feed answer engines. Verify live after deploy with a fresh crawl check.

### 🔴 Blocker 2 — `kohai.co/llms.txt` returns 404
- **Finding:** No llms.txt — AI engines get no canonical, machine-readable product summary.
- **Recommended fix:** Publish `https://kohai.co/llms.txt` in week 1 (explicit GEO must-fix per 04-content-strategy.md). Contents: the §1 desired description verbatim, the 3-step story, named roles, verified claims list (06 § quotable-claims), pricing table, and links to the /ai-colleagues explainer + comparison pages + sitemap. Keep it short, factual, structured — it's a briefing document for engines, not marketing copy.

### 🔴 Blocker 3 — `kohai.co/ai-colleagues` returns 404
- **Finding:** The /ai-colleagues explainer page — the designated GEO anchor (04 § launch week D3) — is not live. It's the citation target for the "AI colleagues, not agents" category phrase and the link target in the hiring-trigger email templates (07 week 1).
