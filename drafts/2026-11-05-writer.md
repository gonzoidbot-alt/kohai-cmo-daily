Every solo founder knows the Monday morning ritual. You open six tabs — Stripe, Google Analytics, email, Calendly, your CRM, and that Notion doc you swore you'd update last week. You copy numbers into a spreadsheet. You chase three people for updates. Two hours later you have a half-baked status and a headache.

The problem isn't that you're disorganized. The problem is you're doing work that doesn't need a human.

## What an AI-powered Monday report looks like

Imagine this: you walk into Monday with a single document already in your Library. It contains:

- **Revenue metrics** — MRR, churn, new MRR from the last 7 days, pulled from Stripe
- **Support snapshot** — tickets opened, resolved, and still open; average response time; any escalations flagged
- **Sales pipeline** — new leads, outreach sequences running, replies received, meetings scheduled
- **Content tracker** — posts published last week, traffic to each, comments or replies needing a response
- **Top action items** — the three things that actually need *you* this week, everything else delegated

No copy-paste. No "let me check." No manual reconciliation across five logins.

## The workflow (it's four words)

"Run the Monday report."

That's the brief. Your AI Operations Lead — one of the colleagues in your Kōhai team — does the rest. It connects to your tools (Gmail, Slack, Stripe, Notion, Calendly — ~1,000 integrations, connected day one with per-agent tool scoping), queries the numbers, formats them into a clean brief, and lands the finished document in your Library.

You review it. You approve the actions that need your sign-off. Everything else gets assigned and executed.
