## Fix 1 (HIGH severity): Money-page title tags target no category keywords

Every crawled title is brand-first with zero money keywords from 04-content-strategy.md:

- Home: `Kōhai | Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.` — "AI teammates" is good, but the SERP ecosystem (listicles we want to appear in) uses **"AI employees / AI employee platforms / AI workforce platform"**, which appear nowhere in any title tag.
- Pricing: `Pricing | Kōhai` — pure-label title on the highest-commercial-intent page.
- Solutions: `Solopreneurs | Kōhai`, `Marketing & growth | Kōhai` — same pattern.

Severity HIGH because titles are the strongest on-page ranking signal and these are the pages all other channels funnel to.

**Copy-paste snippets (title + meta, home and pricing):**

```html
<meta name="description" content="Kōhai staffs your business with AI employees from day one. Brief like a colleague, finished work back today. Start free — no card. $20/mo entry." />
```


## Fix 2 (MEDIUM severity): Add FAQPage (+ Offer) structured data to /pricing

/pricing has a substantial FAQ ("What's a credit?", "Is there a free plan?", "What if a task fails…") rendered only as HTML. JSON-LD on the page is the sitewide Organization + WebSite block only — no FAQPage, no Product/Offer for the three paid plans. FAQPage markup is the cheapest path to rich results on a pricing page and doubles as clean extraction material for AI engines (GEO). Verified evidence: single `application/ld+json` block on /pricing, containing Organization/WebSite only.

**Copy-paste snippet (drop into /pricing; Q&As quote the page verbatim, all current pricing):**

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{"@context":"https://schema.org","@type":"FAQPage","mainEntity":[
 {"@type":"Question","name":"Is there a free plan?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"Yes, for a limited time. Kōhai Free is open to the first 1,000 users, needs no card and includes credits that refresh every month."}},
 {"@type":"Question","name":"What's the difference between the plans?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"Credits. Kōhai includes 8,000 credits a month for $20, Kōhai Pro 40,000 for $100 and Kōhai Max 80,000 for $200. Every plan includes unlimited team seats, the whole AI team, every role template and every integration."}},
 {"@type":"Question","name":"Do unused credits roll over?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"Monthly credits don't roll over; each cycle starts with a clean allowance. Add-on credits, the ones you buy on top, never expire."}},
 {"@type":"Question","name":"What if a task fails or I cancel it halfway?","acceptedAnswer":{"@type":"Answer","text":"If a task fails, no credits are consumed. If you cancel, only the credits used up to the cancellation point come off your balance."}}
]}
</script>
```

Optional extension: wrap the three paid plans in `Product`+`Offer` markup (price 20/100/200 USD, priceCurrency USD) for price inclusion in SERPs.
