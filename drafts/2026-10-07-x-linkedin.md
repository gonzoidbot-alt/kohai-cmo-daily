## LinkedIn post — Wednesday general cadence (colleague thesis: ticket vs brief; ICP-2 primary; claim #5; 120–180 words)

For two years, every small tool we needed went into the developer backlog.

The quote calculator: ticket #14, two sprints out. The onboarding checklist tracker: "we'll get to it." Small things, real time savings, never urgent enough to outrank the roadmap.

Then I tried a different route. I wrote one paragraph describing the calculator — what it should pull, what it should show — and briefed a colleague instead of filing a ticket. App Studio ships deployed software, not mockups. The calculator was live the same afternoon, reading our real numbers, not sitting in a design file waiting for review.

The backlog was never a capacity problem. It was a routing problem — every small job queued behind every big one. Now the small things go to a colleague, and our developer keeps the work that actually needs a developer.

Two sprints of backlog, cleared in an afternoon. What's the ticket that's been "two sprints out" since spring?
