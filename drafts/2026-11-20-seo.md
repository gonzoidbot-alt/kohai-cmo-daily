## Fix 1 — HIGH: homepage title/description target zero money keywords

**Evidence:** Live title is `Kōhai | AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates`; H1 is "Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished." Neither contains any of the strategy's money queries from 04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets: "AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees". The word "teammates" is brand vocabulary; searchers and AI engines use "AI employees" and "AI workforce" (the blog already ranks assets on those terms — the homepage, the strongest page on the domain, doesn't reinforce them). Competitor comparison: Lindy's homepage leads with category language ("AI employees/workforce") rather than brand-first framing.

**Fix (copy-paste):**

```html
<title>Kōhai | AI Employees for Small Business — AI Workforce Platform</title>
<meta name="description" content="Kōhai staffs your business with AI employees from day one — brief the work, finished deliverables land in your Inbox. Start free, no card required.">
```

(Title = 64 chars; description = 148 chars. Mirror both into `og:title/og:description` and `twitter:title/twitter:description` — they currently duplicate the live title verbatim.)

Softer variant if the brand line must survive: append the category to the existing title — `Kōhai | AI workspace, fully staffed with AI employees for small business` (71 chars, will truncate in SERPs — the clean version above is preferred).

## Fix 2 — MEDIUM-HIGH: pricing page has no Product/Offer or FAQPage structured data

**Evidence:** `curl -s kohai.co/pricing` head shows JSON-LD of only `Organization`, `WebSite`, `ImageObject` — no `Product`/`Offer`, and zero `FAQPage` markup despite a fully-rendered 6-question FAQ ("What's a credit?", "What's the difference between the plans?", annual pricing, add-on credits…). Pricing is the highest-intent page on the domain and the comparison pages all carry "dated pricing" — structured offers are what AI engines and SERPs can quote with confidence. This also pre-empts the month-2 implementation-plan item ("Pricing/alternatives page rewrite for citation — schema markup"). Caveat stated honestly: since 2023 Google restricts FAQ rich results to authoritative gov/health sites, so the FAQPage block is aimed at AI-engine citation and other engines, not a Google rich result; the Product/Offer block is the one with direct SERP upside.

**Fix (copy-paste JSON-LD — current pricing only):**

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {"@type": "Question", "name": "What's a credit?", "acceptedAnswer": {"@type": "Answer", "text": "A credit is your unit of usage — the way Kōhai abstracts away tokens, model time and tool calls. Small tasks use 5–10 credits, medium tasks (a chart, landing page, blog post) 20–50, large jobs (a deck, market research, building something) 100–300. Each plan ships a monthly allowance."}},
    {"@type": "Question", "name": "What's the difference between the plans?", "acceptedAnswer": {"@type": "Answer", "text": "Credits. Kōhai includes 8,000 credits a month, Kōhai Pro 40,000 and Kōhai Max 80,000. Every plan includes unlimited team seats, the whole AI team, every role template and every integration."}},
    {"@type": "Question", "name": "Is there a free plan?", "acceptedAnswer": {"@type": "Answer", "text": "Yes, for a limited time. Kōhai Free is open to the first 1,000 users, needs no card and includes credits that refresh every month."}}
  ]
}
```

## Keyword-gap observation — "AI chief of staff" has no page, despite Kohai owning the role

The strategy's long-tail list (04-content-strategy.md) names **"AI chief of staff"**; none of the 72 blog slugs target it, and no /vs page covers it. Meanwhile "Chief of Staff" is Kohai's *product differentiator* — it's the named role that assigns all work (Step 02 of the homepage story), which no competitor in 01-competitor-analysis.md structures around (Lindy = build agents, Artisan = one sales rep, Sintra = named helpers, Marblism = fixed cast without a coordinator story). A single post — `/blog/ai-chief-of-staff` ("What does an AI chief of staff do?") — would convert a branded product element into the exact query founders type when they're shopping for leverage, and it internally links naturally to /vs/lindy and /vs/virtual-assistant. Recommended: draft it as next week's writer-channel post rather than a rushed SEO page.
