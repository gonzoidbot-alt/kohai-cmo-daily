## Fix 2 — HIGH: /vs/ comparison pages are under-optimized for the queries they target, and /vs/lindy carries a wrong pricing claim

**Severity: High.** The `/vs/*` set is the right strategic asset (06 Stage 2: 1 comparison/week; competitor doc flags Lindy already ranking "Sintra pricing" takedown pages that siphon these searches). Three problems on the pages as crawled:

1. **Meta descriptions name neither product.** E.g. `/vs/lindy` description is "Build your own assistant vs. a ready-made team that just works." — zero query-match for "Lindy alternative", no brand token for SERP CTR, and nothing for an AI engine to lift. Same pattern on all 10 pages.
2. **No FAQPage schema** despite every page having a "…, answered" H2 section, and the pricing page having 8 visible FAQ items — all invisible to AI engines as structured Q&A.
3. **Factual error on /vs/lindy:** it states Lindy pricing as "from about $50 to $200 a month." Lindy's actual self-serve ladder is **$29.99 / $99.99 / $199.99** (per 01-competitor-analysis.md, lindy.ai/pricing). This makes Lindy look 40% more expensive than it is — if an AI engine quotes this page and a buyer checks lindy.ai, the Kohai page loses credibility. It also hides Kohai's real edge (same entry price, far more credits at mid-tier, ready-made team vs builder).

**Snippets:**

`/vs/lindy` — title + meta description:

```html
<title>Kōhai vs. Lindy (2026): Ready-made AI Colleagues vs. Build-It-Yourself Agents</title>
```
