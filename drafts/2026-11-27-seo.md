## Priority Fix 1 — SEVERE: /ai-colleagues returns 404

**Impact:** "AI colleagues" is Kohai's deliberate terminology differentiator (vs. "AI agents"/"AI employees"). A 404 on the canonical term page means:

- No landing page for the "AI colleagues" keyword cluster.

## Priority Fix 2 — HIGH: Homepage title misses money-keyword surface area

**Issue:** Current title `Kōhai | AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates` is brand-first. It doesn't surface any of the four money-page keywords the strategy defines ("AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "Lindy alternative", "Artisan alternative"). For a seed-stage site, the homepage is the strongest domain-authority page and should carry at least one high-intent keyword.

**Impact:** The homepage (highest DA page on the domain) does not rank for "AI employees for small business" or "AI workforce platform" because neither phrase appears in the title or meta.

**Suggested title rewrite:**
```
Current:     Kōhai | AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates
Suggested:  Kōhai: AI Employees for Small Business — a Full Team, $0–$200/mo
```

**Suggested meta rewrite:**
```
Current:     Kōhai is a collaborative AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates from day one. Brief the work; finished deliverables land in your Inbox.
Suggested:  Hire a team of AI employees for your small business — Strategy, Sales, Marketing, Support, Ops. Every plan has unlimited seats. Start free, no card.
```

The H1 (`Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.`) is fine and can stay — the keyword lift comes from the title tag.

**Pricing page meta** could also be tightened:
```
Current:     Pricing | Kōhai
Suggested:  Kōhai Pricing: $0 Free to $200/mo Max | AI Workforce Platform
```

## Keyword Gap Observation: "AI workforce platform" is unaddressed

**Money pages target list** (from 04-content-strategy.md):
- ✅ "AI employees for small business" — partial coverage via blog post "What is an AI employee?" (2026-05-11, updated 2026-10-15) and homepage body copy mentions "AI teammates." No dedicated landing page.
- ✅ "Lindy alternative" — well covered: /vs/lindy page + blog post "Lindy AI alternatives for small businesses (2026)" (2026-11-19). Strong.
- ✅ "Artisan alternative" — well covered: /vs/artisan page + Artisan comparison on /vs hub.
- ❌ **"AI workforce platform"** — **not covered anywhere.** No page title, H1, or meta description contains this phrase. The nearest match is "AI workspace" (homepage). This is a distinct gap because:
  - Competitor terms: Relevance AI positions as "AI workforce," and the search volume for "AI workforce platform" is significant.
  - Kohai's product (multi-role team, Chief of Staff routing, unlimited seats) is literally an AI workforce platform.
  - Lindy's blog does not target this phrase either — an opportunity to claim it.

**Recommended action:** Either (a) add a dedicated landing page `/ai-workforce-platform` with title `AI Workforce Platform for Small Business | Kōhai` or (b) rewrite the "What is an AI employee?" blog post to include "AI workforce platform" in the title and H1, converting it from a blog post to a product explainer page.

## Competitive Context Note

Lindy (`lindy.ai`) publishes aggressive head-to-head pricing takedowns (e.g., "Sintra AI Pricing 2026" — verified live, last updated 2026-10-30). These are structured as buyer-guide blog posts and rank for `[competitor] pricing` queries. Kohai's /vs/ pages are comparison pages (good for consideration-stage traffic), but they lack the SEO pattern of a pricing-takedown title that captures `"Kohai pricing vs [competitor]"` search traffic. A blog post titled "Lindy Pricing 2026: Plans, Credits, and How Kohai Compares" would mirror Lindy's pattern on Kohai's own turf.
