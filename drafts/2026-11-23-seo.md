## Fix 1 (HIGH): Homepage title + meta description target only coined vocabulary — no money keyword, no ICP qualifier

**What's live:**
- `<title>Kōhai | AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates</title>`
- meta description: "Kōhai is a collaborative AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates from day one. Brief the work; finished deliverables land in your Inbox."
- H1: "Onboard AI teammates."

**Why it matters:** The title is the strongest on-page ranking signal, and it spends it entirely on "AI workspace" + "AI teammates" — vocabulary Kohai coined (the site even has an explainer post defining "AI workspace"). That's fine for category defense but it cedes the demand-side head terms from 04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets: "AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees". None appear in the title, description, or H1. The description also never names the buyer (small business / solo founder), so the SERP snippet doesn't pre-qualify ICP-1. Competitors aren't sophisticated here either — Lindy/Artisan lean on "AI employee(s)" directly, so this is a winnable head term, not a contested one.

**Copy-paste replacement (title ≤60 chars, meta ≤155):**

```html
<title>Kōhai: AI Employees for Small Business | AI Workforce Platform</title>
<meta name="description" content="Hire AI employees that finish real work — sales, content, support, ops — in the tools you already use. Built for small business. Start free, no card needed.">
```

(Keep the H1 — "Onboard AI teammates." — as-is; it's on-brand and the title now carries the keywords. Mirror the new title/description into og:title, og:description, twitter:title, twitter:description, which currently duplicate the old wording verbatim.)

## Fix 2 (HIGH): Pricing page has zero structured data beyond Organization/WebSite — no FAQPage, no Product/Offer

**What's live:** /pricing ships only the site-wide Organization + WebSite JSON-LD. The page has 9 FAQ items (credit costs, rollover, add-ons, proration, Free plan terms) and 4 tiers with prices — none marked up. `<title>` is the bare "Pricing | Kōhai".

**Why it matters:** (a) FAQPage schema is the highest-yield schema for exactly this page: Google can lift individual Q&As into the SERP, and it's the format AI engines quote most reliably — the credit-cost answer ("small tasks 5–10, medium 20–50, large 100–300 credits") is a ready-made quotable. (b) Product/Offer schema puts $20/$100/$200 into the knowledge graph, which supports the strategy's plan to make pricing/alternatives pages citation-ready (07-gtm-implementation-plan.md, Month 2). (c) Lindy's SEO play is pricing-takedown pages; our own pricing page should be maximally machine-readable when those comparisons land on kohai.co.

**Copy-paste snippet (FAQPage — attach the Product/Offer block beside it with offers at 0/20/100/200 USD per month):**

```json
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "FAQPage",
  "mainEntity": [
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "How many credits does a task use?",
      "acceptedAnswer": { "@type": "Answer", "text": "Small tasks (a quick research query, a short email) use 5–10 credits. Medium tasks (a chart, a landing page, a blog post) use 20–50. Large jobs (a deck, market research, building something) use 100–300. Failed tasks consume no credits." }
    },
    {
      "@type": "Question",
      "name": "Is Kōhai Free really free?",
      "acceptedAnswer": { "@type": "Answer", "text": "Yes, for a limited time. Kōhai Free is open to the first 1,000 users, needs no card and includes credits that refresh every month." }
    }
  ]
}
```

**Also while editing /pricing:** upgrade the title from `Pricing | Kōhai` to `Kōhai Pricing: AI Team Plans from $20/mo — Free to Start` (the current title has zero keyword payload for "AI employees pricing"-type queries).

## Keyword-gap observation (1)

**"AI workforce" / "hire AI employees" have zero page coverage.** The sitemap (185 URLs) is strong on long-tail — industry pages (law firms, construction, logistics), /use-cases/, /guides/, and a full /vs/ set covering all five tracked competitors (lindy, artisan, sintra, marblism, relevance-ai) plus /vs/virtual-assistant. But no page targets the two money phrases the strategy itself names: "AI workforce platform" and "hire AI employees" — no slug contains "workforce" or "hire", and the closest pages (blog/ai-for-small-business, blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business, solutions/smb) are adjacent, not exact. The site's entire head-term bet is the self-coined "AI workspace", which has near-zero existing search demand. **Recommendation:** make the homepage title/metadata change in Fix 1 the first step, then back it with one dedicated page (e.g. /ai-workforce-platform) structured per the Stage 2 playbook (H2-as-question, answer in first 2 sentences, comparison table) so the head term has a landable, citable URL.
