## Fix #1 (Severity: HIGH — this is the GEO must-fix from 04-content-strategy.md, currently inverted)

**Problem: Cloudflare's robots.txt content-signals block the exact crawlers that decide how AI engines describe Kohai.**

Live robots.txt (verified 2026-09-10) disallows `GPTBot`, `ClaudeBot`, `Google-Extended`, `CCBot`, `Amazonbot`, `Applebot-Extended`, `Bytespider`, `meta-externalagent`, and sets `Content-Signal: search=yes,ai-train=no,use=reference`. This is almost certainly the Cloudflare-managed default ("block AI training"), not a deliberate Kohai decision. Consequence:

- ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini grounding cannot crawl kohai.co → the daily GEO tracking in 04-content-strategy.md (10–15 prompts) will keep returning competitor descriptions, because Kohai gives the engines nothing to read.
- The 04-content-strategy.md GEO goal — "ensure ChatGPT/Perplexity describe Kohai as 'AI colleagues/workforce platform with approval gates and open-weight models'" — cannot be achieved while these blocks stand. This is the single highest-severity item on the site.

## Keyword-gap observation (from 04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets)

**The /vs/* comparison pages exist for every money keyword — but their titles abandon the money phrase.** 04-content-strategy.md names "Lindy alternative" as a money-page target, and 01-competitor-analysis.md flags that Lindy is already publishing competitor-takedown pages ("Sintra AI Pricing") to siphon exactly these searches. Kohai's /vs/lindy page (verified live, indexed in sitemap) is the perfect landing asset — but its title is `Kōhai vs. Lindy — which should you choose?` and the word "alternative" appears exactly once on the whole page. Nobody searching "Lindy alternative" matches that title. Same pattern across the set: /solutions/smb targets "AI employees for small business" but is titled `Small businesses — Kōhai` (no "AI employees" in title or description); nothing on the site targets "AI workforce platform" in any title.

Suggested copy-paste fixes (titles stay in Kohai's calm register — no hype):

| Page | Current title | Proposed title | Proposed meta description |
|---|---|---|---|
| /vs/lindy | Kōhai vs. Lindy — which should you choose? | Lindy alternative: Kōhai vs. Lindy — colleagues, not agents | Build your own assistant vs. a ready-made team that just works. Kohai hires you a team of AI colleagues in your existing tools — from $29.99/month. |
| /solutions/smb | Small businesses — Kōhai | AI employees for small business — Kōhai | More output without more headcount. Hire AI employees who work in the ~1,000 tools you already use. 7-day free trial. |

**Why this is the gap worth closing first:** the demand exists, the pages exist, the content exists — only the title/meta rewrite is missing. It is a same-week, zero-new-content win on the exact queries 04-content-strategy.md names as money pages, against a competitor (Lindy) already actively targeting them.
