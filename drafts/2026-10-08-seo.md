## Fix 1 — P0: Ship llms.txt + the /ai-colleagues explainer (GEO must-fixes are still undone)

**Severity: highest.** 51% of B2B buyers now start research with an AI chatbot (G2, per 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md), and Kohai's ICP is AI-native — assume 60%+. AI crawlers issue plain GETs and cannot execute the site's JS-heavy interactive sections; llms.txt + a plain-text explainer is the cheapest possible way to make sure ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini describe Kohai correctly (the 04-content-strategy § GEO must-fix: "AI colleagues/workforce platform with approval gates and open-weight models"). This was scheduled for launch week and is still missing. The `https://***` corruption visible in the raw JSON-LD (`"@context":"https://***@type"` — the `schema.org` host appears to be getting mangled, possibly by a build sanitizer) should also be checked in the deployed HTML; if the schema.org context is genuinely malformed in production, AI parsers will silently drop the one schema block that does exist.

## Keyword-gap observation: kohai.co has literally zero instances of "AI employee" — it is competing with Lindy for "AI teammate," a term with almost no search demand, while ceding every money keyword in 04-content-strategy.md

The crawl found **0 occurrences of "AI employee(s)"** across homepage, /pricing, /vs/lindy, /solutions/smb, and the blog post whose own slug is `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` (its H1/title say "AI teammate"). "AI teammate" appears 19× on the homepage alone. Meanwhile:

- **"AI teammates" is now Lindy's own homepage title** ("Lindy – The AI Teammate That Lets You Do More" — verified today). Kohai picked the same category word as its biggest, best-funded competitor at the exact moment that competitor adopted it, and gave up the established demand term.
- Every money keyword in 04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets uses "employee/employees/workforce": "AI employees for small business", "AI workforce platform", "hire AI employees". Competitor titles confirm the demand side: Sintra ranks on "Hire Your First AI Employees Team", Marblism on "AI Employees To Scale Your Business".
- **"Lindy alternative"**: /vs/lindy exists (good) but its title is "Kōhai vs. Lindy: which should you choose?" and its meta description never says "alternative" — the phrase searchers actually type (and the phrase in the monthly GEO audit prompt list).
- **"AI chief of staff"**: Mochi/Chief of Staff is mentioned 10× on the homepage but there is no page targeting the long-tail keyword ("AI chief of staff" is in the 04 long-tail list and audit prompt #6).

**Recommendation (draft, needs Gabe's positioning call):** keep "AI teammates/colleagues" as the brand voice in body copy, but put the demand term in the crawlabile real estate — title tags, meta descriptions, H1s of money pages. Concretely:

- `/solutions/smb` title: `AI Employees for Small Business | Kōhai` (currently "Small businesses | Kōhai")
- Homepage title: `Kōhai — Hire AI Employees & Teammates. Get Work Finished.` (currently "Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.")
- `/vs/lindy` title: `Lindy Alternative: Kōhai vs. Lindy — Ready-Made AI Employees vs. Build-It-Yourself` (keeps the comparison, adds the "Lindy alternative" money phrase)
- New page: `/solutions/ai-chief-of-staff` — "AI Chief of Staff: what Mochi does" mapped to the 5-step meeting flow already on the homepage.
- The /vs/ hub should interlink all 11 comparison pages from the homepage footer — currently /vs is orphaned from the main nav path (it exists only in the sitemap and the vs index), which caps how much equity the highest-intent pages receive.

**Evidence caveat:** no rank-tracking or Search Console data was available for this audit; the demand signals are inferred from competitor title targets and the strategy docs' keyword list. Verify volume assumptions in GSC/Keyword Planner before committing the roadmap order.
