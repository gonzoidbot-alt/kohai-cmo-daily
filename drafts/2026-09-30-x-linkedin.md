## LinkedIn post — daily cadence, founder voice (ICP-1 anchor; claim #1, new "what the reclaimed hours bought" frame)

A founder walked me through her week before her first AI hire: sales and outreach 11 hours, content and SEO 8, ops and reporting 7, support triage 5. All of it before the work she actually started the business to do.

She hired an Operations Lead for the reporting and support triage, and a Sales & Growth Lead for the outreach, on Kohai. One message each — the whole job description. Anything touching a client invoice waits at an approval gate she clears once a day.

Kohai's example week: 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed.

Her 9am block is client work now, not software management. The reclaimed hours went back into the business — not into babysitting tools.

What would you ship with 31 hours back?
