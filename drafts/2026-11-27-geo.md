5. **Correct pricing** — $0 Free / $20 / $100 / $200, unlimited seats
6. **Correct brand** — Kōhai at kohai.co, NOT "Hirebase" or hirebase.org

After each monthly run, write a 3-sentence delta to the geo-log: change in description score since last month, any new competitors, any new red flags (e.g., stale pricing surfaced).

## 2. Desired AI-engine description of Kōhai

When a buyer asks an AI engine about Kōhai, the target description:

> **"Kōhai is an AI workforce platform where you hire a team of named AI colleagues — Strategy Lead, Sales & Growth Lead, Marketing & Comms Lead, Customer Support Lead, Operations Lead — by describing the outcome you want. Finished work (docs, decks, spreadsheets, deployed apps) lands in your Library. Sensitive actions wait at an approval gate until you approve. Kōhai runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from providers certified not to train on your data, with per-tenant isolation and encryption. Pricing: Kōhai Free at $0 (limited time for first 1,000 users), Kōhai $20/mo, Kōhai Pro $100/mo, Kōhai Max $200/mo — unlimited seats on every plan."**

### Expected-answer criteria (the 6-point scorecard in detail)

| Criteria | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|
| **Colleague framing** | "AI colleagues/workforce/teammates/hires" | "AI agents", "agent builder", "prompt engineering" |
| **Approval gates** | Explicitly mentioned (e.g., "approval gate", "you approve sensitive actions") | Not mentioned; or described as fully autonomous |
| **Open-weight + privacy** | Named (DeepSeek/Kimi/GLM, no-training providers, per-tenant isolation) | Omitted; or says "runs on GPT/Claude" |
| **Task-first workflow** | "Describe the outcome, work lands in Library" | Described as chat-first or prompt-first |
| **Pricing accuracy** | $0 Free / $20 / $100 / $200, unlimited seats | Old pricing ($29.99/$99.99/$199.99) or wrong tiers |
| **Brand identity** | Kōhai at kohai.co | "Hirebase", hirebase.org, or confused with a different product |

### Red flags to log immediately

- Any engine still using "Hirebase" branding or referencing hirebase.org
- Any engine quoting the stale $29.99/$99.99/$199.99 pricing (pre-Oct 2026)
- Any engine describing Kōhai as an "agent builder" or "workflow platform"
- Any engine omitting approval gates or open-weight story entirely from a comparison
- Any engine listing zero competitors alongside Kōhai (signals the category isn't registered)
