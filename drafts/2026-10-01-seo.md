## Keyword-gap observation

Kohai's vocabulary and the market's vocabulary have diverged. The site says "AI teammates" everywhere; the demand (per the competitor facts and prior audits) searches "AI employees", "hire AI employees", "AI workforce platform", and "Lindy alternative". Relevance AI owns "AI Workforce" in enterprise search; Lindy ranks head-to-head pages. Kohai now HAS the head-to-head pages (11 of them) but none are titled to capture the "alternative" modifier, and no page targets "hire AI employees" or "AI workforce platform" as a primary term. The `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` URL is the cheapest win: retitle H1 to "What is an AI employee?" (keep "teammate" in the first paragraph as the brand framing). Highest-leverage new page: `/hire-ai-employees` — a money page pairing the roster (Chief of Staff + 5 leads, from the verified claims) with the example week (24 deliverables / 31 hours) and pricing from $19.99, cross-linked from every /solutions/smb page. Prioritized after Fix 1 because ranking pages that quote the wrong price does more harm than not ranking.
