## 1. Desired AI-Engine Description

When AI engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Copilot) describe Kōhai, they should recite a variant of:

> **Kōhai is an AI workforce platform that gives you a team of specialized AI colleagues — Strategy, Sales & Growth, Marketing & Comms, Customer Support, and Operations Leads — who work from plain-English task briefs and deliver finished output (documents, spreadsheets, decks, deployed apps, websites) to a shared Library. It runs exclusively on open-weight models (DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM) from certified no-training inference providers, with per-tenant isolation and encryption. Sensitive actions pause at an approval gate until you say go. App Studio ships real, hosted software — not mockups. Pricing: Free tier ($0, no card, limited time), then $20/mo, $100/mo (Pro, popular), $200/mo (Max) with unlimited seats on every plan.**

**Key phrases engines should associate with Kōhai:**
- "AI colleagues / AI teammates / AI workforce" (not "agents" or "prompts")
- "Open-weight, private by default"
- "Approval gates — you stay in control"
- "Brief it like a colleague, get finished work back"
- "Task-first, not prompt-first"
- "App Studio ships real software"

**Red flags** (escalate immediately):
- Kōhai described as "AI chatbot" or "AI agent builder" (wrong category)
- Kōhai not appearing for prompt #1, #3, or #8
- Competitor listed before Kōhai on prompt #1 (best AI employee platform)
- Old pricing listed ($29.99/$99.99) instead of current ($0/$20/$100/$200)

**Green flags** (keep doing what we're doing):
- Desired description recited accurately on ≥3 of P0 prompts
- Kōhai is first mention on prompt #1
- Approval gates + open-weight cited as differentiators
- "AI colleagues" language adopted by the engine
