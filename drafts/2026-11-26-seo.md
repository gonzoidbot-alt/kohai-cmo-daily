## Fix 1 (Severity: HIGH) — Homepage title + meta description target none of the five money phrases

**Evidence (live today):**
- `<title>Kōhai | AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates</title>` (54 chars)
- `<meta name="description" content="Kōhai is a collaborative AI workspace, fully staffed with AI teammates from day one. Brief the work; finished deliverables land in your Inbox.">` (177 chars — also runs long; Google truncates ~155–160)
- H1: `Onboard AI teammates. Get work finished.`

"AI workspace" and "AI teammates" are brand vocabulary, not search vocabulary. Zero occurrences of "AI employees," "AI workforce," or "small business" on the page Google weights most. The homepage is the site's strongest asset and it competes for none of the money terms in 04-content-strategy.md § SEO/GEO targets.

**Copy-paste replacement (head):**

```html
<title>Kōhai | Hire AI employees — the AI workforce for small business</title>
<meta name="description" content="Hire AI employees that work like colleagues. Brief the outcome and your AI workforce finishes it in your tools; anything sensitive waits for your approval. Start free, no card.">
<meta property="og:title" content="Kōhai | Hire AI employees — the AI workforce for small business">
<meta property="og:description" content="Hire AI employees that work like colleagues. Brief the outcome and your AI workforce finishes it in your tools. Start free, no card.">
```

Title is 68 chars — trim to taste; `Kōhai | AI employees for small business` (40) is the tighter option if Gabe prefers restraint. Keep "AI teammates" in the H1 and body copy — the differentiation stays; this only fixes what crawlers and SERPs read.

**Cannibalization check:** no other indexed page title contains "AI employees" or "AI workforce" (verified against all 4 sitemaps), so retitling the homepage creates no internal collision.

## Fix 2 (Severity: HIGH) — /solutions/smb is the natural "AI employees for small business" money page but its metadata never says it

**Evidence (live today):**
- `<title>Small businesses | Kōhai</title>`
- `<meta name="description" content="Pipeline that stays warm and books closed on time. Tell Kōhai the growth goal, and your team focuses on strategy while your workspace handles the follow-ups, tickets and vendor admin.">`
- H1: `Scale output without scaling headcount`

The query with the clearest commercial intent for ICP-1 lands on a page whose title is two words with no keyword, and whose description never mentions AI employees, small business, or hiring. Blog posts (`/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee`, `/blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business`, `/blog/how-ai-employees-help-every-company-size`) carry the vocabulary but funnel to a solution page that drops it.

**Copy-paste replacement (head):**

```html
<title>AI employees for small business | Kōhai</title>
<meta name="description" content="Hire AI employees for your small business — sales, marketing, support and ops teammates in one shared workspace, from $0 to start. No card needed.">
```

Plus two on-page edits (not copy-pasteable without the CMS):
1. Add "AI employees for your small business" to the first hero paragraph — one sentence, brand voice intact ("Scale output without scaling headcount" can stay as the H1 or become "AI employees for your small business" — Gabe's call; the `<title>` fix alone captures most of the value).
2. Cross-link: `/blog/what-is-an-ai-employee` and `/blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business` should link to `/solutions/smb` with anchor "AI employees for small business" (they currently funnel blog readers to a page titled "Small businesses," which dilutes the signal).

**Same pattern, lower priority (log for later, don't fix today):** `/features` ("Features | Kōhai") and `/pricing` ("Pricing | Kōhai") are equally generic; and the pricing meta lists only the paid plans — the $0 Free plan (limited time, first 1,000 users) is the strongest conversion asset on the page and isn't in the title or description.

## Keyword-gap observation

**Three of the five money keywords have no dedicated target page; two are covered well.**

| Money keyword | Status today |
|---|---|
| Lindy alternative | ✅ Covered twice: `/vs/lindy` + `/blog/lindy-alternatives` ("The best Lindy AI alternatives for small businesses (2026)") |
| Artisan alternative | ✅ Covered: `/vs/artisan` |
| AI employees for small business | ❌ No page targets it — fix 2 above repositions `/solutions/smb` |
| AI workforce platform | ❌ Zero occurrences anywhere in titles/descriptions (all 4 sitemaps checked) |
| Hire AI employees | ❌ Only in blog body copy; no title/meta/H1 targets it |

**Why this matters competitively:** the site's entire vocabulary is "AI teammates / AI workspace," which is strong differentiation (deliberately not "agents") but means Kōhai doesn't appear for the phrases buyers actually type. Two notes from 01-competitor-analysis.md: Relevance AI literally brands as the "AI Workforce" platform (LinkedIn-heavy GTM around that exact phrase), so that SERP has an incumbent — worth a differentiated angle (team-of-teammates vs. agent-builder) rather than a head-on title match. And Lindy publishes head-to-head pricing takedowns on its own blog, so the /vs/lindy page's pricing section should stay dated and current (its meta description — "One AI employee in Slack, priced per person, vs. a whole team on one plan" — is good copy but contains no keyword; a follow-up tweak could append "A Lindy alternative for teams that don't build workflows" if Gabe wants it).

**Recommended sequence:** Fix 1 + Fix 2 (same deploy), then one new explainer page for "AI workforce platform" (the `/ai-colleagues` GEO anchor already planned in 04-content-strategy.md could absorb this — it currently 404s).
