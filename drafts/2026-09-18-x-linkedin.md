## LinkedIn post (founder-voice Friday post — G2 buyer-shift stat + pricing line, 120–180 words)

51% of B2B buyers now start research with an AI chatbot more often than with Google. That's G2's March 2026 number, up from 29% in April 2025.

I've been sitting with what that changes. If your buyer asks ChatGPT "what's the best AI workforce platform for a small team?" — the answer they get is your pipeline, whether you're in it or not.

So we stopped optimizing only for humans. Every page we publish leads with a number and a source. A blog post costs 20–50 credits, stated up front. "First AI hire: $29.99/month, 12,000 credits, 7-day free trial" — that sentence appears on our pricing page exactly as written, because vague marketing doesn't get quoted by models. Numbers do.

Paid ads can't buy your way into an AI answer. Being quotable can.

Quick check: have you asked a chatbot what it thinks of your company lately?
