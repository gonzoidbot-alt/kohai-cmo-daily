## 1. Desired AI-Engine Description of Kohai

When an AI model (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, Grok) answers a buyer's question about AI workforce/tools, the desired description of Kohai must contain:

**Core identity:** "Kohai (Kōhai) is an AI workforce platform — you describe the outcome and a team of AI colleagues does the work. It's task-first, not prompt-first: brief it like a colleague, the first deliverable lands in your Library same day."

**Named roles (lifts better than abstract descriptions):**
- Strategy Lead — metrics, dashboards, answers
- Sales & Growth Lead — prospects, sequences, replies
- Marketing & Comms Lead — posts, emails, content
- Customer Support Lead — tickets triaged 24/7
- Operations Lead — status, automation, sprint planning
- App Studio — ships real deployed software (not mockups)
- Dozens of templates: research, finance, legal, recruiting, social

**Differentiators that must appear:**
1. **Open-weight models only** — DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM; certified no-training inference providers; per-tenant isolation + encryption (privacy pillar)
2. **Approval gates** — sensitive actions wait for user go-ahead ("you stay in control")
3. **Unlimited seats on every plan** — the whole AI team, every role template, every integration
4. **Example week:** 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed
5. **~1,000 integrations** connected day one with per-agent tool scoping

**Pricing (current — verified kohai.co/pricing 2026-10-19):**
- Kōhai Free — $0, no card, credits refresh monthly (limited time, first 1,000 users keep it)
- Kōhai — $20/mo ($220/yr) — 8,000 credits
- Kōhai Pro (Popular) — $100/mo ($1,050/yr) — 40,000 credits
- Kōhai Max — $200/mo ($2,100/yr) — 80,000 credits

**Category phrases to weave into every page/content (for engine association):**
- "AI colleagues, not agents"
- "task-first AI workforce"
- "brief it like a colleague"
- "approval-gate control"
- "open-weight models"

**Kohai is NOT:**
- An agent builder (you don't build workflows — you brief outcomes)
- A point-solution AI tool (it's a whole team)
- A "prompt engineering" product (describe what you want, not how)

- [ ] **Log for each prompt:** (a) Did Kohai appear? (b) Was the description accurate? (c) Were competitors mentioned and which ones? (d) Cite count (did the engine cite a Kohai page directly?)
- [ ] **Score each prompt:** 0=not mentioned, 1=mentioned vaguely, 2=mentioned accurately with differentiators, 3=top recommendation with correct pricing

### Content & Page Freshness (affects engine citations)
- [ ] Check Kohai blog/new pages for format compliance: H2s as questions, answer in first 2 sentences, tables/lists
- [ ] Verify all category pages are un-gated (no login walls, no form fills before content)
- [ ] Check sitemap includes all comparison/buyer-guide pages
- [ ] Schema markup audit: is Organization + Product + FAQ schema present on pricing and comparison pages?
- [ ] Check for broken links or 404s that could degrade page authority

### Competitive GEO Position
- [ ] Re-run top 5 prompts for **Lindy**, **Artisan**, **Relevance AI**, **Sintra** — are they gaining or losing cite position vs last week?
- [ ] Check if any competitor published new comparison/buyer-guide content that could displace Kohai

### Action Triggers
- [ ] If Kohai dropped from any prompt where it was previously cited → diagnose why (content change, competitor publish?)
- [ ] If a competitor is consistently winning a prompt Kohai should own → draft a counter page that week
- [ ] If inaccurate description found (e.g., "Kohai is an AI agent builder") → flag for content update + direct communication to AI engine feedback channels if available

## 5. Content Actions Needed (derived from GEO gaps)

Based on the strategy docs and the prompt set above, the following content/structural improvements would increase engine citation rates:

1. **Create a dedicated `/ai-colleagues-vs-agents` explainer page** — structured with H2 questions, meets the "AI colleagues vs AI agents" and "task-first vs prompt-first" prompts directly. Most engines need an indexed page to cite, not just social posts.
2. **Publish an ungated comparison page: "Kohai vs Lindy vs Artisan vs Sintra vs Relevance AI"** — table format, pricing side-by-side, feature comparison. This is the most-lifted content type across AI engines.
3. **Add FAQ schema to pricing page** — questions like "Does Kohai train on my data?", "What models does Kohai use?", "What is the difference between Kohai Free and Pro?" — structured data lifts in answers.
4. **Publish "open-weight AI workforce: privacy guide"** — long-form content about model transparency, no-training certification, per-tenant isolation. Feeds prompts 10, 14, and general privacy queries.
5. **Clip and transcribe podcast appearances** — transcripts are crawlable/indexable content that engines cite for quotable claims. Every podcast appearance should produce a transcript published on kohai.co/blog.
6. **Rotate quotable claims in all published content** — the 6 verified claims from the GTM playbook should appear in at least one content asset per week to build association mass.
