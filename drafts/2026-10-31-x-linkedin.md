## LinkedIn post — Saturday general cadence (the mockup test; ICP-1 primary, ICP-2 readable; claim #5; 120–180 words)

A founder told me he asked an AI tool for an internal dashboard and got a nice picture of one.

That's the difference between a mockup and software. A mockup shows you what it could look like. Software runs — deployed, hosted, reachable by your team on Monday morning.

App Studio is the part of Kōhai that closes that gap. You describe the app, landing page, or internal tool you need. "App Studio ships deployed software, not mockups." It's the same bar we hold the rest of the team to: your Sales & Growth Lead doesn't hand you a draft outreach screenshot — the sequence lands ready to run, and sensitive actions wait at an approval gate until you say go.

The test is simple. When the work is done, is it in your Library and live — or is it a picture of done?

What's the last thing you asked AI for that came back as a mockup of the thing you actually needed?

Ends with a question (sanctioned ending per 03-design-guide). ~140 words. 0 hashtags (≤3 limit met).
