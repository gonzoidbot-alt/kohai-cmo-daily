## LinkedIn post (founder-voice general awareness, 120–180 words, numbers-led)

I keep hearing the same sentence from solo founders: "I pay for seven AI tools and I still do everything myself."

The tools aren't the problem. You are the manager of all of them — building workflows, writing prompts, checking outputs. That's a second job.

The alternative is to stop building and start hiring. Brief an AI colleague the way you'd brief a capable new hire: here's the outcome, here's where the data lives, here's what never leaves the building. One message. No workflow diagrams.

Kohai's example week: 24 deliverables shipped, 31 hours reclaimed. And the entry point is deliberately low — first AI hire: $29.99/month, 12,000 credits, 7-day free trial.

You stay in control. Sensitive actions wait at an approval gate until you say go.

If you run a small team: what's the one recurring task you'd hand over first?
