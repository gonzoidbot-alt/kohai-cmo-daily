## LinkedIn Post — integration + scoping workflow story

Your week starts Monday. Kohai's starts Friday.

Friday, you brief Mochi, your Chief of Staff: growth report, support triage, follow-ups on every unanswered lead.

Monday morning, she pulls numbers from Stripe, context from Notion, and open threads from Gmail — ~1,000 integrations connected day one, with per-agent tool scoping. The finished report lands in your Library before your first coffee.

Each colleague touches only the tools its role needs. Your Support Lead sees the help desk, not your books. Anything sensitive waits at an approval gate for your OK.

No flowcharts. No builder canvas. One brief, finished work.

If you could hand one recurring task to an AI colleague this week — the Monday report, the invoice chase, or support triage — which would it be?
