## LinkedIn post (founder-voice workflow story, numbers-led, 120–180 words)

Last month a 9-person agency asked me how to cover two gaps: their ops lead was drowning in client reporting, and they needed a small internal tool to triage inbound requests. No budget for two hires.

We did it one way instead. They briefed an Operations Lead the way they'd brief a new hire — where the data lives, what the Monday report needs, what never leaves the building. First deliverable landed the same day. Sensitive client actions wait at an approval gate; they approve, it ships.

The internal tool took an afternoon in App Studio. App Studio ships deployed software, not mockups — it went live for the team that afternoon, not onto a whiteboard.

Total cost: Pro tier, $99.99/mo for 40,000 credits and 3 seats. Less than one day of contractor time.

If you lead a lean team: which function would you hand a colleague first — ops, sales, or support?
