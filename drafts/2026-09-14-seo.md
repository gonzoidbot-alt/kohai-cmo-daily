## (a) Prioritized on-page/technical fixes

### Fix 1 — CRITICAL: Cloudflare-managed robots.txt is blocking the AI crawlers the GEO strategy depends on

**Severity: Critical — this single file contradicts the entire GEO pillar.**

The live `https://kohai.co/robots.txt` contains Cloudflare's managed bot-blocking block:

```
User-agent: GPTBot        Disallow: /
User-agent: ClaudeBot     Disallow: /
User-agent: CCBot         Disallow: /
User-agent: Google-Extended  Disallow: /
User-agent: Amazonbot / Applebot-Extended / Bytespider / meta-externalagent  Disallow: /
```

Per 06-gtm-funnel-playbook.md: 51% of B2B buyers start research with an AI chatbot, and Kohai's ICP skews even more AI-native. With these disallows, ChatGPT/Claude/Perplexity cannot crawl kohai.co at all — the planned daily GEO prompt tracking (04 § daily GEO) and the "What is Kohai?" accuracy check (06 § audit prompt 8) will fail no matter how good the content is. It also blocks ai-input-style retrieval. This is almost certainly a Cloudflare dashboard default ("Block AI bots"), not a deliberate choice — but note the tension honestly: the `Content-Signal: ai-train=no` line already handles the training concern while still allowing retrieval. Blocking the bots entirely forfeits the "get quoted by models" strategy documented in 04 § SEO/GEO targets and 06 § Stage 1.

**Fix:** In Cloudflare → (per-zone) AI Crawl Control / Bots, set GPTBot, ClaudeBot, CCBot, Google-Extended, Amazonbot, Applebot-Extended, meta-externalagent to *Allow*. Then verify robots.txt reads:

```
User-agent: *
Allow: /

Sitemap: https://kohai.co/sitemap-index.xml
```

Keep `Content-Signal: search=yes, ai-train=no, use=reference` if desired — it permits AI retrieval/summarization while reserving training rights, which matches the product's own privacy stance ("certified not to train on your data" — we ask the same of crawlers, symmetric and defensible). **Do not keep `use=reference` if it's causing fetchers to see blocked/limited content; `Allow` for all AI agents is the Stage-1 requirement.**

**Verification step after change:** `curl https://kohai.co/robots.txt` should show no `Disallow: /` for GPTBot/ClaudeBot/CCBot; then re-run audit prompt 8 ("What is Kohai?") and log whether engines can now retrieve the site.

## (b) Keyword-gap observation vs competitors

**Kohai covers the "X vs. Kohai" comparison set but not the "alternative" intent — and the highest-value SMB money keyword has no page targeting it.**

What's live today (verified from the sitemap and pages): `/vs/lindy`, `/vs/artisan`, `/vs/sintra`, `/vs/marblism`, `/vs/relevance-ai` all exist — genuinely good coverage, ahead of where 01-competitor-analysis.md said the site was on 2026-09-08. But three gaps remain against the money-keyword list in 04 § SEO/GEO targets:

1. **"Lindy alternative" / "Artisan alternative" — framing mismatch.** All comparison pages are titled "Kōhai vs. X." Buyers typing "Lindy alternative" or "Artisan alternative" are a *different intent* (actively dissatisfied, searching for a switch) than "Kohai vs. Lindy" (neutral evaluation). Lindy already publishes exactly this kind of head-to-head content — its blog ranks dedicated competitor-pricing pages (e.g. its Sintra pricing page, noted in 01 § Lindy marketing channels) — so this is a proven-playbook gap, not a speculative one. Lowest-cost move: publish `/lindy-alternative` and `/artisan-alternative` as distinct pages (or, minimally, rework the vs-pages' title tags to carry both phrasings, e.g. "Kohai vs. Lindy — an AI-colleagues alternative to building agents"). Keep the comparison factual and generous to the competitor — lead with where each genuinely excels, let pricing/privacy/approval-gates be the honest differentiators (per design-guide voice rules).

2. **"AI employees for small business" — no page owns it.** `/solutions/smb` targets small businesses but is titled "Small businesses — Kōhai" with H1 "Scale output without scaling headcount" — it never claims the keyword itself. The blog has `/blog/best-ai-agents-for-small-business`, which (a) uses "AI agents" — the exact term the brand position says *not* to use for Kohai — and (b) is blog-intent, not money-intent. One dedicated page (H1: "AI employees for small business") mapping the three pillars to SMB pains (pipeline, content, ops, support) would own the phrase on-site; today nothing does.

3. **"AI workforce platform" and "hire AI employees" — homepage language only.** The closing CTA says "Your instant AI workforce is ready when you are," which is good in-copy usage, but no standalone page targets either query. These can be served by the planned `/ai-colleagues` explainer (04 § launch week D3) — worth checking that its eventual title tag carries "AI workforce platform" explicitly, since that page is currently 404 and is the designated GEO anchor.

**Priority order if only one gets built this week:** `/lindy-alternative` first — Lindy is documented (01 § vulnerability #1) as the near-price-twin with the most brand gravity, so it's where competitive search traffic already concentrates, and the comparison-guide cadence (06 § week 1, Thursday: "Kohai vs Lindy: hiring colleagues vs building agents") can ship it ungated as planned.
